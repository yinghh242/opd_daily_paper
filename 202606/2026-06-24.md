### 1. V-Zero: Answer-Label-Free On-Policy Distillation with Contrastive Evidence Gating for Fine-Grained Visual Reasoning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25319
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25319
- Authors: Haoxiang Sun, Zhihang Yi, Langxuan Deng, Yuhao Zhou, Peiqi Jia, Jian Zhao, Li Yuan, Jiancheng Lv, Tao Wang
- 作者单位：四川大学；西安交通大学；中国电信人工智能研究院 TeleAI；北京大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-06-24
- arXiv updated: 2026-06-24
- Categories: cs.CV

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Fine-grained visual reasoning requires multimodal large language models (MLLMs) to identify task-relevant visual evidence and ground their reasoning in local image regions. Existing agentic methods typically rely on reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards or supervised fine-tuning on large-scale annotated reasoning traces, leading to costly exploration, hand-designed verification rules, or heavy dependence on textual supervision. A natural way to avoid such external answer labels is to learn from trajectories sampled by the student itself, which points to On-Policy Distillation (OPD). To understand what OPD can and cannot provide for visual reasoning, we revisit it as negative-free stop-gradient alignment. This perspective shows that, although OPD provides effective token-level correction, its ceiling is constrained by the absence of trajectory-level discrimination. Motivated by these observations, we propose V-Zero, an answer-label-free framework for visual reasoning with contrastive evidence gating. V-Zero uses no annotated textual answer labels; instead, during training it pairs a question-relevant regional crop with a negative visual view to evaluate student-sampled trajectories and gate dense token-level distillation. Experiments on multiple visual reasoning benchmarks show that V-Zero consistently improves fine-grained visual reasoning while preserving strong generalization. Notably, V-Zero is more than 5$\times$ faster than previous supervised fine-tuning methods and more than 10$\times$ faster than reinforcement learning baselines. Code and dataset will be released at https://github.com/eVI-group-SCU/V-Zero

#### 摘要中文翻译
细粒度视觉推理需要模型识别局部关键证据。现有方法常依赖可验证奖励的强化学习或大量带答案、推理轨迹的监督数据。V-Zero 将 OPD 理解为无负样本的停止梯度对齐，并在词元监督之外增加轨迹级判别：学生只看完整图像生成多条回答，教师分别获得相关区域裁剪和负视觉视图，比较同一回答在两种视图下的支持程度，用其差异控制蒸馏权重，最终仍只向正视图教师学习。训练不使用标注文本答案；推理时不增加裁剪工具调用。多项视觉基准显示能力提升，作者同时报告较既有 SFT、RL 方案更短的训练时间。

#### 研究背景
教师在错误学生前缀上提供合理的下一词元，并不等于识别整条回答是否正确。本文用额外视觉证据对同一轨迹的影响作为可靠性信号，试图筛选更依赖真实图像细节的回答。其监督来源仍包括训练数据提供的相关区域裁剪；“不使用答案标签”不等于没有区域标注或特权视觉信息。

#### Method
每个完整图像与问题采样 $G=8$ 条学生回答。正视图是问题相关区域；负视图来自原图缩小两倍后、目标区域以外的等大小随机区域。教师始终保留原始完整图像，同时接收额外的正或负视图。沿相同学生前缀计算
$$
\Delta_k^{(g)}=\log\pi_t(y_k^{(g)}\mid x,z^+,y_{<k}^{(g)})-
\log\pi_t(y_k^{(g)}\mid x,z^-,y_{<k}^{(g)}),
\qquad p^{(g)}=\frac1{T_g}\sum_k\Delta_k^{(g)}.
$$
在同题兄弟回答内标准化 $p^{(g)}$，得到 $a^{(g)}$；门控为 $w^{(g)}=\operatorname{sg}[\operatorname{clip}(1+a^{(g)},0,2)]$。负视图只决定权重，不作为学生拟合目标。实际更新使用正视图的采样反向 KL surrogate：
$$
\widetilde L=\frac1G\sum_g\frac{w^{(g)}}{T_g}
\sum_k\operatorname{sg}[\log\pi_s(y_k^{(g)})-\log\pi_t^+(y_k^{(g)})]\log\pi_s(y_k^{(g)}).
$$
因此门控对整条轨迹相同，密集的正视图蒸馏仍在词元层实施。[原文 Method、算法 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25319)

### 2. PolicyAlign: Direct Policy-Based Safety Alignment for Large Language Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25442
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25442
- Authors: Chang Wu, Junfeng Fang, Houcheng Jiang, Kai Tang, Pengyu Cheng, Xiaoxi Jiang, Guanjun Jiang, Xiang Wang
- 作者单位：阿里巴巴 Qwen Large Model Application Team；新加坡国立大学；中关村学院。
- arXiv published: 2026-06-24
- arXiv updated: 2026-06-24
- Categories: cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Safety alignment of large language models (LLMs) typically depends on high-quality supervision data, such as safe demonstrations or preference pairs. However, in real-world deployment, emerging safety requirements are often specified as natural-language policies, while corresponding supervision data may be costly, delayed, or unavailable. This creates a mismatch between rapidly evolving safety policies and conventional data-driven alignment methods. To address this, we propose PolicyAlign, a simple yet effective framework for directly aligning LLMs with safety policies. Given a safety policy, PolicyAlign first synthesizes policy-violating instructions and then performs on-policy self-distillation to internalize policy-guided behavior. To improve training stability and data efficiency, we further introduce Policy-Sensitive Filtering, which selects instructions where the policy induces the largest behavioral shift. Experiments across multiple models show that PolicyAlign consistently improves safety while maintaining low over-refusal and preserving general capabilities. PolicyAlign also generalizes to medical, legal, and financial safety scenarios, highlighting its potential as a scalable and maintainable approach to policy-based LLM safety alignment. The code is released at https://github.com/Qwen-Applications/PolicyAlign.

#### 摘要中文翻译
常规安全对齐依赖高质量安全示范或偏好标注，而新出现的要求往往首先以自然语言政策发布，训练数据来得更晚。PolicyAlign 直接将政策作为教师上下文：先合成违反政策的指令，再用在线自蒸馏将带政策教师的行为迁入不带政策的学生。政策敏感度筛选优先选择政策会显著改变回答行为的指令。多个模型和通用、医疗、法律、金融基准显示，该方法提高了安全性，并比所比较的训练基线更好地保留正常请求响应能力与一般能力。

#### 研究背景
政策更新和标注数据建设存在时间差，长政策持续放入推理上下文也增加依赖。论文将上下文内的规则服从转化为参数更新：教师和学生来自同一初始模型，但只有教师得到政策。知识来源是政策文本对已有模型行为的调制，而非额外训练一个专门奖励模型。

#### Method
首先用强模型依据政策生成覆盖不同类别与表达方式的候选指令。对每条指令分别采样政策条件教师回答 $y_T$ 和默认学生回答 $y_S$，都用无政策学生评分，定义
$$
s(x)=\frac{1}{|y_S|}\log\pi_\theta(y_S\mid x)
-\frac{1}{|y_T|}\log\pi_\theta(y_T\mid x).
$$
选择分数最高的候选，称为 Policy-Sensitive Filtering。分数高表示学生更偏好自己的默认回答，而不易产生政策条件下的回答；它是行为差异分数，不是直接观测的安全标签。

训练在学生当前生成的前缀上最小化
$$
\mathcal L=\mathbb E_{x,\hat y\sim\pi_\theta}
\sum_tD_{\rm KL}\!\left(
\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid x,\hat y_{<t})
\;\|\;
\pi_{\rm ref}(\cdot\mid x,\mathcal C,\hat y_{<t})
\right).
$$
教师以 EMA 更新，衰减 0.99；实际在学生 Top-256 token 上近似 KL。教师接受政策、学生不接受，推理时无需重新附加政策。作者选择反向 KL，是为了抑制学生对教师弱支持行为的概率分配。[原文第 3 节及 4.1 节](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25442)

### 3. On-Policy Self-Distillation with Sampled Demonstrations Reduces Output Diversity

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26091
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26091
- Authors: Andrei Liviu Nicolicioiu, Mohammad Pezeshki, Aaron Courville
- 作者单位：Mila；Université de Montréal；FAIR at Meta；CIFAR AI Chair。
- arXiv published: 2026-06-24
- arXiv updated: 2026-06-24
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy self-distillation achieves strong pass@1 accuracy by using a single model as both teacher and student, with the teacher conditioned on a correct demonstration to provide dense token-level feedback. We show that this could come at a hidden cost: rollout diversity decreases and pass@k curves flatten (i.e., generating more rollouts fails to improve accuracy). We trace this to compounding biases in the design of self-distillation with sampled demonstrations. The teacher scores each student rollout while conditioned on a sampled correct rollout, channeling its feedback through the model's own biases. We theoretically analyze the optimal self-distillation policy and show that it tilts the base distribution by a pointwise conditional mutual information score between the student's rollout and the correct rollout used as context. Unlike the ideal optimal on-policy reinforcement learning (RL), which preserves probability ratios among equally correct rollouts, self-distillation can amplify existing probability gaps, concentrating mass on already-dominant modes. On a controlled graph path-finding task and science question-answering benchmarks, self-distilled models match or exceed RL on average performance but exhibit substantially lower functional and semantic diversity, failing on out-of-distribution settings that require diverse strategies.

#### 摘要中文翻译
使用正确示范作教师上下文的在线自蒸馏，可以提升单次回答准确率，却可能降低 rollout 多样性，使 pass@k 随采样数增加的收益变平。本文认为，学生轨迹和正确示范的双重采样，以及教师对与示范相似解法的偏好，会不断放大已有概率不平衡。理论上，在固定教师和示范分布的正则化模型中，最优策略按与示范的逐点条件互信息倾斜基础分布；这不同于仅按正确性奖励倾斜的理想 RL 最优解。概念图寻路和科学问答实验中，自蒸馏的平均准确率可匹敌或超过 RL，但功能及语义多样性较低，在需要不同策略的分布外任务上失效。

#### 研究背景
正确回答不只有一种。若从当前模型的成功回答中抽取示范，常见解法更容易进入教师上下文，教师又更容易支持相似轨迹，形成重复强化。文章区分三种观测量：pass@1 衡量平均成功；pass@k 的增长体现更多采样是否覆盖新题；概念路径类别衡量高层策略差异。token 熵仅反映局部不确定性，未必对应后两者。

#### Method
研究对象是 SDSD：学生为每題生成多条回答，从验证正确的集合中抽取示范，EMA 教师看到正确示范和学生前缀，学生最小化与教师的 token 级反向 KL。理论将教师冻结为基础策略 $\pi_0$，将正确示范分布 $p_{\rm corr}$ 固定，并加入相对基础策略的 KL 正则：
$$
\min_\pi\;\mathbb E_cD_{\rm KL}(\pi\|\pi_0(\cdot\mid x,c))
+\beta D_{\rm KL}(\pi\|\pi_0(\cdot\mid x)).
$$
其最优解是
$$
\pi^*_{\rm SD}(y\mid x)\propto\pi_0(y\mid x)
\exp\!\left(\frac{\mathbb E_c\,i(y;c\mid x)}{1+\beta}\right),\qquad
i(y;c\mid x)=\log\frac{\pi_0(y\mid x,c)}{\pi_0(y\mid x)}.
$$
相同奖励的两条轨迹在 KL 正则 RL 最优解下保留基础概率比；SDSD 还会按两者预期 PCMI 的差异重加权。只有当更常见轨迹也有更高预期 PCMI 时，已有不平衡才进一步放大，不能将其改述为所有自蒸馏都必然坍缩。附录 B.3 给出固定前缀的下一词元版本。[原文第 2—3 节及附录 B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26091)

### 4. FORCE: Efficient VLA Reinforcement Fine-Tuning via Value-Calibrated Warm-up and Self-Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26006
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26006
- Authors: Shuyi Zhang, Yunfan Lou, Hongyang Cheng, Yichen Guo, Chuyao Fu, Yaoxu Lyu, Xiaojie Zhang, Haoran Li, Pengwei Wang, Zhongyuan Wang, Shanghang Zhang
- 作者单位：中国科学院自动化研究所；北京智源人工智能研究院；北京大学计算机学院多媒体信息处理国家重点实验室。
- arXiv published: 2026-06-24
- arXiv updated: 2026-06-24
- Categories: cs.RO, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要同时包含 on-policy/on policy 与 distillation/self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models are often constrained by the imitation ceiling imposed by sub-optimal data. While Reinforcement Learning (RL) fine-tuning can surpass this limit, it is notoriously sample inefficient. This challenge arises from two core issues: (1) catastrophic initial unlearning due to an unstable Q-function and (2) inefficient policy updates caused by low-quality exploration data, often forcing a reliance on costly human interventions. We introduce FORCE, a 3-stage framework that stabilizes fine-tuning by tackling both issues. FORCE first incorporates a Value-Calibrated Warm-Up phase, utilizing on-policy rollouts to mitigate the distributional shift of the Q-function. Subsequently, during the online stage, this calibrated Q-function acts as a filter for both the policy's own action proposals and expert data, ensuring only high-value actions are used for the policy update. We evaluate FORCE on various simulation and real-world tasks, and the result shows that FORCE achieves a 79% absolute improvement in success rates and outperform prior RL methods by 10%, while accelerating training by 32.5%. Critically, it mitigates the common success rate drop and achieves this robust performance without human intervention, marking a significant step towards deploying capable and autonomous robotic agents.

#### 摘要中文翻译
VLA 模型的模仿学习受示范质量限制，而直接在线强化微调又常在初期掉点，并因低质量探索而浪费交互。FORCE 采用三阶段流程：先在示范上做保守离线 RL，再用策略自身采集的数据校准价值函数，最后通过价值引导的策略自蒸馏筛选在线学习目标。仿真与真实机器人结果显示成功率和样本效率改善；作者报告约 79 个百分点的成功率提升及 32.5% 的训练加速。其主要证据是具体任务的成功率和达到目标成功率所需环境步数，而非通用语言模型蒸馏的 token 指标。

#### 研究背景
离线 critic 的数据支持与在线策略访问状态不一致，可能造成 offline-to-online 的初始性能下降。仅靠行为克隆不能超越示范，直接追逐不可靠 Q 又会破坏已有行为。论文把价值校准和高价值动作再拟合结合，目标是在在线阶段减少人工接管纠正。

#### Method
第一阶段以专家轨迹训练 Cal-QL critic，并组合行为克隆与 Q 最大化训练 actor。第二阶段采集当前策略 rollout 与专家数据混合，继续校准 critic；只对成功样本施加 BC，Q 项使用混合状态。第三阶段维护专家缓冲区和策略缓冲区，等量抽样；前者包含离线示范及在线成功轨迹，后者包含所有在线轨迹。

VGPD 从正则策略改进
$$
\max_\pi\;\mathbb E_\pi Q(s,a)-\tau D_{\rm KL}(\pi\|\pi_{\rm old})
$$
得到目标形式 $\pi^*(a\mid s)\propto\pi_{\rm old}(a\mid s)e^{Q(s,a)/\tau}$。对策略缓冲区状态采样 $K$ 个当前动作，以其平均 Q 为 $q_{\rm mean}$。若缓冲区原动作的 Q 不低于该均值，则继续拟合原动作；否则只保留 $Q(s,a_k)\geq q_{\rm mean}$ 的候选，按
$$
\widetilde w_k=
\frac{\mathbf1[Q(s,a_k)\geq q_{\rm mean}]e^{Q(s,a_k)/\tau}}
{\sum_j\mathbf1[Q(s,a_j)\geq q_{\rm mean}]e^{Q(s,a_j)/\tau}}
$$
加权拟合。专家样本直接执行 BC。实际策略采用 consistency action head；这是高价值动作分布的自蒸馏，不是语言模型教师 logits 的 OPD。[原文第 3 节、附录算法 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26006)

#### 我的分析：优点

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

### 5. Causal-rCM: A Unified Teacher-Forcing and Self-Forcing Open Recipe for Autoregressive Diffusion Distillation in Streaming Video Generation and Interactive World Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25473
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25473
- Authors: Kaiwen Zheng, Guande He, Min Zhao, Jintao Zhang, Huayu Chen, Jianfei Chen, Chen-Hsuan Lin, Ming-Yu Liu, Jun Zhu, Qianli Ma
- 作者单位：清华大学；NVIDIA；University of Texas at Austin。
- arXiv published: 2026-06-24
- arXiv updated: 2026-06-24
- Categories: cs.CV, cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要同时包含 on-policy/on policy 与 distillation/self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Autoregressive video diffusion with causal diffusion transformers has emerged as a major paradigm for real-time streaming video generation and action-conditioned interactive world models. In this work, we extend rCM, an advanced diffusion distillation framework, to autoregressive video diffusion. The core philosophy of rCM lies in the complementarity between forward and reverse divergences, represented by consistency models (CMs) and distribution matching distillation (DMD), respectively, in diffusion distillation. This philosophy naturally carries over to the autoregressive setting, where teacher-forcing (TF) provides an offline, forward-divergence causal training paradigm, while self-forcing (SF) corresponds to an on-policy, reverse-divergence refinement. Our contributions are: (1) through extensive experiments, we show that teacher-forcing CM is currently the best complement to self-forcing DMD as an initialization strategy (2) we present the first implementation of teacher-forcing-based continuous-time CMs (e.g., sCM/MeanFlow) for autoregressive video diffusion, enabled by our custom-mask FlashAttention-2 JVP kernel, achieving 10$\times$ faster convergence compared to discrete-time CMs (dCMs) (3) we introduce Causal-rCM, a leading, unified, and scalable algorithm-infrastructure open recipe for diffusion distillation and causal training (4) we achieve state-of-the-art streaming video generation performance in both frame-wise and chunk-wise settings, using only synthetic data for training. Notably, our distilled 2-step causal Wan2.1-1.3B model achieves a VBench-T2V score of 84.63 with only 1 or 2 sampling steps. We further apply Causal-rCM to Cosmos 3, an advanced omnimodal world foundation model for physical AI with action-conditioned generation capability, enabling an interactive world model.

#### 摘要中文翻译
自回归视频扩散通过因果 Transformer 支持流式视频与动作条件世界模型，但 teacher-forcing 和真实生成存在上下文差异，直接 self-forcing 分布匹配又对初始化和模式覆盖敏感。Causal-rCM 将离线的一致性蒸馏与在线生成分布匹配组成分阶段流程，并实现带自定义因果掩码的 FlashAttention-2 JVP，使连续时间 sCM/MeanFlow 能用于因果视频蒸馏。论文报告，连续时间一致性初始化比离散版本以约十分之一训练迭代数达到更好初始质量；Wan2.1-1.3B 的逐帧 1/2 步模型达到 VBench-T2V 84.63，并展示 Cosmos 3 的动作条件交互生成。

#### 研究背景
TF 使用真实历史，DF 为真实历史加噪，两者都不完全覆盖模型生成历史中的累积误差。SF 则在学生自己生成的视频上下文中训练，但 DMD 的反向散度倾向可能造成模式收缩。本文的重点是稳定初始化、在线细化与工程执行之间的组合，而非语言模型上的词元 KL 蒸馏。

#### Method
流程分三阶段：先以 TF 将双向扩散模型适配为因果模型；再用 TF-CM 把因果教师蒸馏成少步学生；最后用 SF-DMD 在学生自回归 rollout 上细化。TF 前向将干净历史和噪声目标拼接，掩码仅允许目标看到过去历史与本块；损失只施加在噪声分支。

RF 坐标下的一致性映射为 $f_\theta(x_t,t)=x_t-tv_\theta(x_t,t)$。连续时间目标需要沿教师 ODE 的切向量：
$$
h=v_{\rm teacher}-v_{\theta^-}
-t\,\operatorname{JVP}\!\left(v_{\theta^-};(x_t,t),(v_{\rm teacher},1)\right).
$$
干净历史的切向量设为零，JVP 与原前向采用同一因果掩码。新内核以允许访问的 query-key 区间表示掩码，流式计算 primal 与 tangent，避免构造完整注意力中间量。

SF-DMD 用双向真实教师和学生样本上训练的 fake-score 网络提供分布差异。每块仅最后一步去噪保留梯度，先前步骤和历史 KV 均停止梯度。可选 replayed backward 在无梯度 rollout 后重算各块末步以降低显存。Noisy context 直接复用末次去噪 KV，将常规每块 $N+1$ 次前向降为 $N$；自定义步数让首块做 4 步，后续块做 1 或 2 步。附录 A 还证明 RF 与 TrigFlow 的一致性映射可等价，但加入切向量归一化后的训练目标通常不等价。[原文第 3 节、附录 A—B](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25473)
