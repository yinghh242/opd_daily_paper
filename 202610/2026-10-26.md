### 1. When Fine-Grained Credit Assignment Helps and Hurts: Multimodal Agentic Reinforcement Learning through Video Editing

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

#### Abstract 原文

> Reinforcement learning (RL) has shown promise for training multimodal agents to reason and use tools. However, it remains unclear when fine-grained credit assignment improves learning and when it fails to improve task performance. Video editing provides a useful testbed for this question, combining multimodal perception, long-horizon planning, and tool use with delayed outcome feedback. We construct four movie-trailer editing task types---Content Selection, Narrative Sequencing, Rough Cut, and Fine Cut---that reflect real-world editing workflows and span different levels of task complexity and interaction horizon. Decaying process rewards provides the largest benefit on Fine Cut, with a 45.4% relative gain over outcome-only GRPO on tasks averaging more than 60 turns under outcome-only GRPO. On the three shorter-horizon task types averaging 30--40 turns under the same baseline, outcome-only GRPO remains competitive, with decaying process rewards yielding at most a 10.4% relative gain. Process rewards from an LLM judge initially improve tool use and encourage visual inspection. As training progresses, however, GRPO+PRM with a fixed process-reward weight increasingly exhibits excessive reasoning and redundant tool calls: process rewards continue to rise while task completion rates and outcome scores decline. Decaying the process-reward weight to zero mitigates this reward-hacking behavior while preserving the early gains. Despite dense token-level supervision, on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) yields little improvement or substantial degradation and is highly sensitive to the choice of privileged information. Our analysis reveals differences in teacher--student disagreement and gradient magnitudes, and suggests possible dilution of informative signals over long trajectories.

#### Abstract 原文

> General-purpose embodied intelligence requires robots to coordinate diverse skills over long horizons, track task progress, and recover from failures. Specialist policies alone do not ensure task-level reliability, while powerful multimodal agents rely on costly reasoning and external experience that compact models struggle to reproduce. We introduce OmniBrain-0, a lightweight unified embodied agent that coordinates manipulation and navigation through a shared high-level decision model, integrating task understanding, planning, progress tracking, and feedback-driven recovery. Our progressive training framework combines embodied pretraining, domain-specialized agent distillation, and multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD). It distills powerful teachers' interaction experience into manipulation and navigation experts, then consolidates their decision capabilities into OmniBrain-0. Unification therefore occurs within the learned decision model rather than only at the tool interface. We further introduce Rubric-Guided Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI), a data flywheel in which a Meta-Agent turns execution evidence into refined rubrics, step-level reminders, and verified trajectories for domain-expert updates before their reconsolidation into OmniBrain-0 through MOPD. We evaluate OmniBrain-0 on LIBERO-Pro, LIBERO-Plus, RoboCasa365, RoboTwin 2.0, and R2R-CE Val-Unseen, as well as multiple real-world manipulation tasks. OmniBrain-0 combines strong cross-domain task performance with substantially lower inference and execution overhead than its closed-source teacher, demonstrating efficient transfer and consolidation of embodied capabilities into a compact unified agent.

### 3. How Much Does the Hint Matter? Scope Conditions for Hindsight Self-Distillation in Financial Reasoning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36082
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.36082
- Authors: Johnny Yu, Cyril Gorlla
- 作者单位：待全文阅读确认
- arXiv published: 2026-10-26
- arXiv updated: 2026-10-26
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Financial institutions operating under data-residency constraints are restricted to open-weight models small enough to locally host, which trail frontier systems on multi-step quantitative reasoning. On-policy distillation narrows such gaps by supervising a student on its own rollouts, but traditional OPD requires a strong external teacher, and on-policy self-distillation (OPSD) conditions on a full reference solution. We ask how much of that signal is necessary. We present Fin-SD, a localized hindsight self-distillation recipe for multi-step reasoning. Given a problem the student fails, the student itself identifies the first incorrect reasoning step and writes a short critique of that step. We truncate the student's trajectory immediately before the error (aiming to obtain a correct prefix), condition a frozen copy of the student on the same prefix plus the hint, and distill that hint-conditioned teacher into the student over a short rollout under reverse KL. No external model contributes a gradient. We apply Fin-SD to GPT-OSS-20B on a custom multi-step financial reasoning benchmark that requires deriving formula inputs from realistic scenarios via accounting conventions. We find: (i) replacing the critique with a generic-feedback control matches Fin-SD's entire 3.03 pp accuracy gain over the base model on the FinanceReasoning Hard subset, suggesting the gain comes from where supervision is applied, not hint content; (ii) the model writing the hint does not need to be stronger than the model receiving it: hints from same-family GPT-OSS-120B, at roughly 6x the parameters, yield no materially greater benefits than self-written hints; and (iii) Fin-SD matches on-policy self-distillation from full reference solutions on accuracy while generating 9.5% fewer tokens. With Fin-SD, general reasoning on MMLU-Pro and a fourteen-metric compliance audit on FinTrust show no material regression.

### 4. EvoRouter: Cost-Efficient Multi-Agent Routing via Insight-Guided On-Policy Distillation

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Serving web queries at scale with multi-agent systems requires jointly configuring large language models, collaboration modes, agent counts, interaction rounds, and skills. However, the resulting configuration space grows combinatorially, while sparse execution rewards provide limited fine-grained supervision for interdependent planning decisions. We propose EvoRouter, a self-evolving multi-agent routing framework driven by insight-guided on-policy distillation. A dedicated expert periodically synthesizes performance-cost feedback from configuration trajectories across tasks into reusable conditional routing insights about capability requirements, returns from resource scaling, and interactions among configuration choices. A privileged teacher combines these evolving insights with current execution feedback to provide dense token-level supervision for the router's own planning trajectories through on-policy distillation. Cost-aware reinforcement learning jointly optimizes task performance and computational cost. Together, these training signals internalize execution insights into the routing policy. The updated policy generates new trajectories that support subsequent insight extraction, forming an execution-driven self-improvement loop. We evaluate EvoRouter on 19 in-domain datasets spanning five domains and two out-of-domain benchmarks. EvoRouter achieves an average in-domain accuracy of 68.69%, outperforming the strongest routing baseline by 7.23 percentage points while reducing inference compute cost by 50.95%. Our code is open-sourced at https://github.com/CenYangtze/EvoRouter.
