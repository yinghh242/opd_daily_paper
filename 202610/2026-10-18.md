#### Abstract 原文

> Large-scale action pretraining equips Embodied Foundation Models (EFMs) with strong manipulation capabilities, with particular strengths in generalization and closed-loop robustness, but reproducing such pretraining requires substantial robot data and computation. To transfer these capabilities from pretrained EFMs to policies without action pretraining, we introduce TILDE (Teacher-In-the-Loop Distillation from EFMs), which synergizes on-policy distillation at student-induced states with learning from teacher-executed recovery trajectories. We further propose AutoDAgger, an autonomous data aggregation pipeline in which a robotic reward model is utilized to monitor task progress and trigger teacher intervention, thereby collecting recovery experience without human monitoring or manual control. AutoDAgger accelerates recovery-trajectory collection by more than $2\times$ compared with the human-in-the-loop method. By unifying learning from demonstrations, on-policy teacher supervision, and teacher's corrective behavior, TILDE improves task performance by up to $4.1\times$ over behavior cloning across three real-world tasks and supports effective cross-architecture distillation.

#### Abstract 原文

> Teacher takeover in on-policy distillation (OPD) can produce incorrect responses even when the student and teacher independently answer the same problem correctly, a failure we quantify using Collaboration Failure Rate (CFR). To address it, we propose Contextual Digest for On-Policy Distillation (CD-OPD). CD-OPD structures the earlier context of teacher guidance through a teacher-generated dependency graph, curating the required problem conditions and preceding reasoning as node summaries alongside the unchanged guidance in a Contextual Digest before student continuation. We further develop online meta-reweighting, which compares Digest segment gradients with a task gradient from independent student responses to improve student-only performance, favoring updates predicted to reduce task loss. Across eight benchmarks, CD-OPD improves average accuracy by 1.32--7.05 percentage points over representative existing OPD methods. It also reduces CFR by 27.4\%--56.4\% relative to existing takeover-based OPD methods.

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) trains agents on their own trajectories using supervision from a stronger teacher. We uncover a counterintuitive phenomenon in multi-turn agentic tasks: a smaller, initially weaker student can substantially outperform its teacher during training, only to regress as distillation continues. We term this phenomenon distillation overshoot. Through decision- and task-level analyses, we trace overshoot to two competing effects of OPD: repairing weak student decisions and eroding successful student choices that the teacher disfavors. Early in training, repair outpaces erosion, allowing the student to combine the teacher's corrections with its own strengths and surpass the teacher. As training continues, erosion overtakes repair, causing these gains to vanish. Motivated by this finding, we propose Success-Preserving On-Policy Distillation (SPOD), which selectively masks conflicting teacher supervision on successful student choices and introduces a soft probability floor to counteract their erosion, while retaining teacher guidance elsewhere. SPOD requires only binary episode success and model probabilities already available during OPD. Across two model families and three agentic benchmarks, SPOD consistently outperforms ordinary OPD and evaluated variants, improving final success by up to 15.2 percentage points and surpassing the teacher by up to 20.0 points. Moreover, SPOD's final checkpoints outperform even ordinary OPD's best intermediate checkpoints in all six settings, demonstrating that beyond-teacher gains can be not only preserved but further extended.
