### 1. Teach the Magnitude, Not the Direction: Verifier-Bounded Credit Assignment for Multi-Turn Multi-step LLM Agents

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13179
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13179

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) offers a verifier-bounded performance ceiling for training multi-turn tool-use agents, yet its trajectory-level credit assignment conflates heterogeneous per-turn outcomes into a single reward signal. On-policy distillation provides dense per-token supervision but is either teacher-bounded or prone to gradient concentration collapse. We introduce $\textbf{CrEST}$, a hierarchical credit assignment framework that retains RL's verifier-bounded ceiling while incorporating dense token-level signals from a privileged self-teacher. $\textbf{CrEST}$ resolves credit at two levels: turn-segmented verified advantages address inter-turn dilution, while entropy-gated self-teacher modulation refines intra-turn token contributions. Experiments on BFCL V3 and WildToolBench show that $\textbf{CrEST}$ consistently outperforms both RL and distillation baselines across two model scales, with the largest gains on long-trajectory and strict session-level metrics. Our work demonstrates that the teacher's role in policy optimization can be reduced from determining update directions to modulating update magnitudes, unlocking dense credit assignment without sacrificing the verifier-bounded ceiling.

#### 摘要中文翻译

多轮多步骤智能体的整段奖励混合不同用户轮的成功与失败，普通 OPD 虽提供稠密信号，也可能受教师偏好与梯度集中影响。CrEST 以分层信用分配结合两者：每个用户轮独立计算可验证优势，特权自教师再通过不确定性门调制该轮内 token 的更新幅度。BFCL V3 与 WildToolBench 两规模实验中，方法整体优于比较基线，长上下文与严格整会话指标改善较明显。作者主张教师只决定幅度、验证奖励决定方向；严格成立的范围是逐 token 标量优势符号保持。

#### 研究背景

同一 session 的不同用户请求可能一成一败，不能把全局均值奖励广播给所有轮；但只分轮仍无法区分关键参数和格式 token。研究利用基准提供的对齐用户轮和逐轮验证，先解决跨轮误归因，再调节轮内贡献。

#### Method

在相同有序用户请求的 rollout 组内，逐轮标准化 $R_k$ 为 $A_k$。自教师额外看到先前轮的正确工具调用结果，计算 $\Delta_t=(\log\pi_T-\log\pi_S)/2$。将 $e^{\operatorname{sgn}(A_k)\Delta_t}$ 裁剪到 $[0.72,1.28]$；只有教师差值与优势同号时开方向门，采样 token surprisal 的标准化 sigmoid 控制额外放大。最终

$$
\mathcal A_t=A_{[t]}\phi_t,\qquad \phi_t=1+\lambda_t^{eff}(w_t-1),\quad 1\le\phi_t\le1.084.
$$

默认 $\lambda=0.3$，门强度裁剪不超过它。附录 A 真正证明的是 $\|\sum_t(\phi_t-1)g_t\|\le0.084\sum_t\|g_t\|$，不是相对聚合梯度的 8.4% 界，也不证明全局最优性能由 verifier 唯一决定。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

第6节指出仅两类工具使用基准和4B/8B，surprisal 是重要性启发式。附录A明确局部符号保持不保证聚合梯度方向、驻点或全局性能上限；设置要求同一用户轮语义对齐与逐轮可验证反馈，不能直接用于轮数变化或强耦合、只有终局奖励的任务。

#### 我的分析：优点

轮间/轮内两个干预层清楚，可在已有RL中局部实现；附录对符号保证与绝对梯度扰动界给出了正确边界。严格session指标保留了长交互失败累积的现实难度。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

主文“verifier-bounded ceiling”和相对梯度8.4%表述强于附录已限定的定理；必须以后者为准。不同token方向重新加权会改变最终参数方向，且去方向门后乘数仍可为正，不能据此说teacher翻转了单token符号。梯度集中诊断按优势幅度排序，并未完整报告参数梯度；长session仅128题，9.38%约12题成功，单训练seed与不等步数不足以支持广泛稳定性结论。

### 2. Latent On-Policy Self-Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13040
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13040
- Authors: Guibin Zhang, Jiayang Lyu, Ran Sun, Xinlei Yu, Haoyu Zhao, Qibing Ren, Shuicheng Yan
- 作者单位：National University of Singapore；北京邮电大学；上海交通大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-13
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-13
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Enabling agents to learn from experience and internalize it into their policy has become a central problem in self-evolving AI. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) offers an effective pathway by using a privileged self-teacher to provide dense supervision on the student's own trajectories; however, existing methods still rely heavily on designer-specified privileged artifacts (e.g., answers, feedback, skills, or trajectories), limiting the end-to-end learnability and scalability required for continual self-improvement. In this work, we introduce Latent On-Policy Self-Distillation (LOPD), which, rather than proposing another hand-crafted OPSD variant with a newly prescribed form of privileged context, makes the teacher's privileged context itself learnable end-to-end from experience. Technically, LOPD retrieves relevant experiences and composes them into continuous latent tokens that condition a self-teacher, while the student generates trajectories from the task and interaction history and receives dense token-level supervision at every visited prefix. We further introduce a privileged-margin objective to stabilize and regulate the learning of latent context. Empirically, LOPD demonstrates (I) strong performance, outperforming RLVR and representative OPSD methods including OPSD, SDPO, and Skill-SD across both agentic tool use and code generation; and (II) high learning efficiency, surpassing GRPO and Skill-SD with less than 30% of their rollout budget. Ablation studies further provide direct evidence that making privileged context learnable is necessary for realizing these gains. Together, these results position LOPD as a step toward a more scalable and self-directed paradigm for agent evolution.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPSD 通过特权自教师把经验内化，但特权信息通常是人为定义的答案、反馈、技能或轨迹。LOPD 将特权上下文本身设为端到端可学习：检索相关经验，压缩成连续latent token供教师读取，再在学生自身前缀提供密集监督。特权间隔目标避免可学习教师只向学生退化。工具调用与代码生成上，LOPD优于RLVR及代表性OPSD方法，并在较少rollout预算下达到更好表现；消融支持联合学习经验表示的作用。

#### 研究背景

关键不在再设计一种文本记忆格式，而在让经验压缩器按当前学生需求学习。由于师生共同最小化KL可能只让教师变容易，论文需额外结果约束维持有意义的监督。

#### Method

训练集成功经验形成冻结库，用Qwen3-Embedding-8B检索3条；带encoder LoRA与共享权重8层QFormer压缩器每条生成32 latent token，共96个。冻结教师backbone读取这些token，在学生前缀计算teacher top-20加tail桶反向KL；梯度通过教师激活回到composer，教师权重不更新。

令采样token特权 $\delta=\log\pi_T-\operatorname{sg}\log\pi_S$，轨迹结果符号 $A=2r-1$，要求平均 $\Delta=\mathbb E[A\delta]\geq m=0.05$。联合最小化蒸馏、初始latent锚定和对偶惩罚 $\beta(m-\Delta)$，对偶变量投影上升。推理删除经验库、检索器和composer，仅保留学生（第3节）。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未独立列limitations。作者承认解码latent得到碎片化词汇不能证明教师具体用了哪些信息；更多latent或检索并无单调收益，且无间隔联合优化会退化。基线保留各自KL方向与特权形式，并非只改变是否可学习一个因素。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

#### Abstract 原文

> Scientific discovery increasingly requires AI systems that can reason over scientific evidence of heterogeneous modalities, interact with scientific tools and environments, and sustain progress across long task horizons. We present Intern-S2-Preview, a series of scientific agentic foundation models designed to support multimodal scientific understanding, reasoning, generation, and long-horizon tasks. The training pipeline begins with scientific multimodal pre-training over rendered scientific documents, interleaved image-text data, and diverse scientific corpora. Starting from the pretrained checkpoint, we apply a unified post-training pipeline consisting of supervised fine-tuning, scalable multi-task reinforcement learning (RL), black- and white-box agentic RL, and on-policy distillation. #### 摘要中文翻译

科学发现需要理解异构证据、调用科学工具，并持续完成长时域工作。Intern-S2-Preview 通过科学文档视觉预训练、交错图文数据及科学语料建立基础，再采用SFT、多任务RL、黑白盒智能体RL及OPD整合能力。训练包含部分rollout、离策略修正、自适应长度调节、在线推测解码和精确轨迹组装。397B主模型增加数值时序预测分支；另独立研究冻结主模型的Memory Decoder领域扩展。评测覆盖科学、多模态、智能体及通用任务；4B记忆扩展把Biology-Instructions均值56.92提高到60.32。

#### 研究背景

报告将科学智能从单次问答延伸到文档、信号、可执行实验和多轮工具流程。OPD用于最终汇总两个广域专家；时序模块和Memory Decoder是不同功能，后者不是397B默认模型的一部分。

#### Method

科学页面视觉预训练预测下一视觉latent，另用解析后交错图文与visual-gain筛选保存跨页关系。时序编码器使用分块CNN、QFormer压缩与跨通道Transformer；数值预测器融合LLM语义及时间特征，独立预测长度和未来数值。

RL采用共置GPU部分rollout，完成足够样本即暂停在途请求并更新权重；保存逐token行为版本和概率，最旧片段超过三次更新则丢弃。以裁剪且停止梯度的importance weight作REINFORCE，R3复放MoE路由，双向二值KL筛除训练/推理数值异常。GEPO和长度调节作用于优势；在线draft用forward KL与TV混合跟踪策略。

OPD只整合从同一SFT点训练的推理专家和agent专家。先用双方轨迹做轻量SFT预热，按领域路由教师，优势 $A_t=\operatorname{sg}[\log\pi_{T_d}(y_t)-\log\pi_{prox}(y_t)]$，复用上述importance-weight更新。最长256K轨迹仅传采样token概率，教师负载由 $O(HV)$ 降为 $O(H)$（第4.5节）。

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

全文确认相关：I-SDPO 明确在学生生成轨迹上使用特权自蒸馏，并按实例是否全失败在 SDPO 与 GRPO 之间路由。摘要未使用标准 OPD/OPSD 短语，旧子串匹配理由不成立；此处以全文机制作为保留依据。

#### Abstract 原文

> Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) learns from reward differences within a rollout group, but receives no useful relative signal when every sampled response is incorrect. Privileged self-distillation can fill this gap with dense token supervision, yet applying it throughout training creates a different failure mode: the teacher is a biased, low-variance surrogate for the reward objective, so persistent imitation can oppose reward-improving updates after the policy becomes capable of producing successful trajectories. We introduce I-SDPO (Instance-Level Adaptive Self-Distillation Policy Optimization), which treats teacher reliance as capability-dependent. I-SDPO makes one routing decision per input instance and shares it across that instance's rollout group: all-incorrect groups use a privileged self-distillation objective, whereas any-success groups remain intact for GRPO. This design uses imitation only where group-relative rewards are uninformative. A local analysis characterizes when teacher and reward directions align and shows that a non-vanishing biased distillation weight induces an optimization bias floor. The routing rule automatically reduces the expected distillation rate as success probability rises, withdrawing teacher influence without a hand-designed schedule. On SciKnowEval, I-SDPO obtains the best result in all four scientific domains and improves average mean@16 accuracy from 56.67% with GRPO to 70.31%, with a maximum domain gain of 18.24 points.

#### 摘要中文翻译
GRPO依赖组内奖励差异，全部回答错误时会缺少有效学习信号。特权自蒸馏能提供稠密监督，但长期模仿有偏教师可能妨碍已经具备成功能力的学生继续优化奖励。I-SDPO对每个输入的完整回答组统一选择训练目标：全部错误且有参考解时使用自蒸馏，只要存在成功回答就保留整组GRPO。局部分析解释持续教师偏差的影响，并说明在独立采样假设下，蒸馏使用率随成功概率提高而下降。科学知识问答实验显示，它优于纯GRPO、纯自蒸馏及逐响应路由。

#### 研究背景
把每个错误回答都转为蒸馏会丢掉混合组中有价值的成功与失败对照。另一方面，教师看到正确解并不保证其风格偏好或错误前缀下的分布就是奖励最优方向。本文以整组是否出现成功作为可观测开关，在奖励没有区分力时才引入教师。

#### Method
每题采样一组回答。记$c_i$为是否存在达到正确阈值的回答，$m_i$为参考解是否可用，则蒸馏掩码$z_i^D=(1-c_i)m_i$，其他组进入GRPO。混合成功失败组完整保留；全对组也进入GRPO，通常没有相对优势。教师为学生EMA，额外接收正确解，在原学生前缀上评分。

蒸馏结合前向与反向KL：$(1-\alpha)\operatorname{KL}(q\Vert p)+\alpha\operatorname{KL}(p\Vert q)$，再以归一化$\exp(-\beta H(q_t))$加权词元，低熵只是置信代理。若每条回答条件独立、成功概率为$p$，整组全错率为$(1-p)^K$；这给出能力相关的使用率，但不保证$p$训练中单调提高。局部同曲率二次模型中的偏差解$\theta_\lambda^*=\theta^*+\lambda b/(1+\lambda)$说明持续偏置可能留下优化误差，不能当作Transformer全局收敛定理。阅读全文与超参数附录来源：[arXiv 官方正文](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.12957)。

### 5. HPSD: Hybrid-Policy Self-Distillation for Text-Image-to-Video Diffusion Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13205
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13205

- 作者单位：Shanghai Jiao Tong University；Nanyang Technological University，S-Lab；Fudan University；Shanghai Innovation Institute；Shanghai AI Laboratory；Adobe Research；JD.com；The Chinese University of Hong Kong；CPII under InnoHK；University of Science and Technology of China。

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Text-Image-to-Video (TI2V) models are an emerging unified architecture, where a single model simultaneously supports text-to-video (T2V) and image-to-video (I2V) generation. Given a high-quality first frame or a detailed textual prompt, TI2V models unlock substantially better visual quality than their T2V mode, raising a natural question: can the capability elicited by such privileged conditions be internalized into the model's own base generation ability? A common approach toward this goal is model self-distillation. However, the most straightforward solution, supervised fine-tuning, follows an off-policy strategy: its supervision is confined to teacher-generated endpoints from a fixed offline distribution rather than student-visited states, lacking precise correction tailored to the evolving policy. Recent on-policy distillation methods instead suffer from condition-state mismatch, where supervision is steered toward the given first frame instead of the student's actual content, misleading the correction. To achieve self-distillation that absorbs the teacher's privileged prior while retaining precise policy correction, in this work, we propose Hybrid-Policy Self-Distillation (HPSD), a novel self-distillation framework where a single TI2V model acts as both teacher and student under different conditions: the teacher operates in TI2V mode with a high-quality first frame and an enhanced prompt, while the student runs in the base T2V mode with only the vanilla prompt. Specifically, the student inherits off-policy teacher trajectory points as anchors, locally refines them toward its own policy, and finally receives velocity-level supervision on these self-generated roll-outs. Extensive experiments demonstrate that HPSD significantly improves T2V performance while also delivering notable TI2V gains, effectively strengthening the model's base generation ability.

#### 摘要中文翻译
统一文本图像到视频模型在获得高质量首帧和详细提示后，通常比只接收普通文本时生成得更好。本文研究能否将这种条件激发能力内化为基础文本到视频能力。普通离策略SFT缺少针对学生当前状态的纠正，而直接在学生状态上查询首帧条件教师，会把不一致的图像内容混入视频。HPSD先用教师轨迹提供内容一致的锚点，再让学生短程去噪，最后在这些混合策略状态上接受教师速度监督。实验显示该方式改善文本到视频质量，并对带首帧生成有所迁移。

#### 研究背景
TI2V教师把指定首帧作为干净条件，学生则自由生成全部帧。如果学生已经形成另一种场景，强行换上教师首帧就产生跨帧内容冲突。因此“教师有更好条件”并不保证它在所有学生状态上给出合理向量场。本文重点解决条件与状态的兼容性。

#### Method
离线用语言模型增强视频提示并编写首帧说明，再用图像模型生成首帧。在线EMA教师在这些条件下生成完整去噪轨迹，从中抽取锚点。锚点首帧重新加噪到与其他帧相同的时刻，使之成为学生合法的T2V输入；学生仅看普通文本，从锚点继续去噪若干步。

在所得状态上，教师再将首帧恢复为干净条件，提供速度目标。损失只作用于后续帧：
$$\mathcal L=\mathbb E\left\|v_\phi(x^{\mathrm{hyb}}_t,t\mid c)-\operatorname{sg}\widetilde v([c_{\mathrm{img}},x_t^{(2:F)}],t\mid c^+,c_{\mathrm{img}})\right\|_{2:F}^2.$$
教师首帧速度没有去噪意义，因此不用于监督。短子轨迹兼顾教师内容锚定与学生状态变化；长度为零就是教师轨迹蒸馏，更长也仍带教师初始化，不能等同于完全自由的在策略分布。教师参数用学生EMA刷新。全文及实现、额外实验附录来源：[arXiv 官方正文](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.13205)。

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13387
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.13387
- Authors: Enhan Li, Junhao He, Hongyang Du
- 作者单位：香港大学。

- arXiv updated: 2026-08-13
- Categories: cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) supervises a student language model on trajectories sampled from its current policy, but assigns equal credit to response tokens with unequal supervision value. Selective OPD addresses this limitation by allocating supervision non-uniformly across response tokens according to their estimated training value. Most existing criteria, however, focus primarily on optimization need, such as uncertainty or teacher-student disagreement, while task relevance, namely whether the supervision is tied to the semantic content of the current input, remains less directly characterized as a complementary dimension. To address this gap, we introduce Counterfactual Relevance for On-Policy Distillation (CROP), which operationalizes task relevance through a paraphrase-calibrated counterfactual sensitivity margin. For each source prompt, CROP constructs a validated original-paraphrase-counterfactual triplet, holds the student rollout fixed, and measures each response position by its sensitivity to a task-relevant condition change calibrated by its sensitivity to a meaning-preserving rewrite. Matched selection controls show that CROP identifies more useful supervision positions than random or lowest-relevance selection, while component comparisons confirm the value of both counterfactual sensitivity and paraphrase calibration. Across two teacher-student settings, CROP improves aggregate performance by 1.92 and 2.96 points over the strongest non-CROP selector. #### 摘要中文翻译

选择性OPD通常用不确定性、师生差异等判断token是否需要优化，却较少直接衡量它与当前任务语义的关联。CROP为每题构造原题、等义改写和单条件反事实三元组，保持学生回答固定，计算教师对反事实的敏感度减去其对等义改写的敏感度，再按每条回答预算选择监督位置。20%预算下两个师生配对分别超过最强非CROP基线1.43与0.96点；反向排序、学生评分等对照支持其选择信号，在5%—20%预算均有优势。

#### 研究背景

#### Method

### 7. Beyond Outcome Rewards: Step-Level Self-Distilled Policy Optimization for Deep Search Agents

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12764
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12764
- Authors: Haoze Wu, Chuqiao Kuang, Tianyi Zhuang, Xiaoguang Li
- 作者单位：香港科技大学；华为技术有限公司。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-13

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Deep search agents operate over trajectories spanning dozens of steps, yet standard reinforcement learning provides only a single outcome reward per trajectory, which is far too sparse for effective credit assignment. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) addresses this by using the model's own logits as dense token-level teachers, but extending it to search agents introduces a fundamental tension: the teacher, having access to privileged information such as the correct answer, produces a distribution that differs systematically from the student's exploration-based reasoning, and naive distillation causes the student to inherit this information asymmetry rather than learn better search strategies. We resolve this tension through two contributions. First, we construct Evidence Anchors, which are concise, step-level evidence snippets extracted from the web, as privileged information that captures key reasoning steps without revealing the entire answer path. Second, we propose Step-Level Self-Distilled Policy Optimization (SSPO), which converts teacher-student disagreement into step-level advantage weights within GRPO, applied exclusively to incorrect trajectories. This design decouples what to update from how much to update: the outcome reward determines the direction of policy change, while the teacher modulates its magnitude at each step. Correct trajectories are left untouched, preserving their diversity. On Qwen3-8B, SSPO consistently outperforms GRPO across BrowseComp, GAIA, and FRAMES, surpassing or matching GRPO trained with twice as many gradient steps while adding only about 5 percent overhead per step from a single additional forward pass.

#### 摘要中文翻译

深度搜索轨迹可跨数十步，单个结果奖励过于稀疏；直接自蒸馏又会让看过答案与证据的教师把信息不对称传给学生，导致提前停止搜索。本文构造简洁的网页 Evidence Anchors 作为训练特权，并提出 SSPO：仅在错误轨迹上，把师生分歧转换成轮级优势权重，验证器仍决定更新方向，教师只调节幅度。Qwen3-8B 在 BrowseComp、GAIA、FRAMES 中优于同配置 GRPO，100 步可达到或超过 200 步 GRPO，额外教师评分开销较小。

#### 研究背景

看到证据的教师无需经历学生的搜索过程，直接模仿会复制过短轨迹。本文将监督单位对齐到一次思考＋工具调用，而非独立 token。

#### Method

DeepSeek-V3.2 为约 6,000 训练问答采网页证据，均值 5.24 条；教师另见学生错误答案。每步联合概率由思考及工具调用 token 概率相乘，$\Delta_\tau=\operatorname{sg}\log(P_T/P_S)$。错误轨迹的轮权重为 $w_\tau=\min(\exp[\operatorname{sign}(A)\Delta_\tau],1.2)$，同轮 token 共享 $w_\tau A$；正确轨迹保持原优势。

当失败轨迹原优势为负，教师支持的中间步骤减轻处罚、不支持的步骤加强处罚。由于格式奖励，错误轨迹也可能具有正优势，公式显式按优势符号处理。教师每 50 步同步，观察 token 不计损失（第 3 节、附录 B）。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

附录 H 指出 API 成本限制数据与评测规模，仅测 8B、英语；更大、多语言及更多 agent 场景未验证。中间 checkpoint 使用 BrowseComp/FRAMES 子集。

#### 我的分析：优点

直接蒸馏、token 权重与轮权重有同 scaffold 对照；仅失败轨迹受调制，减少对多样成功策略的额外牵引。分离证据与错误答案反馈，并给出教师前向实际时间占比。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

联合 log-ratio 随轮长度累积，可能使权重频繁饱和，缺少长度归一化对照。anchor 来源验证主要检查 URL 可访问及标题，不保证内容支持性。保留优势符号不能消除特权信息对更新选择的影响；“避免泄漏”应理解为设计动机，而非信息论保证。当前无多训练种子，等步数效率仍需完整墙钟及离线证据构造成本。
