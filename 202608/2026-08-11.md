### 1. Test-Time Self-Evolving GUI Visual Grounding via Reflection-Guided On-Policy Self-Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11191
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.11191
- Authors: Shiyu Xuan, Zechao Li
- 作者单位：南京理工大学计算机科学与工程学院。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-11
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-11
- Categories: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> GUI Visual Grounding is a fundamental capability for GUI agents. Existing models typically freeze their parameters after deployment, limiting their ability to adapt to unseen interfaces. Although recent methods attempt to adapt models via test-time reinforcement learning, they cannot reflect upon failed exploration. To overcome this, we propose a Test-Time Self-Evolving framework that enables models to improve after deployment without human-annotated ground truth. It constructs a closed-loop of Exploration, Evaluation, Reflection, and Internalization. Specifically, the agent first explores unseen interfaces by predicting grounding coordinates for given instructions. To evaluate these explorations, we introduce an MLLM-based Reflector to assess the generated results and provide the corresponding reasoning reflections. To internalize reflection knowledge into the model weights, we propose Reflection-Guided On-Policy Self-Distillation, which translates high-level reasoning into dense token-level supervision via a conditioned self-teacher. Furthermore, we design a Contrastive Calibration method to prevent incorrect auto-regressive prefixes from corrupting the supervisory signals during failed explorations. Extensive experiments across six benchmarks demonstrate our framework's effectiveness, achieving an average accuracy improvement of 7.4% over the base model. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first work to successfully exploit on-policy self-distillation for test-time adaptation in GUI visual grounding. By filling the gap in post-deployment adaptation, our framework completes the self-evolving capability of GUI agents. The code will be released.

#### 摘要中文翻译

GUI 定位模型部署后通常参数冻结，难适应新界面；测试时 RL 虽能适配，却难利用失败原因。本文构造探索、评估、反思与内化闭环，用多模态 Reflector 评价坐标并生成解释，再通过反思条件自教师把文本反馈转为密集 token 监督。失败坐标的错误自回归前缀可能污染监督，因此加入对比校准。六个基准上相对基座最高平均改善7.4个百分点，测试适配阶段不使用人工真值。

#### 研究背景

定位输出通常是短坐标序列，第一个错误数字会影响后续数字的条件分布。方法不仅需要判别对错，还要使反馈集中在起始错误处，避免把已偏离前缀后的噪声学进参数。

#### Method

Reflector 先离线用 GroundCUA 真值训练，部署适配时冻结。它读取截图、指令和预测坐标，输出二值S与反思R。当前定位模型在S/R上下文形成自教师，和无反馈模型的token log-ratio作为优势。

对于失败预测，对比分母改为“错误预测其实正确”的反向提示分布，使起始错误获得负优势，并期望后续被错误前缀支配的两个分布趋同。再按S裁剪优势方向：成功只留正、失败只留负。完整方法将token优势以0.2系数加入GRPO组优势。定位与Reflector共享基座、切换LoRA；测试时适配仍更新权重，**并非 training-free**（Method）。

### 2. SkillLens: Visual Skill Cards for Retrieval-Augmented GUI Action Prediction and On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10775
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.10775
- Authors: Zhou Liu, Ligang Huang, Zeli Su, Zewei Pan, Zhaoyang Han, Xing Chen, Yuanfeng Song, Wentao Zhang
- 作者单位：北京大学；字节跳动；上海交通大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-11

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Computer-using agents can perceive rich software interfaces, yet their decisions often lack visual procedural memory: they may recognize individual controls without identifying which familiar workflow is active, which control matters next, or what evidence would confirm progress. Raw interaction traces preserve such information but are long and noisy to condition on, whereas text-only skills often omit the visual state that makes a procedure applicable. We introduce Visual Skill Cards (VSCs), a state-conditioned memory representation that binds reusable procedures with applicability cues, visual evidence, and verification signals. SkillLens constructs VSCs from heterogeneous interaction experience through Trace-to-Visual-Skill-Card and, at inference time, retrieves relevant cards and selectively expands only the evidence needed by a fixed visual-language model executor for grounded GUI action prediction. The same representation also supports CardDistill, which uses VSC evidence as privileged teacher context to train a student that acts without runtime card retrieval. Across Multimodal-Mind2Web and WebLINX-BrowserGym, SkillLens improves the frozen GPT-5.4-mini executor by +11.6 points in Step SR and +2.9 points in Overall, respectively; CardDistill further improves the corresponding student-only Qwen3-VL-2B metrics by +12.0 and +3.2 points.

#### 摘要中文翻译

计算机智能体可识别界面，却常缺少视觉流程记忆。SkillLens 提出 Visual Skill Cards，将可复用操作、适用状态、视觉证据和验证线索绑定，先检索再只展开必要证据，为固定 VLM 提供 GUI 动作指导。同一表示也用于 CardDistill：教师训练时看技能卡，学生在自身动作前缀上蒸馏，推理不再检索。GPT-5.4-mini 在 Mind2Web 的 Step SR 提高 11.6 点、WebLINX-BG Overall 提高 2.9 点；无检索 Qwen3-VL-2B 学生对应提高 12.0、3.2 点。

#### 研究背景

纯文本技能缺乏视觉适用条件，原始完整轨迹又冗长。重点是如何以有预算的接口把历史视觉过程交给固定执行器，并进一步内化。

#### Method

Trace-to-VSC 将源轨迹变成过程文本、状态/完成线索、截图或局部裁剪。默认检索用任务、历史、可见站点等元数据的词汇重叠，再重排兼容卡；高分卡才加载原始视觉证据。最终点击仍应定位于当前截图，不能复用卡中的坐标。

CardDistill 在学生生成的动作 token 前缀上做教师置信加权反向 KL：$\mathcal L=\sum_jw_jD_{\rm KL}(p_S\|p_T)$，$w_j=1-H(p_T)/\log|V|$。温度 1，不额外加动作 SFT；训练教师看源对齐卡，评价学生仅看基准原生上下文（方法节）。

### 3. ReOrder-OPD:Reliability-Aware Prompt Ordering for On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10905
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.10905
- Authors: Ximo Zhu, Ruiqi Liu, Rong Wang, Ping Wu, Xiang Zheng, Wenzhuo Xu, Xubin Yao, Zhiyuan Yan, Bo Li, Jun Gao, Xiaolei Lv
- 作者单位：挚文集团（Hello Group Inc.）；中国科学院自动化研究所；中国科学院大学前沿交叉科学学院；北京大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-11
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-11
- Categories: cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) applies token-level teacher supervision to student-generated trajectories, but this supervision is not always reliable. Existing methods use local confidence or teacher-student agreement to weight, filter, or truncate the sampled trajectory. These signals do not directly determine whether the teacher can continue a student prefix to a correct answer, and trajectory-level interventions can conflate one rollout's unreliability with low expected training value of its prompt. We define prompt-level teacher continuation reliability $R$ as the teacher's probability of reaching a correct answer from a student prefix, averaged over prefixes and trajectories induced by the current student. Oracle experiments show that high-$R$ prompts yield larger OPD gains and that descending-$R$ training outperforms random and ascending orders on a fixed prompt pool. Because estimating $R$ requires many teacher continuations, we use the maximum ROUGE-5 F1 between one independent student rollout and verifier-correct same-prompt teacher trajectories. Across ten equal-frequency bins of this actual score, mean $R$ rises monotonically, showing that the proxy separates coarse reliability levels. ReOrder-OPD sorts prompts by the proxy, then draws independent on-policy training trajectories for vanilla OPD. It improves every matched aggregate comparison across Qwen3 and Gemma4 mathematics settings and Qwen3 code settings. Gains in all six FiRe-OPD and ExOPD settings show that prompt ordering complements within-trajectory supervision.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD 的局部教师置信度或师生一致性，不能直接判断教师能否从学生前缀继续得到正确答案；在单条轨迹内筛选也可能错把坏 rollout 等同于低价值题目。本文定义教师续写可靠性 $R$，对学生轨迹及其中间前缀上的教师续写成功率取期望。Oracle 实验表明高 $R$ 题目更利于 OPD，固定题池按 $R$ 降序优于随机与升序。实际用一条独立学生完整响应，与同题验证正确的教师响应库之间最大 ROUGE-5 F1 作粗排序代理。ReOrder-OPD 只改题目访问顺序，训练时重新在线生成；在 Qwen3/Gemma4 数学和 Qwen3 代码设置中改善匹配平均成绩，也能叠加 FiRe-OPD、ExOPD。

#### 研究背景

课程应考虑“教师在这个学生诱导状态上还能可靠指导吗”，不只是题目内在难度。排序先影响早期模型，再改变后续同题的采样轨迹，因此即使题池、目标和更新数不变，访问顺序也可改变训练结果。

#### Method

Oracle 量为 $R(x)=\mathbb E_{y\sim\pi_S}\mathbb E_{z\sim G(y)}\mathbb E_{c\sim\pi_T(\cdot\mid x,z)}V(x,z\oplus c)$。诊断每题 16 学生轨迹、每条 5 前缀、每前缀 16 教师续写，成本很高。

实际每题生成最多 16 条教师答案并保留 verifier 正确者，再独立生成一条学生评分响应，计算 $q(x)=\max_{y_T\in C_T(x)}\mathrm{ROUGE5F1}(\tilde y_S,y_T)$。按 $q$ 降序访问，无正确教师库的题放尾部，不丢题；真正训练 rollout 重新采样。动态版只重新评分尚未访问题目、教师库不变。底层使用普通采样 token OPD 裁剪目标，完全不修改 token 权重（方法节）。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

结尾与设置明确需要可验证的正确教师库；动态重排增加学生评分成本。分数只分开粗可靠性层，不是校准的 $R$。题库/教师改变需重新建库，部分子基准存在下降，尺度与组合扩展主要为单 seed。

#### 我的分析：优点

严格分开排序 rollout 与训练 rollout，避免把离线数据复用误称在线。先用昂贵 oracle 检验排序方向，再检验廉价代理，且公开保留全部题池和额外生成成本，研究链条清楚。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

ROUGE 依赖文字风格、长度和最终答案重叠，不等于可恢复性；分箱单调仍允许大量逐题误排。每题16教师样本会显著提高前处理成本，不能把比 oracle 便宜80倍当比普通 OPD 更省。题目全覆盖的一轮实验支持顺序效应，但若预算提前结束则同时改变实际训练题目分布，应另分离选择与排序。

### 4. Reference-Free Post-Training of Open Large Language Models for Multilingual Machine Translation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10812
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.10812
- Authors: Chris Han, Pengzhi Gao, Pei Fu, Jian Luan
- 作者单位：小米（Xiaomi Inc., Beijing）。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-11
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-12
- Categories: cs.CL, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> We study reference-free post-training for multilingual machine translation with open large language models. Starting from the supervised-finetuned MiLMMT-46-v0.1 models, we apply Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with a reward that averages two reference-free quality estimation models and is gated by language identification. We then linearly interpolate the supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and reinforcement learning (RL) model checkpoints to obtain MiLMMT-46-v1.0. Across 46 languages, the resulting models consistently improve translation quality over their SFT counterparts, outperform strong recent open baselines, including Seed-X, HY-MT2, and TranslateGemma, and achieve leading reference-free scores against evaluated proprietary systems such as Google Translate, Gemini 3 Pro, and GPT-5. We further investigate on-policy distillation (OPD) and find that it reaches, but does not surpass, the quality frontier achieved by RL with checkpoint interpolation. We release the models and code to facilitate future research.

#### 摘要中文翻译

本文研究开放模型的无参考多语翻译后训练。在已 SFT 的 MiLMMT-46-v0.1 上，用两个无参考质量估计器平均分加语言识别门控进行 GRPO，再线性插值 SFT 与 RL checkpoint，得到 v1.0。1B、4B、12B 和 46 种语言实验显示，学习型翻译质量指标普遍优于 SFT 起点，与近期开放及商业系统具有竞争力。作者进一步研究 12B→1B/4B 的在线蒸馏：它接近 RL+插值形成的质量—词面一致性前沿，但没有系统超过该前沿。模型与代码公开。

#### 研究背景

平行参考翻译分布不均，源语言文本较易获得。QE 可以只看源文和候选给奖励，但会被错误语言或指标偏好利用，因此论文结合语言门控和 checkpoint 回拉。这里无参考只指后训练不使用目标译文，起点本身已接受平行语料 SFT。

#### Method

奖励在 OpenLID-v3 识别目标语言正确时为 $(s_{XCOMET}+s_{COMETKiwi})/2$，否则为 0，两 QE 均为 10B。将原 SFT 数据丢弃参考后得到 263982 条、192 翻译方向，再按八样本平均分 $0.30<\mu<0.95$、标准差不低于 0.05 筛选，保留 31572 条，30572 训练、1000 验证。

GRPO 后采用 $\theta_\alpha=\alpha\theta_{SFT}+(1-\alpha)\theta_{RL}$，三规模统一取 $\alpha=0.5$。OPD 以 v1.0 的 12B 固定教师，在学生采样 token 上使用负 log-ratio 奖励；纯 OPD 每题一条，RL+OPD 每题八条并加 QE 奖励，扫描蒸馏系数。另有 top-128 前向 KL 的 GKD 对照（第 2、5 节、附录 C/E）。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未单列 limitations。作者明确承认 RL 降低 spBLEU、插值与 OPD 均只在既有前沿上取舍；OPD 对小学生不总能匹配 v1.0。不同基线的语言覆盖不一致，主表按共同子集分块；结尾将更稳健、人类对齐的奖励及多模态翻译列为后续方向。

#### 我的分析：优点

语言识别门控处理明确的错误语言问题，插值简单且无需新增训练。对 OPD 没有超越基线的结果如实报告，附录提供全部语言方向及蒸馏系数矩阵，适合研究“蒸馏能否代替每规模单独 RL”。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

训练奖励和主要无参考评估复用 XCOMET/COMETKiwi，因此高分部分可能反映指标适配；参考 XCOMET 仍属相近学习型评价，需要独立人工翻译评审确认真实增益。spBLEU 下降可能是合理改写，也可能是遗漏/偏离，不能仅凭单参考局限排除后者。筛选仅保留约 12% 数据，低资源方向覆盖需按附录分布检查；无完整成本对照，不能直接称 OPD 更省总计算。
