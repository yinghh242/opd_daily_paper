### 1. When Privileged Guidance Misaligns: State-Matched Routing and Contextualized Self-Distillation for Multi-Turn Agents

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05219
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.05219
- Authors: Junzhuo Liu, Weiwei Li, Jun Ling, Peng Wang
- 作者单位：电子科技大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-05
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-05
- Categories: cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Privileged on-policy distillation provides dense supervision for multi-turn agents by allowing a synchronized teacher to re-score the student's response at every turn with access to training-only references, such as successful trajectories. In interactive environments, however, the student's preceding actions continually change the execution state. As the student takes different actions or completes subgoals in a different order, its rollout may reach states not covered by the reference, making the reference an unreliable source of guidance for the state actually reached. Applying privileged distillation indiscriminately therefore creates state--reference mismatch. This mismatch motivates a central objective: providing privileged reference guidance that remains compatible with the student's current execution state. We introduce State-Matched Routing and Contextualized Self-Distillation (SMRC-SD), which explicitly determines when and how a privileged trajectory should guide an on-policy student. At each turn, SMRC-SD verifies whether the student's current execution state matches a supported state along the reference trajectory. Distillation is applied only at matched states, filtering out turns for which the reference lacks locally compatible guidance. For each matched state, SMRC-SD further constructs state-conditioned teacher context from the successful trajectory, grounding supervision in the state actually reached. Across ALFWorld and WebShop, SMRC-SD consistently outperforms unconditional successful full-path distillation. With Qwen3-1.7B, it improves task success from $0.746$ to $0.865$ on ALFWorld and from $0.574$ to $0.693$ on WebShop. Controlled routing and context ablations support both selecting locally supported turns and constructing state-compatible teacher context as contributors to these gains. Code is available at https://github.com/liujunzhuo/SMRC-SD.

#### 摘要中文翻译

多轮智能体的特权在线蒸馏可用成功轨迹重评分学生响应，但学生动作会不断改变执行状态；同任务成功路径在学生到达的状态上可能已无适用续行动作。SMRC-SD 将这种 state–reference mismatch 作为监督可靠性问题：先检查当前状态是否满足参考路径某个位置的条件，只在匹配位置蒸馏；再把完整路径、当前状态摘要与可执行下一动作组合成教师上下文。ALFWorld 和 WebShop 上优于无条件 FullPath-SD，Qwen3-1.7B 成功率分别从 0.746→0.865、0.574→0.693。路由与上下文消融支持二者的贡献。

#### 研究背景

参考“整题能成功”不意味着“当前位置应照做”。例如学生位于另一产品详情页，参考的购买动作可能不适用，需要先返回搜索。这里解决的是教师输入在执行状态上的有效性，发生在 token 分歧打分之前。

#### Method

训练按稳定任务 ID 查一个参考。ALFWorld 从已执行动作、当前观测和合法动作重建位置、库存、目标物体位置/属性；参考状态由动作前缀符号重建。要求关键字段一致或满足必要进度，再检查候选动作当前可执行；选择最新匹配位置。WebShop 用页面、目标 ASIN、已选选项集合匹配，允许兼容选项换序。

匹配时教师看完整参考+当前状态一句摘要+候选，未匹配只做 GRPO。损失为

$$
L=L_{GRPO}+0.01\frac{\sum_t w_t\sum_i m_{ti}\rho_{ti}(e^{-\delta_{ti}}-1+\delta_{ti})}{\sum_{t,i}m^{resp}_{ti}},
$$

$w_t$ 为匹配门，$\delta$ 是学生减特权教师的采样 log-prob，$\rho$ 校正当前与 rollout 策略。分母包含全部原响应，所以少选步骤同时降低总蒸馏强度。推理无参考、matcher 或摘要（主方法及附录 A–D）。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

附录 J 明确“matched”是工程 matcher 标签，不是动作真值；100% 选中续行动作回放成功不测未匹配项召回。固定状态干预测教师偏好，不替代训练结果。适配器依赖手写环境规则和同任务成功参考，并非通用语义状态匹配器。

#### 我的分析：优点

先验证参考适用性，再决定是否蒸馏，比仅按 logits 置信度筛选更符合可执行计划语义。同数随机路由、状态字段组合、实际环境回放提供较完整机制证据；参考隔离和固定 checkpoint 口径清楚。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

主策略比较固定单 seed，WebShop 3B 的微小收益不能当稳定优势。参考带目标 ASIN 等 oracle 信息且每任务都有路径，是较强训练条件；推广到没有标准状态转移和合法动作列表的真实网页需要另建适配器。路由同时减少全局蒸馏系数，虽有同数随机控制，仍需匹配实际 token 权重/梯度预算才能完全分离效应。

### 2. STEP-OPD: Rethinking Output Targets and Internal Dynamics in On-Policy Distillation for Diffusion Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04887
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.04887
- Authors: Qingyan Wei, Guangzhao Li, Xiaobing Tu, Yinggui Wang, Xiantao Zhang, Jinkui Ren, Xiaohong Liu, Linfeng Zhang
- 作者单位：上海交通大学；Alibaba Group；Shanghai Innovation Institute。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-05
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-05
- Categories: cs.CV

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) has become an effective approach for consolidating multiple task-specialized image generation models into a single student. However, existing OPD methods optimize the student mainly to match the teacher's output velocity, making the teacher the upper limit of the optimization objective. While output-level supervision alone leaves the student's blockwise representation evolution underconstrained, which weakens the transfer of capabilities that must be progressively developed across layers. We propose STEP-OPD, an on-policy distillation framework for image generation that extends the student's learning target beyond the teacher and introduces explicit constraints on its internal representation evolution. Instead of treating the teacher as the final target, we use the velocity difference between each task-specific teacher and the shared base model as a direction for further learning and add a scaled version of this difference to the teacher velocity. In addition, we align the direction and magnitude of representation changes between the student and teacher, enabling the student to learn how representations are progressively transformed across network blocks. Experiments on compositional alignment, text rendering, and human preference show that our method consistently improves Standard OPD methods. In particular, it increases the GenEval score of DiffusionOPD from 0.927 to 0.961, while also improving OCR and all preference-based metrics. The resulting unified student surpasses the corresponding single-task teachers across all three capability groups, showing that output extrapolation enables beyond-teacher learning. And representation change alignment provides complementary guidance for the student's internal transformations.

#### 摘要中文翻译

图像生成 OPD 能汇总多个领域教师，但匹配输出速度把优化目标限制在教师，且没有充分约束网络内部表征演化。STEP-OPD 将教师相对共享基座的速度差外推为目标，并匹配师生相邻网络块之间表征变化的方向与幅度。实验覆盖组合对齐、文字生成和偏好；在 DiffusionOPD 上将 GenEval 从0.927提高到0.961，并提高 OCR 和偏好指标，整合学生在报告的三组能力上超过相应专用教师。

#### 研究背景

多个网络层的误差可以在最终速度预测中抵消，因此输出拟合不必然传递内部处理方式。本文同时改变输出目标和增加中间监督，应用于共享 SD3.5-Medium 架构的专家整合。

#### Method

冻结基座 B 与各任务教师 T，在同一学生状态构造 $\tilde v_k=v_{T_k}+\alpha_k(v_{T_k}-v_B)$；外推强度早期线性预热，GenEval/OCR/偏好最终取0.01/0.06/0.20。保留底层 OPD 状态查询策略，可嵌入全轨迹 DiffusionOPD 或抽样单状态 DanceOPD。

内部 RCA 对每对相邻块计算 $\Delta h=h^{l_1}-h^{l_0}$，匹配 token 级 RMS 归一化后的方向以及整体 RMS 的对数幅度。总损失 $L=L_{out}+0.005(L_{dir}+0.1L_{mag})$；24块由12个不重叠相邻对覆盖。内部目标仍来自教师、不作外推，rollout状态停止梯度（Method）。

#### 我的分析：优点

外推与表征变化监督互补，单组件消融及两个OPD底层可辨别方法来源。RCA对相邻层变化而非绝对表征匹配，与共享基座模型的内部结构自然对应。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

### 3. SPOT: Sparse Probing and Outcome Calibration for On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04419
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.04419
- Authors: Zikun Qu, Min Zhang, Mingze Kong, Zhiwei Shang, Yikun Ban, Shuang Qiu, Zhongxiang Dai
- 作者单位：香港中文大学（深圳）；华东师范大学；美团 LongCat；北京航空航天大学；香港城市大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-05
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-05
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) provides dense teacher supervision on student-generated trajectories, but standard reverse-KL training can assign insufficient probability to other plausible continuations. Teacher entropy alone does not reveal whether uncertainty is concentrated among a few plausible next tokens or dispersed over a long probability tail, nor whether the student already represents those candidates well. Moreover, local teacher probabilities may not predict downstream success. We introduce Sparse Probing and Outcome-calibrated Targets OPD (SPOT), which addresses two coupled decisions, where to probe and what to distill, through an acquisition--exploration--exploitation procedure. During acquisition, a position-level score combines normalized teacher entropy, the probability mass captured by a small top-$k$ candidate set, and student--teacher mismatch to allocate a limited probing budget. During exploration, SPOT evaluates teacher-proposed candidates through verifier-scored student continuations. During exploitation, these outcomes produce a closed-form, KL-regularized target that favors candidates with better downstream outcomes while remaining anchored to the teacher distribution. Extensive experiments across multiple student models and reasoning benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of SPOT in improving reasoning performance while balancing solution quality and coverage.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD 在学生轨迹上提供密集教师监督，但反向 KL 容易遗漏其他合理续写。教师熵无法区分紧凑候选集的不确定性与长尾噪声，也不知道学生是否已经掌握这些候选；局部概率更不必然代表最终成功。SPOT 将“在哪探测”与“蒸馏什么”分开：以归一化教师熵、top-k 质量和师生差异选择少量位置，让学生从教师候选继续 rollout 并用验证器评分，再构造教师锚定的 KL 正则目标。实验显示多次采样覆盖率更稳定提高，同时保留有竞争力的单次平均正确率。

#### 研究背景

教师只是局部分支提议者，真正应学习的是当前学生接得住且能完成的分支。额外续写昂贵，因此先用低成本分数分配探测预算。

#### Method

选位分数 $s_t=\bar H_T C_tG_t$：C 是教师 top-16 概率质量，G 平均结合学生对此集合缺失的质量与集合内 JSD。每条轨迹选 2 个有效位置，各测试教师 top-4 token，每候选仅续写一次；全失败位置不加分支损失。

有效位置目标为 $q_t(v)\propto p_T^{top4}(v)\exp(\hat V_t(v))$，以 0.1 权重的交叉熵辅助全轨迹 OPD。其最优性针对已估计分支价值，不能直接保证真实成功概率增加；理论附录对此作了区分。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未单列 Limitations。附录说明分支价值只是一条续写的二元估计；跨家族实验不能推出架构不变性，域外结果也非全面领先。AlpacaEval 长度控制后 EOPD 优于 SPOT。

#### 我的分析：优点

选位与分支目标分开且均有消融；不是把教师置信当奖励，而实际测学生后续可完成性。Avg@8 与 Pass@8 并列，突出收益主要来自覆盖率。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

每候选一条续写噪声较大，二元指数倾斜可能强化偶然成功。主结果多付最多八条后缀采样，未给相同总 token/墙钟预算下优势；相对 EOPD 的 Avg@8 增量仅 0.29–0.68 点，需要多种子确认。GRPO 的非 thinking 模板也与蒸馏组不同，应限制跨目标比较。

### 4. Poly-OPD: Heterogeneous Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Capability-Selectable Flow Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04349
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.04349
- Authors: Siming Fu, Haojun Xu, Ruizhe He, Zheming Fu, Hualiang Wang, Jie Huang, Xiaoxiao Ma, Mingchen Zhong, Weihu Huang, Xiaoxuan He, Linjiang Huang, Si Liu
- 作者单位：京东 Joy Future Academy；浙江大学；北京航空航天大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-08-05
- arXiv updated: 2026-08-05
- Categories: cs.CV

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Leading open text-to-image models often carry complementary strengths: one may lead on preference-aligned aesthetics while another follows compositional instructions more faithfully. However, differences in their autoencoders and noise schedules make it difficult to transfer these strengths across models. In this paper, we present Poly-OPD, a framework that can consolidate complementary strengths of heterogeneous teachers into a single compact flow-matching student. To bridge the incompatible latent spaces of different teachers, Poly-OPD performs on-policy distillation through a pixel bridge. Each student-generated image is re-encoded by a selected teacher's encoder and refined from a noise level matched by magnitude under the teacher's noise schedule. The resulting target is further matched to the student in frozen DINOv2 space, enabling supervision across incompatible latent spaces. To retain complementary capabilities without cross-teacher interference, Poly-OPD uses a gradient compatibility diagnostic to organize its adapters: attention LoRA modules are shared across teachers, whereas feed-forward adapters remain teacher-specific. During distillation, a gap-aware curriculum devotes more training to compositional categories where the student still falls short of the teacher. As each gap narrows, training shifts toward categories with larger remaining gaps. By distilling FLUX.1-dev and Z-Image into a 2.5B SD3.5-Medium student, Poly-OPD improves GenEval from 67.3 to 73.3, surpassing both larger teachers, and raises DrawBench HPSv3 from 9.34 to 11.35, consolidating both strengths within a switchable model.

#### 摘要中文翻译

开源文生图教师具有互补优势，但不同 VAE 与噪声日程阻碍直接蒸馏。Poly-OPD 将异构教师能力合并到紧凑 flow 学生：把学生图像经像素空间重新编码到教师 latent，在噪声幅度匹配的位置精炼，再于冻结 DINOv2 空间对齐。梯度兼容性诊断决定共享 attention LoRA、隔离各教师 FFN adapter；差距感知课程把训练分给尚未追上教师的构图类别。FLUX.1-dev 与 Z-Image 蒸馏至 2.5B SD3.5-Medium 后，GenEval 从 67.3 到 73.3，DrawBench HPSv3 从 9.34 到 11.35，能力可通过 adapter 切换。

#### 研究背景

异构模型不能直接比较 latent 或速度场；多能力完全共享更新又可能冲突。本文用像素桥解决坐标接口，并通过模块隔离管理干扰。

#### Method

先用教师图像在学生坐标做 flow-matching 热身。随后学生无梯度生成图像，教师重新编码、加噪并精炼；学生从缓存的相近噪声幅度状态重跑尾段，最小化 $1-\cos(f(x_S),\operatorname{sg}f(x_T))$，f 为 DINOv2 CLS。每步只更新共享 attention 与当前能力 FFN LoRA。

组合类别差距 $g_a\leftarrow0.8g_a+0.2\max(s_a^T-s_a^S,0)$，再按 $\operatorname{softmax}(g/0.1)$ 采样；每类独立 32 道探针、50 步刷新。推理选择偏好或构图 adapter，不是每次同时启用所有教师能力（第 3 节）。

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05131

- Authors: Aniri, Jinhe Bi, Peng Liao, Zengjie Jin, Volker Tresp, Fei Shen, Yunpu Ma, Tat-Seng Chua
- 作者单位：慕尼黑大学（Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich）；慕尼黑机器学习中心（MCML）；新加坡国立大学；中山大学。

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

#### Abstract 原文

> On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) has become a standard post-training approach for improving visual reasoning in multimodal large language models (MLLMs). #### Method

$$
L=\mathbb E\frac1{\sum_t r_t}\sum_t r_t[\delta_t]_+\mathrm{JSD}(q_t^+,p_{\theta,t}).
$$

分母为所有有效响应 token 数，不按保留 token 或权重和归一化；因此同时改变选择与总损失尺度。分布在学生 top-100 加一个词表尾部概率桶上计算，负教师只控制权重；EMA 每步混入学生 5%。参考答案用于匹配基线协议，OPD-V 正负教师公式自身只条件化图像、问题与前缀。推理仅保留原图学生（第 3 节、附录 B/F）。

### 7. Agentic Reinforcement Learning with Observation-Calibrated Self-Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04788
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.04788
- Authors: Yi Yang, Cong Qin, Xiaodan Liu, Chishui Chen, Qing Dong, Yan Zhang, Cao Liu, Zhao Yang, Lu Pan, Jiaye Lin, Yi Feng
- 作者单位：美团 LongCat Interaction；南京大学；北京大学；麦克马斯特大学；复旦大学；华东师范大学。

- arXiv updated: 2026-08-05
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Large language model agents are commonly trained through reinforcement learning with sparse trajectory-level rewards, which offer limited guidance on how strongly individual tokens should be updated. On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) addresses this by re-scoring generated tokens under a privileged replay view to obtain dense, token-level supervision. However, we identify a confounding issue: the resulting support may reflect both the privileged information contained in the replay view and score shifts induced by the replay scaffold, making it difficult to attribute the support specifically to that information. This issue is especially pronounced when future environment observations serve as privileged information, since replaying them requires reconstructing an extended scaffold that itself perturbs token scores. To resolve this confounding, we propose Observation-Calibrated Self-Distillation (OCSD), which contrasts two structurally matched replay views, Full and Observation-Ablated, differing only in whether the actual future observation is present, to derive an observation residual that discounts score changes shared by the replay scaffold. OCSD then applies this residual to modulate token-level GRPO updates at high-uncertainty steps, while preserving the trajectory-level update direction. Experiments on ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search-QA across three Qwen3 model scales show that OCSD consistently outperforms strong baselines. Diagnostic analyses further confirm that the calibrated residual aligns better with local environment feedback. Our code is publicly available at https://github.com/yiy1x/OCSD.

#### 摘要中文翻译

智能体 RL 的稀疏最终奖励难以区分 token 更新强度，在线自蒸馏常通过带未来证据的 replay 重新评分获得稠密监督。但分数变化既可能来自真实未来观测，也可能只是 replay 框架、未来动作结构等额外上下文。OCSD 构造 Full 和 Observation-Ablated 两个结构匹配视图，仅切换实际未来观测，二者差形成观测残差；在高不确定交互步骤上用它调制 token GRPO 更新，同时保留轨迹奖励决定的方向。三个智能体基准、三种 Qwen3 规模实验显示整体提升，诊断中残差也更符合局部环境反馈。

#### 研究背景

“加入环境反馈后 token 概率提高”不必然表示反馈支持该动作，格式或未来动作模板本身也会改变分数。把这些共享变化扣除，比直接使用 Full 对原始学生的概率差更接近观测条件下的增量信息。

#### Method

每条轨迹按平均 token NLL 选最高 20% 交互步骤。Full 包含后两次观测和中间动作的去具体参数模板；Ablated 保留字段顺序与动作模板，仅把观测替换为固定缺省文本。相同旧策略、相同学生前缀评分，$e=\log\pi_F(y_t)-\log\pi_A(y_t)$，$q=\tanh(e/2)$。选中 token 使用

$$
A^{OCSD}_{i,k,t}=A_i\left[1+\beta\operatorname{sgn}(A_i)q_{i,k,t}\right],\qquad \beta=0.5.
$$

未选中保持 $A_i$。正残差加强正轨迹或减轻负轨迹惩罚，负残差相反；乘数在 [0.5,1.5]，不会翻转标量优势。没有独立 imitation 损失，也不生成 hindsight 动作（第 3 节、附录 A）。
