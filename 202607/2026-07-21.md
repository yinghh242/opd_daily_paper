### 1. REGEN: Replay-recycling for Expert-to-Generalist distillation with Offline Reinforcement Learning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19450
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19450
- Authors: Yunjie Chen, Xiaoxin Chen, Fang Wang
- 作者单位：vivo AI Lab；深圳青燕科技；中山大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-21
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-21
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Large-scale online reinforcement learning (RL) is the predominant means of eliciting advanced abilities including long-term reasoning and agentic tool use in large language models (LLMs). However, continuing to scale it across vast task domains of interest remains challenging in both computational infrastructure and cost, especially when considering RL as merely a one-off learning stage. Recently, a widely used technique for distilling knowledge across various domains and training stages, multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD), helps to decouple the RL stage, saving costs, while maintaining generality across vast domains. Nonetheless, similar to online RL, MOPD requires coupled inference and backward passes, which continues to limit its scalability and computational efficiency. To address these challenges, we propose REGEN: Replay-recycling for Expert-to-Generalist Distillation with Offline RL. Instead of distilling from multiple teacher models, REGEN trains a generalist by simply recycling the replay memory -- the free by-product of the teachers' specialized RL training -- and employing offline RL algorithms. REGEN completely decouples the rollout sampling from the backward training process and thus greatly reduces the training cost. Across mathematical reasoning, code generation, and instruction following, REGEN matches the accuracy of MOPD at substantially lower cost. It potentially turns online RL into a data synthesis process instead of a one-off learning stage, and can potentially be extended to large-scale post-training without requiring heavy computational load.

#### 摘要中文翻译

多领域在线 RL 和多教师在策略蒸馏仍需耦合推理、评价与反传，限制计算效率。REGEN 不再请求最终教师监督，而是回收专家 RL 训练中产生的历史轨迹，用离线 RL 训练通才。它把 rollout 采样与学生更新完全分离，复用原训练副产物。在数学、代码和指令遵循中，准确率与 MOPD 接近，所报学生训练成本更低。该结果将既有 RL 过程视为可再次利用的数据生产流程。

#### 研究背景

最终专家通常丢失训练早期多样轨迹；若保存所有阶段的正负样本和行为概率，通才可利用这些信息，而无需重新部署多个教师。

#### Method

记录每条轨迹的提示、回答、奖励及行为策略 NLL，恢复 $\mu(y\mid x)$。同题仅保留至少有一正一负的组，按较小类数量平衡下采样，再在下采样组内归一化奖励为 $\hat A$。

正样本直接以 $\hat A$ 加权平均 token NLL；负样本再乘截断重要性比 $\min(\pi_\theta(y\mid x)/\mu(y\mid x),1)$，对权重 stop-gradient。正负分界是每域奖励 1。这个不对称离线目标既保留成功模仿，也避免极低概率历史失败主导更新（式 5–9）。

### 2. OPD-IAD: From Language Judgment to Industrial Anomaly Detection via On-Policy Self-Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18850
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.18850
- Authors: Shuimu Chen, Jing Jin, Nan Su, Hongbo Xu, Zebang Cheng, Wenming Yang, Fei Ma, Guijin Wang
- 作者单位：清华大学；广东省人工智能与数字经济实验室（深圳）；深圳大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-21

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Large vision-language models (LVLMs) have recently shown strong potential for industrial anomaly detection (IAD) by providing image-level anomaly judgments and interpretable defect reasoning. However, current LVLM-based IAD methods still struggle to produce precise pixel-level anomaly maps from generated language judgments. We aim to achieve precise pixel-level localization while using language as guidance rather than letting it dominate the visual response. Specifically, we propose \textbf{OPD-IAD}, an evidence-privileged dense on-policy self-distillation framework for LVLM-based IAD. OPD-IAD distills privileged defect evidence onto the model's own on-policy judgment trajectory, enabling the final generated judgment to be learned under dense supervision rather than treated only as a textual answer. The resulting judgment serves as a semantic condition for dense anomaly perception. To turn this condition into dense visual evidence, we introduce \textbf{Language-guided Visual Anchoring}, which uses a judgment reforward to re-encode the image and question under the final-judgment condition into semantic anchors and contrasts them with dense visual features through a contrastive heatmap head to generate anomaly maps. The language judgment therefore provides compact semantic guidance, while dense visual features remain the basis for pixel-level scoring, allowing language to guide anomaly localization without letting language quality directly dictate the pixel-level response. Extensive experiments show that OPD-IAD achieves the best overall performance among LVLM-based IAD methods, leading on most image-level, pixel-level, and QA metrics.

#### 摘要中文翻译

大型视觉语言模型可提供工业异常判断及解释，但将语言判断转成精确像素定位仍困难。MOPDA 用混合轨迹监督结合学生在策略回答与证据条件化教师回答，在共享 token 蒸馏目标下学习判断。定位分支仅将最终判断作为紧凑语义条件，构造图像特定的正常、异常锚点，再与密集视觉特征对比生成热图。五个跨数据集零样本基准中，方法在大多数检测、定位和语言判断指标上优于所测 LVLM，并与 CLIP 方法竞争；消融支持混合轨迹与最终判断条件化。

#### 研究背景

日报原收录题名为 OPD-IAD，本次读取的是官方 **2026-09-28 修订的 v2：MOPDA**，方法已加入混合教师轨迹；v1 发表于 2026-07-21。[官方版本记录](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18850v2)。核心问题是语言定位容易随推理文本变化，而完全独立的视觉头又不能利用语言证据。

#### Method

每批以 0.8 概率采学生轨迹，0.2 概率采 EMA 教师看参考证据的轨迹；两支都在相同前缀上做 JSD，教师生成回答须格式有效且最终判断与源标签一致。教师 EMA 衰减 0.999。最终二元判断重前向，与正常/异常 token 形成锚点，像素分数为 $s_p=\tau(\langle\bar v_p,\bar z_a\rangle-\langle\bar v_p,\bar z_n\rangle)$。

总损失包含蒸馏、仅作用于学生生成支的 0.1 倍结果奖励，以及图像 BCE、像素 BCE/Focal/Dice 和锚点分离；图像异常分数取热图最高 1% 的均值（第 3 节、附录 A）。

### 3. ISO: An RLVR-Native Optimization Stack

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19331
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19331
- Authors: Hanqing Zhu, Wenyan Cong, Zhizhou Sha, Sagnik Mukherjee, Xinyuan Song, David González-Martínez, Xiaoxia Wu, Yuandong Tian, Shiwei Liu, David Z. Pan, Zhangyang "Atlas" Wang
- 作者单位：得克萨斯大学奥斯汀分校；UIUC；埃默里大学；Together AI；Recursive Superintelligence Inc；ELLIS Institute Tübingen。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-21
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-21
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) is rapidly advancing the reasoning capabilities of language models, yet the optimization layer that converts reward feedback into weight-space updates remains poorly understood. Building on our prior analysis (Zhu et al., 2025), we study this missing layer through the singular structure of model weights and identify spectral inheritance: RLVR can reuse the base model's weight spectra while acquiring new behavior through changes in the associated input and output singular frames. We operationalize spectral inheritance as Isospectral Optimization (ISO), an RLVR-native, fixed-spectrum optimization framework with complementary offline and online instantiations. Offline, ISO-Merger combines the frame changes of shared-base specialists into a single fixed-spectrum model, requiring no post-merge data, rollouts, gradient updates, or on-policy distillation (OPD). It recovers complementary specialist capabilities and achieves the strongest aggregate performance among the compared data-free merging methods. Online, ISO-Optimizer applies a chosen base optimizer, including AdamW and Muon, to the frame variables while keeping the base spectra fixed. Across reasoning and coding tasks ranging from 1.5B to 8B parameters, ISO-Optimizer improves accuracy in the reported runs and reaches matched scores with substantially fewer training steps. On Qwen3-8B-Base, AdamW reaches an aggregate accuracy of 0.495 after 270 training steps. ISO-AdamW reaches the same accuracy after only 100 training steps and improves further to 0.509 after 210 training steps. Together, ISO offers a concrete answer to RLVR's missing optimization layer: rather than inheriting pre-training optimization wholesale, design post-training around the structure of reward-driven adaptation: inherit the spectrum, optimize the frames.

#### 摘要中文翻译

本文研究 RLVR 如何将奖励反馈转为权重更新，提出谱继承：模型可沿用基础权重的奇异值，通过改变左右奇异向量框架获取行为。只在原子空间内重混合或固定其中一侧，无法充分重建已学变化。ISO 将这一现象实现为固定谱优化体系：离线 ISO-Merger 合并同基座专家，无需合并后数据、rollout、梯度或 OPD；在线 ISO-Optimizer 将 AdamW、Muon 应用于框架变量并保持谱固定。在 1.5B–8B 的推理、代码实验中改善所报精度与步数效率；8B 达到 AdamW 270 步的 0.495 均分仅需 100 步，并在 210 步达 0.509。

#### 研究背景

RL 权重奇异值变化小可能仅来自高维几何，作者先作维数归一化，再通过恢复原谱和全程固定谱训练验证其功能可复用性，避免把相关现象直接当优化规律。

#### Method

以 $W=U\Sigma_0V^\top$ 参数化每个矩阵，$U,V$ 列正交，固定基础谱。在线梯度为 $G_U=G_WV\Sigma_0$、$G_V=G_W^\top U\Sigma_0$；AdamW/Muon 分别更新两因子，再用 polar retraction 恢复正交。不是先更新 W 再投影。

离线合并先对齐奇异对符号，将专家因子变化投影到共同基座切空间，默认保留前 90% 模式；用诱导权重变化的 Gram 矩阵求带 ridge、截断的保留系数，再聚合、重新投影和 polar 重建。该流程严格保持谱，但不保证每专家行为无损（第 4 节、附录 E/F）。

### 4. Contrastive On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19046
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19046
- Authors: Jiacheng Ruan, Jun Tang, Wenzhen Yuan, Ting Liu, Shuai Bai, Dayiheng Liu, Zhibo Yang, Yuzhuo Fu
- 作者单位：上海交通大学；Alibaba Group（Qwen Team）。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-21
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-21
- Categories: cs.CV

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy Distillation (OPD) supervises a student model on trajectories sampled from its own policy by minimizing the divergence between the output distributions of the teacher and student at each token position, thereby providing dense token-level supervision. Although existing OPD methods have demonstrated strong performance in improving the reasoning ability of student models, their objectives fundamentally rely on token-level distribution matching. Consequently, they lack an explicit signal for comparing a token's relative compatibility across reasoning modes and thus do not directly model preferences between these modes. To address this limitation, we propose COPD, a contrastive OPD framework. Specifically, for each token generated by the student model, a frozen teacher model scores the same student state under two contrasting instructions that elicit light and heavy reasoning. The difference between the resulting log probabilities serves as a token-level advantage signal to guide the OPD update. Rather than merely imitating a single teacher distribution, COPD directly encourages the student model to learn more concise and efficient reasoning strategies. We conduct experiments on nine multimodal benchmarks covering both reasoning and understanding tasks. The results show that COPD substantially reduces reasoning length without compromising model performance and consistently improves efficiency across different tasks and model scales. Furthermore, the contrastive formulation can be seamlessly integrated into the On-policy Self-distillation (OPSD) framework, where self-contrastive supervision is constructed without an additional teacher model, thereby enabling the model to distill itself toward lightweight reasoning.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD 在学生自采样轨迹上最小化师生分布差异，提供密集监督，但也可能继承未经筛选的冗长思维链。COPD 让冻结教师在低推理投入与高推理投入两种提示下评价相同学生 token，以两者对数概率差作为逐 token 优势，引导学生形成简洁策略。九个多模态基准显示，该方法能显著压缩输出并总体保持或改善准确率。相同机制也可使用学生初始快照进行自蒸馏，无需外部教师。

#### 研究背景

直接训练短答案依赖离线示例，长度惩罚 RL 又难区分必要推理与冗余。本文固定图像、问题、学生前缀和 token，只改变教师推理投入指令，用相对偏好提供压缩方向。

#### Method

学生采样 $y\sim\pi_{\theta_{\mathrm{old}}}$；教师分别计算轻思考 LT 与重思考 HT 条件下的概率，令 $A_t=\log\pi_T(y_t\mid x^{LT},y_{<t})-\log\pi_T(y_t\mid x^{HT},y_{<t})$。优势截断至 [-10,10] 并停止梯度，经 PPO 概率比裁剪更新学生（式 3—5）。不加外部任务奖励、KL 惩罚或显式长度奖励；每条轨迹需要两个教师评分前向。

COPSD 将教师替换为冻结初始学生。LT/HT 提示的作用不是提供答案，而是诱导相反的输出投入偏好；方法奖励 LT 相对更支持的 token，不能据此断言每个被压低的 token 都是无用推理。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

#### 我的分析：优点

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

Acc@1K 是精度与长度的比值，不是标准固定 token 预算下的成功率；很短且略低精度输出也可能获得高分。主文称三个独立运行，附录 A.3 实际写三次评测，不能当作三个训练种子。强 LT 指令包含不复查和容许近似，偏好信号没有正确性保证，当前总体收益依赖所测任务的冗余程度。

### 5. AdaFlash: Adaptive Speculative Decoding via On-Policy Distilled Diffusion Drafters

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19223
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19223
- Authors: Yu-Yang Qian, Hao-Cong Wu, Chen Chen, Jiacheng Sun, Zhenhua Dong, Peng Zhao, Zhi-Hua Zhou
- 作者单位：南京大学计算机软件新技术国家重点实验室、人工智能学院；华为基础模型部门。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-21
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-21
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Speculative decoding, in which a lightweight draft model first generates a draft sequence that is then verified in parallel by the target model, has become a prevalent paradigm for accelerating large language model inference. Recent work such as DFlash further boosts drafting efficiency by leveraging diffusion drafters, whose parallel denoising mechanism enables draft generation in a single forward pass. In this work, we uncover a central pitfall of diffusion drafters: bidirectional attention is a double-edged sword. On one hand, it endows the model with parallel generation and global contextual modeling capabilities; on the other hand, this inherent global dependency introduces high variance at both the domain-level and the token-level: acceptance rates fluctuate substantially across different domains, and draft token quality also varies heterogeneously at different token positions. To tackle this issue, we propose AdaFlash framework, comprising two components: (i) an on-policy distillation (OPD) algorithm with reverse-KL divergence tailored for diffusion drafters, bringing stable convergence and effectively reducing domain-level variance; and (ii) an adaptive length head that dynamically adjusts the candidate sequence length on the fly, substantially lowering the verification cost of the target model and handling token-level variance. Experiments demonstrate that AdaFlash consistently improves speedup rate during deployment, with especially significant gains in high-concurrency scenarios, achieving up to approximately 66% higher throughput than previous state-of-the-art methods.

#### 摘要中文翻译

推测解码由轻量草稿生成候选，再由目标模型验证；扩散草稿可一次并行生成多个 token，但双向注意力和目标 KV 注入也伴随跨领域及位置间接受质量变化。AdaFlash 以两部分应对：面向扩散草稿的在线蒸馏持续适应部署分布，使用反向 KL、逐词表项裁剪及硬标签混合损失；自适应长度头预测可接受长度，减少无用验证。结合异步训练推理和变长调度，方法在多任务、多模型与并发设置中提高吞吐，相对某些既有方法最高获得约 66% 的平均吞吐提升。

#### 研究背景

接受长度提高不一定意味着服务吞吐提高：高并发时固定验证 15 个草稿 token，即使只接受少数也会挤占目标模型算力。论文把分布适应与验证预算分别交给草稿更新和长度预测，避免只优化离线接受率。

#### Method

DFlash 式草稿利用目标隐藏特征，一次前向生成候选。验证结果提供在线监督：$\mathcal L=\alpha\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CE}}+(1-\alpha)\mathcal L_{\mathrm{RKL,clip}}$，默认 $\alpha=0.8$，CE 标签为目标 top-1，反向 KL 中每个位置/词表项 $q(v)\log(q(v)/p(v))$ 上限 0.01。

独立 MLP 长度头经均值池化和 sigmoid 预测接受比例 $\hat a$，发送 $\hat k=\operatorname{clamp}(\lfloor\gamma k\hat a\rfloor,1,k)$ 个候选验证；接受反馈用 MSE 训练头，梯度不回传草稿。并发不高于 16 时关闭长度头。推理与训练分置 GPU，缓冲区每积累 128 样本更新两轮，再热加载草稿；服务端把不同验证长度打包调度（第 3 节、附录 B.1）。目标模型与标准接受/修正机制不改变，草稿训练不承担提高目标答案质量的职责。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未单列 limitations。文中承认在线适应比固定跨域权重多数场景更好、随机采样降低接受长度；自适应长度只适合较高并发。DSpark 的硬件调度被列为互补方案，当前比较只对其置信头进行离线匹配。

#### 我的分析：优点

把 token 接受与验证成本共同优化，并报告并发 1–128、长输出和 NPU 设置，比只报单请求峰值更实用。等平均验证长度消融支持动态分配价值；在线生成反馈可直接复用，无须人工标签。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

异步训练使用额外 GPU，报告的服务吞吐不等于等总 GPU 成本吞吐；需计训练、热加载及完整适应周期。双向注意力消融方差只从 1.115 降至 1.053，EAGLE 又同时改变架构和 KV 注入，因此不能严格将高方差唯一归因于“全局依赖”。长度头学习的真实接受长度受已截断验证范围限制，全文未详述截尾标签校正；无损性来自正确的推测验证实现，不能仅凭蒸馏目标宣称。

### 6. ABOPD: Antibody CDR Design via On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18835
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.18835
- Authors: Zhuo Yang, Jiaying He, Jiaqing Xie, Daolang Wang, Xipeng Qiu, Yuxin Wang, Tianfan Fu, Beilun Wang
- 作者单位：Shanghai Innovation Institute；浙江大学；复旦大学；上海人工智能实验室；厦门大学；东南大学；南京大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-21
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-21
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Antibodies are essential therapeutic molecules, and their complementarity-determining regions (CDRs) form the primary antigen-recognition interface. Recent protein generative models have demonstrated broad capabilities in biomolecular design, yet post-training strategies for downstream objectives remain limited. Standard denoising training operates on noisy states obtained by perturbing native structures, whereas recursive generation proceeds through model-generated intermediate states. For flexible antibody CDR loops such as CDR-H3, this mismatch can allow backbone deviations to accumulate along the denoising trajectory and compromise antigen-facing loop geometry. We introduce ABOPD, an antibody design framework based on on-policy distillation that leverages privileged native geometry during training to supervise states visited along the model's own denoising trajectories. With this fine-grained structural supervision, ABOPD substantially improves structural recovery on RAbD CDR-H3 generation, reducing RMSD by 0.42 Å (from 2.37 Å to 1.95 Å) and outperforming supervised fine-tuning and offline distillation controls, offering a path to higher-fidelity protein design.

#### 摘要中文翻译

抗体互补决定区 CDR 构成主要抗原识别界面。蛋白生成模型已有广泛能力，但针对下游设计的后训练有限。标准去噪训练使用原生结构加噪状态，递归生成却访问模型产生的中间状态；对灵活 CDR-H3 环，这种不匹配会积累骨架偏差。ABOPD 在训练时利用原生几何作为教师特权，对学生自身去噪轨迹上的状态提供细粒度结构监督。RAbD CDR-H3 的 RMSD 从 2.37 Å 降至 1.95 Å，超过 SFT 与离线蒸馏对照，为提高结构保真度提供途径。

#### 研究背景

准确重建抗体环不仅要求序列相似，还要求两端连接、环空间位置和抗原界面相容。本文针对坐标漂移，区分原生结构加噪状态与模型实际 rollout 状态，避免将所有蛋白生成质量压成单一序列恢复率。

#### Method

先用单 CDR、多 CDR、六 CDR 混合掩码训练 H-DiffAb。教师增加两个共 23.8K 参数的残差 MLP，读取原生 N、C-alpha、C、O、C-beta 几何及目标到复合体的距离，适配后冻结；学生推理时不读取这些信息（第 3 节）。

学生从原 100 步日程的 80 开始 rollout，缓存 80/70/60/50/40/30/20/10/5 状态。在相同状态上匹配教师坐标噪声预测。共享协方差高斯反向核的 KL 等于 $b_t^2\|\hat\epsilon_S-\hat\epsilon_T\|^2/(2\sigma_t^2)$；实现删除日程系数，对九个时刻等权回归。最终 $\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{denoise}}+0.6\mathcal L_{\mathrm{OPD,pos}}$，保留离线序列、坐标、姿态去噪锚定。rollout 停止梯度，因此是状态分布上的 on-policy 蒸馏，并非对整个轨迹分布求策略梯度。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

附录 D 明确指出几何保真与多样性可能存在权衡，侧链和界面改善并不统一；等权时刻采样未利用后期监督更有用的观测，时域机制目前局限于当前 DiffAb 的 CDR-H3。原文同时提醒 AAR 不能直接代表总体设计质量。

#### 我的分析：优点

教师几何收益、生成状态漂移、时间分辨的纠偏方向和最终结构指标形成较具体证据链；保留无 EMA、侧链退化与计算预算信息，使主要贡献能定位到骨架改进。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险
