### 1. SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14777
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14777
- Authors: Jinyang Wu, Shuo Yang, Zhengxi Lu, Fan Zhang, Yuhao Shen, Lang Feng, Haoran Luo, Zheng Lian, Shuai Zhang, Zhengqi Wen, Jianhua Tao

- arXiv updated: 2026-07-16

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Large language models are increasingly trained as interactive agents for long-horizon tasks involving multi-turn interaction, tool use, and environment feedback. Outcome-based reinforcement learning (RL) provides a practical optimization paradigm, but its sparse trajectory-level rewards offer limited guidance on intermediate decisions, leaving a supervision gap between episode-level outcomes and token-level policy learning. We propose SEED (SElf-Evolving On-Policy Distillation), a self-evolving framework that converts completed on-policy trajectories into training-time hindsight skills and distills their behavioral effect back into the policy model. SEED first fine-tunes the policy to analyze completed trajectories and generate natural-language skills that capture reusable workflows, decisive observations, or failure-avoidance rules. During RL, the current policy both collects trajectories and serves as the analyzer that extracts hindsight skills from them. Policy updates therefore improve subsequent decision making and skill analysis together, allowing hindsight supervision to evolve with the policy. SEED then re-scores the sampled actions under ordinary and skill-augmented contexts, converting the skill-induced probability shift into a dense token-level on-policy distillation signal. This signal is jointly optimized with outcome-based RL, keeping the auxiliary supervision aligned with the current trajectory distribution. Extensive experiments on text-based and vision-based agentic tasks show that SEED consistently improves performance and sample efficiency, exhibiting robust generalization to unseen scenarios. Our code is available at https://github.com/jinyangwu/SEED.

#### 摘要中文翻译

长时智能体需多轮交互、工具调用和环境反馈，但结果奖励难以指导中间决策。SEED 将完成的在策略轨迹转为训练期事后技能，再把其行为影响蒸馏回策略。模型先学习分析完整轨迹，提取可复用流程、关键观察和避错规则；RL 时当前策略同时作为行动者与分析者，随参数更新同步产生新轨迹和技能。随后用普通与技能增强上下文重评相同动作，形成密集 token 信号，与结果 RL 联合优化。在文本及视觉智能体任务中改善表现和样本效率，部署不需要额外技能提示或外部记忆。

#### 研究背景

事后轨迹揭示哪些步骤有效，但把它长期保存在静态技能库会与新策略遇到的错误错位。本文试图同步经验来源与分析模型，使事后上下文影响进入参数。

#### Method

每基座先取 180 个训练任务、各 8 条轨迹，请 GLM-5.2 标注技能，再做 3 epochs SFT。之后每轮冻结当前 actor/analyzer 快照收集与分析轨迹，更新时用当前模型在有、无技能上下文重评分。

令 $\Delta=\operatorname{sg}(\ell_{\rm skill}-\ell_S)$、$g=\sigma(5\Delta)$；辅助损失为 $\mathbb E[m g(\operatorname{sg}\ell_{\rm skill}-\ell_S)]$，梯度等价于门控加权 NLL，不是完整词表 KL。总目标为 GRPO 加 0.01 倍辅助项。附录 A 构造目标 $r(v\mid c)\propto\pi(v\mid c)\mathbb E[g\mid c,v]$，说明非恒定技能门能在奖励全平时仍提供信号；收益还需门控与动作价值正相关。

### 2. On-Policy Delta Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15161
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.15161
- Authors: Byeongho Heo, Jaehui Hwang, Sangdoo Yun, Dongyoon Han
- 作者单位：NAVER AI Lab。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-16
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-16
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation is an alternative post-training method in reinforcement learning that alleviates the constraints imposed by reward models by providing token-level supervision from a teacher model. Although on-policy distillation has been studied and applied across various settings, its fundamental design remains underexplored. In this paper, we introduce a new distillation reward, termed the delta signal, instead of directly imitating the teacher's output distribution. The delta signal is defined as the difference between the teacher model and its base model prior to instruction tuning for reasoning capability. It therefore captures the changes induced by reasoning tuning and provides a more direct signal for transferring reasoning capabilities. Using extensive empirical evidence, we show that the delta signal substantially improves on-policy distillation and refer to the new distillation method as On-Policy Delta Distillation (OPD$^2$). Experiments across mathematics, science, and code-reasoning benchmarks demonstrate that OPD$^2$ consistently outperforms conventional on-policy distillation, enabling reasoning LLMs to achieve strong performance with only a short post-training period. Code will be available at https://github.com/naver-ai/opd2

#### 摘要中文翻译

在线蒸馏以教师的逐 token 监督替代对奖励模型的依赖，但其基本奖励设计仍有改进空间。本文提出 delta signal，即推理调优教师与其指令调优前基础模型的对数概率差，用来强调后训练带来的变化，而非复制教师的全部输出偏好。作者通过词频、token 可视化及统计分析研究该信号，再结合中心化和与传统 OPD 方向一致的门控，形成 On-Policy Delta Distillation（OPD2）。数学、科学和代码实验覆盖多规模 Qwen3 的思考/非思考模式及 Gemma4，显示短后训练中总体优于常规 OPD。

#### 研究背景

强教师包含预训练阶段的语言习惯和后训练增量，原始教师—学生 log-ratio 无法区分二者。只最大化教师前后差值又没有依赖学生的停止点，可能不断强化某些 token，因此作者仍用标准 OPD 的方向约束 delta 的应用。

#### Method

定义 $R_t^\Delta=\log\pi_T(y_t)-\log\pi_{T,\mathrm{base}}(y_t)$，与传统 $R_t^{\mathrm{OPD}}=\log\pi_T(y_t)-\log\pi_\theta(y_t)$。二者均减去学生当前分布下的期望，得到 $A_t^\Delta,A_t^{\mathrm{OPD}}$；期望用学生 top-1024 近似。最终采样 token 的权重为

$$
A_t^{D^2}=A_t^\Delta\mathbf1[A_t^\Delta A_t^{\mathrm{OPD}}>0],\qquad g=\mathbb E\sum_t A_t^{D^2}\nabla\log\pi_\theta(y_t).
$$

当学生等于教师时传统优势为零，门控停止更新；同号只保证局部采样方向一致，不保证参数更新后单调逼近教师。实现基于 TRL GRPOTrainer，允许每题一个完成、使用 token 信号并关闭组归一化和额外参考 KL；所有奖励统一乘 0.1（第 2.3、3.1 节）。

### 3. Non-vacuous Generalization Bounds for Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14506
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14506
- Authors: Yuxuan Zhu, Rohan Alur, Daniel Kang
- 作者单位：伊利诺伊大学厄巴纳香槟分校（UIUC）；麻省理工学院（MIT）；Bridgewater AIA Labs（首页列出）。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-16
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-16
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> While reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) is widely used to improve the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs), the generalizability of the resulting models remains poorly understood. In this work, we establish the first non-vacuous generalization bounds for parameter-efficient RLVR fine-tuning at the billion-parameter scale. Our approach adapts PAC-Bayes compression bounds to this setting, and addresses the inherent stochasticity of token generation by applying the Gumbel-max reparameterization trick. To operationalize these bounds, we propose the Progressive RLVR framework, which integrates RLVR with on-policy distillation, TinyLoRA, and model quantization. Progressive RLVR empirically retains 84-97% performance of standard LoRA fine-tuning while producing models that are 14,796x more compressible. We show that this framework yields non-vacuous generalization bounds in four domains: mathematical problem-solving, programming, general-knowledge reasoning, and Text-to-SQL. Our bounds exceed the accuracy of the base model by 9-51% and lie within 6-11% of the accuracy of the fine-tuned models.

#### 摘要中文翻译

RLVR 广泛用于提升推理，但所得模型的泛化保证仍不充分。本文将 PAC-Bayes 压缩界扩展到十亿参数模型的高效微调，通过 Gumbel-max 将 token 生成随机性显式重参数化。为使界非平凡，Progressive RLVR 先训练强 RLVR 教师，再以 OPD、TinyLoRA 和量化压缩参数更新。所测方法保留标准 LoRA 教师 84%–97% 的性能，而更新的可压缩性平均提高 14,796 倍。在数学、编程、知识推理和 Text-to-SQL 中，下界高于基础模型正确率 9–51 点，并距离微调学生正确率约 6–11 点。

#### 研究背景

需要同时控制训练问题抽样误差与随机生成误差；压缩界惩罚的是最终可重建的参数更新描述长度，而非训练过程中用过多少教师容量。

#### Method

令生成 $y=g(x,\xi;h)$，其中 Gumbel 噪声分布独立于假设 h。定理 1 给出 $\mathcal R(h)\ge\hat R-\Delta(1+n^{-1/2})\sqrt{[C(h)\log2+2\log C(h)+\log(2/\delta)]/(2m)}$。m 是训练问题数、n 是每题独立生成数；采用子样本估计时另加 Hoeffding 项。

先用 rank-64 LoRA 做 RLVR，随后冻结教师，在学生轨迹上提供密集 KL。TinyLoRA 将每模块更新写为 $U\Sigma(\sum_iv_iP_i)V^\top$，仅训练 16 个系数，最后量化至 5 个级别。附录 C 将 249 模块共 3,984 个系数、码本和量化方案选择位数纳入描述长度；14,796 倍指适配器编码压缩，不是完整 4B 模型存储减少。

### 4. Beyond Entropy: Correctness-Aware Advantage Shaping via Contrastive Policy Optimization

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14614
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.14614
- Authors: Weiwen Xu, Jia Liu, Hou Pong Chan, Long Li, Deng Cai, Min Chen, Hao Zhang
- 作者单位：香港中文大学；华南理工大学；南洋理工大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-16
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-16
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) commonly uses entropy for advantage shaping. However, entropy cannot distinguish useful uncertainty from detrimental confusion, limiting its effectiveness as a correctness signal. We propose Contrastive Policy Optimization (CPO), which uses token-level contrastive disagreement between reference-guided and vanilla generation distributions for correctness-aware advantage shaping. Both theoretical and empirical results show that this disagreement reliably indicates token-level correctness. We further show that On-policy Distillation is a special case of CPO, where the posterior distribution is instantiated by an external teacher model. CPO also resolves the zero-advantage problem. Experiments on in-domain and out-of-domain benchmarks demonstrate that CPO substantially outperforms entropy-based RLVR methods while maintaining strong generalization. Further analysis shows that correct and incorrect responses naturally support exploration and exploitation respectively, and balancing both leads to the best performance.

#### 摘要中文翻译

RLVR 常用熵调整优势，但熵无法区分有价值的不确定性与错误混乱。CPO 用参考答案条件化分布与普通分布的 token 对比分歧，提供面向正确性的优势塑形。作者从理论与经验上研究该信号，并将外部教师 OPD 视为一种后验分布选择。CPO 也为全对、全错的零优势组提供学习信号。在域内数学和域外推理基准上优于熵干预方法；分析认为正确与错误回答分别更多支持探索和利用，二者平衡表现最好。

#### 研究背景

同一个轨迹奖励广播至所有 token 无法表达局部贡献，全同奖励组则没有相对梯度。参考答案只用于重评当前轨迹，不作为静态 SFT 输出。

#### Method

理想正确性条件化分布满足 $\tilde\pi_{\rm post}(a\mid s)=\pi(a\mid s)g(s,a)/Z(s)$，因而 log-ratio 与未来正确完成概率 g 单调相关。实际以同一模型看参考答案的分布近似该后验，计算 $\delta_t=\log\pi_{\rm post}(y_t\mid s_t)-\log\pi(y_t\mid s_t)$。

对非零轨迹优势加上 $\alpha\delta_t$，并限制相反方向修正最多抵消原优势一半；零优势组直接用对比分歧。默认正确、错误两支系数均为 0.025，再进入 GRPO clipped loss。参考前向保持 stop-gradient（第 2 节、附录 C/E）。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

附录 A 承认额外前向约 20% 开销，并依赖参考答案；换用 critic 或其他上下文仍是未验证方向。

#### 我的分析：优点

明确区分理想正确性后验与实际参考条件分布，且用从分歧 token 继续采样的实验验证信号。零优势控制与三种子结果支持方法确实利用了额外监督。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

参考条件化近似正确后验是核心假设，Bayes 恒等式不证明实际教师可靠；实测主要显示负分歧预测错误，正分歧未提高续写准确率。Pass@16 更侧重覆盖率，不能直接当部署单次提升；GPQA 也并非所有模型都超过基础模型，例如 7B 的 68.8 仍低于 70.5。
