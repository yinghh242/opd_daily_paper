### 1. The Physics of Multi-Turn Long-Horizon Planning: From Pre-training to Post-training via Single- and Multi-Teacher On-Policy Agentic Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24720
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24720
- Authors: Tianyi Men, Zhuoran Jin, Kang Liu, Jun Zhao
- 作者单位：中国科学院自动化研究所，复杂系统认知与决策智能重点实验室；中国科学院大学人工智能学院。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-27
- Categories: cs.CL, cs.AI, cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Multi-turn long-horizon planning is critical for foundation model agents, yet how to fundamentally improve it remains unclear. Existing models are trained on uncontrollable and opaque Internet data, making it difficult to identify how planning ability is acquired, shaped, and integrated. To address this challenge, we introduce a unified and controlled multi-turn environment that enables precise control. It allows systematically study long-horizon planning across three stages. (1) Planning ability acquisition during pre-training. We study data format, distribution, and quality. Explicit world model construction through CoT state transition modeling yields stronger long-horizon generalization. Atomic skills alone are insufficient for compositional generalization, whereas a litte long-horizon data works. Moreover, suboptimal trajectories severely impair performance because errors amplify over long horizons. (2) Planning ability shaping via GRPO and OPD post-training. Through mutual information, we distinguish general planning patterns from task-specific planning knowledge. For planning patterns, we identify three application regions of post-training: unnecessary, effective, and unsupported. OPD has a broader effective region than GRPO under low-quality and long-horizon settings, as it provides more consistent update directions. For planning knowledge, distilling unseen procedures from a teacher with different knowledge may impair student's prior world modeling without fully establishing new knowledge. (3) Planning ability integration through MOPD post-training. We show that multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) integrates capabilities by converging to shared planning-pattern across environments. Compatible patterns enable cross-environment generalization, partially shared patterns support continual learning, while completely conflicting patterns cause severe interference.

#### 摘要中文翻译

多轮长时域规划如何在不同训练阶段获得与整合仍不清楚，现有模型不透明的互联网预训练数据阻碍机制研究。本文建立可控制任务长度、数据质量、规划知识和模式的环境，研究三个阶段：预训练中，显式状态转移 CoT 比直接动作预测泛化更好，仅原子技能不足，少量长轨迹有帮助，低质量轨迹会放大累计错误；后训练中，在理想教师下 OPD 比稀疏奖励 GRPO 能在更差数据和更长任务上改善通用规划模式，但不同程序知识的蒸馏可能损害原有能力；多教师阶段中，共享兼容模式支持跨环境泛化，部分共享有助持续学习，完全冲突会导致干扰和遗忘。

#### 研究背景

本文所称“物理学”是控制变量的合成研究，并非物理环境规划。它区分可跨样本重复的规划模板与具体配方规则，追问后训练是在选择已有模式，还是注入新程序知识。

#### Method

从零训练约 100M 参数 Qwen2 架构和 3000 词 BPE，在炼金、养殖、电子装配三个领域构建 AND/OR 合成图；每域五层、每层四十类、每类二十个实体名。保留新实体组合测试，短/中/长任务为 1—5、6—8、至少 9 步。

预训练改变 CoT 世界模型、时域配比和正确/错误模板比例。OPD 在学生交互前缀上优化 token KL，主要用 student top-100 支持内重归一化的 GKD；GRPO 使用二值最终成功奖励。作者以 $I(T;P)<I(T;K)$ 解释共享模板与样本特定知识的差异，但未直接估计两种互信息。MOPD 仅研究顺序 FA→LF→EA 蒸馏，通过增删共享模板和插入“正确推理、错误动作”制造不同冲突（第 3—6 节）。

### 2. RP-OPSD: Resolution-Privileged On-Policy Self-Distillation for Multimodal Large Language Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24447
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24447
- Authors: Qihui Zhu, Yuchen Wang, Zijian Wen, Tao Zhang, Mengjie Zhang, Yang Liu, Shuangwu Chen, Siying Wu, Jian Yang, Xiaofeng Jiang
- 作者单位：中国科学技术大学；ChangXin Memory Technologies（长鑫存储）。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-27
- Categories: cs.CV

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD) uses privileged information available only to the teacher to provide dense token-level supervision on trajectories generated by the student. However, existing methods often rely on verified solution traces, explanations generated by external models, or manually localized visual evidence, which limits their scalable application to multimodal large language models. To address this issue, we exploit the information gap between high- and low-resolution views of the same image and propose RP-OPSD (Resolution-Privileged On-Policy Self-Distillation for Multimodal Large Language Models). During training, the student policy generates on-policy trajectories from images at one-quarter of the original resolution, while the teacher policy provides supervision using the original-resolution images. By minimizing the divergence between their output distributions along the student trajectories, the student learns the predictive behavior of the teacher under high-resolution inputs, thereby strengthening its low-resolution capability and transferring the learned improvement to original-resolution inference. RP-OPSD requires neither additional human annotations nor external models to generate solution traces but only image--question pairs. Experiments on Qwen3.5-9B show that RP-OPSD achieves a 5.45\% relative improvement in average performance at the original resolution and a $1.78\times$ training speedup over OPSD. These results demonstrate that resolution differences can serve as a simple and scalable source of privileged information, providing an effective and efficient approach to on-policy self-distillation for multimodal large language models.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPSD 依靠教师独享信息为学生轨迹提供密集监督，但既有多模态方法常需参考解、外部模型解释或局部证据标注。RP-OPSD 直接利用同一图像高低分辨率的信息差：学生用原图四分之一像素生成，教师用原分辨率在相同轨迹上监督，学习收益还能迁移到原分辨率推理。方法仅需图像—问题对，无需答案或外部解答生成。Qwen3.5-9B 的原分辨率平均表现相对提高 5.45%，训练相对 OPSD 加速 1.78 倍。

#### 研究背景

降低图像分辨率会损失小物体、文字与纹理，同一模型因此自然形成能力差。这比裁剪证据更容易批量构造，但视觉信息过少时也可能使教师要求超过学生可见证据。

#### Method

学生图像宽高各减半，教师保留原图。师生同架构同初始权重，学生每题采 8 条回答；教师仅对学生前缀评分，不另生成目标答案。每批只更新学生一次再同步 rollout，教师按 $\phi\leftarrow0.95\phi+0.05\theta$ 更新。

在教师 top-100 词表支持上保留原 softmax 概率、不重新归一化，优化 $d_K(p\|r)=\sum_{v\in S_K}[p_v\log(p_v/r_v)-p_v+r_v]$。减去/补回概率质量使所保留位置匹配时梯度为零，但不是恢复所有被省略词表项的精确全 KL。只对 response token 计损失，无结果奖励、reference policy 或答案监督；全参数含视觉编码器训练（第 3 节）。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未单列 limitations。分辨率消融明确显示过强降采样使可用于迁移的视觉证据变弱；效率推导省略文本计算与系统开销，实测加速限定当前视觉 token 占主导的训练设置。EMA 仅平均更好，冻结教师在部分基准更优。

#### 我的分析：优点

无需额外答案和定位标注，教师特权来源明确且易复用；低分辨率与原分辨率分别评测，可区分直接鲁棒性改善和迁移。训练配置、数据组成与对 top-K 概率的处理披露较具体。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

教师可见而学生不可恢复的细节仍会制造不可实现目标，当前没有按信息损失程度分层分析。主要设置固定单种子，平均相对 OPSD 约 0.6 点的优势缺少方差支持。训练省时包含减少视觉 token 和去掉答案提示，不能不加条件地归因于任何多模态自蒸馏都能提速。

### 3. Rethinking Classifier-Free Guidance in On-Policy Diffusion Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24731
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24731
- Authors: Bingnan Li, Haozhe Wang, Haozhong Xiong, Fangtai Wu, Jinpeng Yu, Yang Shi, Jiaming Liu, Ruihua Huang
- 作者单位：Alibaba，Qwen Business Unit。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-27
- Categories: cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) adapts diffusion models by querying a teacher along trajectories generated by the current student, but how it should behave under classifier-free guidance (CFG), a default component of modern diffusion systems, remains poorly understood. Existing OPD methods naturally extend velocity matching to the CFG-composed prediction, directly matching teacher and student guided velocities. We show that this objective is under-identified at the branch level: positive- and negative-branch errors can compensate in the guided prediction. Through two contrasting cases, we find that naive matching remains effective under shared negative conditioning, where both branch errors decrease jointly. When the model's native CFG schema retains privileged information in the teacher's negative branch that is unavailable to the student, however, this joint reduction breaks down and the composed objective induces antagonistic branch-error dynamics, reducing the positive-branch error while increasing the negative-branch error. We term this failure mode Negative Branch Asymmetry (NBA). To address NBA, we introduce Positive--Direction Matching (PDM), a branch-aware OPD objective that separately constrains the positive prediction and the CFG conditional direction. We apply PDM to dense-to-sparse video control, where naive guided matching is highly sensitive to inference guidance scales, while branch-aware supervision enables more robust and effective knowledge transfer.

#### 摘要中文翻译

扩散 OPD 在学生轨迹上查询教师，但如何与 CFG 结合仍不清楚。直接匹配师生引导后速度存在分支不可识别性：正负分支误差可以互相抵消。共享负条件时这未必有害；若教师负分支仍包含学生看不到的特权信息，训练却可能降低正分支误差、增加负分支误差，形成 Negative Branch Asymmetry。PDM 分别约束正分支预测与 CFG 条件方向，避免组合掩盖分支错误。在由密集视频控制向稀疏关键帧控制的蒸馏中，它改善控制保真度，并显著降低对推理 guidance scale 的敏感性。

#### 研究背景

CFG 的负分支并不总是纯空条件，有些原生模型接口保留参考图或控制序列。保留双分支的学生仍允许用户改变 guidance scale，若只在训练尺度匹配组合，换尺度就会暴露抵消误差。本文研究这一接口，区别于把 CFG 烘焙进单分支、用于少步生成的蒸馏。

#### Method

引导速度为 $\tilde v=\gamma v^++(1-\gamma)v^-$。设师生误差 $e_+,e_-$，普通损失只约束 $\gamma e_++(1-\gamma)e_-$，零损失允许 $e_+=(\gamma-1)e_-/\gamma$ 的无穷非零解。PDM 使用 $\ell=\|e_+\|^2+\lambda\|e_+-e_-\|^2$，$\lambda>0$ 时零损失迫使两分支误差均为零。对照 IBM 则直接分别匹配两分支，去掉组合交叉项（第 3 节）。

视频中学生完成全部 50 步 rollout，但只在最早 8 个状态查询教师监督。教师看到全帧控制，学生只有第 0 帧参考外观与第 20/40/60 帧控制；正负分支均沿用各自原生条件。默认 $\lambda=1$。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

#### 我的分析：优点

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

### 4. FlowCTS: On-policy Continuous Trajectory Supervision of Flow Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24522
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24522
- Authors: Kaiyang Ye, Yuan Ge, Junxiang Zhang, Bei Li, Ziming Zhu, Haishu Zhao, Xiaoqian Liu, Chenglong Wang, Jingbo Zhu, Zhengtao Yu, Tong Xiao
- 作者单位：东北大学；NiuTrans Research；昆明理工大学。
- arXiv published: 2026-07-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-07-27
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CV

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> While on-policy distillation (OPD) effectively addresses sparse rewards and exposure bias in large language model post-training, its extension to flow models remains underexplored. To this end, we propose Flow Continuous Trajectory Supervision (FlowCTS), which matches subsequent student and reference trajectories initialized from the same student-visited state. Using the integral relation between trajectories and velocity fields, we derive a temporally weighted velocity-matching upper bound and discretize it into practical objectives parameterized by the number of supervision steps. Under a multi-reference setup, single-state FlowCTS-OPD outperforms vanilla KL-based OPD with faster convergence. FlowCTS-OPD improves GenEval from 0.90 to 0.93, OCR from 0.90 to 0.92, and PickScore from 22.75 to 23.06, while outperforming a mixed-reward RL baseline across all target metrics. Further analysis reveals a clear temporal supervision mismatch in vanilla KL-based OPD arising from its auxiliary SDE transition kernels. Beyond on-policy setting,FlowCTS also consistently outperforms vanilla SFT , particularly on OCR, while increasing supervision steps exhibit a trade-off between richer trajectory information and greater optimization difficulty.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD 可以缓解语言模型后训练中的稀疏奖励与分布偏移，但推广至 flow 模型仍不足。FlowCTS 从学生访问的同一状态出发，匹配随后学生与参考模型的连续轨迹；根据轨迹和速度场的积分关系推导带时间权重的速度匹配上界，再离散为由监督步数控制的目标。多参考模型设置中，单状态版本比 KL 型 OPD 收敛更快，GenEval 从 0.90 到 0.93、OCR 从 0.90 到 0.92、PickScore 从 22.75 到 23.06。分析指出辅助 SDE 产生的 KL 权重与实际纠偏时段错配。离线 SFT 扩展也有收益，但增加监督步数带来信息与优化难度之间的权衡。

#### 研究背景

自回归序列有自然的下一 token 分布，确定性 flow ODE 则没有同样的转移核。将其人为转成 SDE 后再计算 KL，会引入依赖噪声方差的时间权重。因此本文直接从后续轨迹差异定义监督，而非把任意 SDE 的局部 KL 当成唯一合理目标。

#### Method

从共享 $x_{t_s}$ 出发，连续损失是 $\mathbb E\int_{t_e}^{t_s}\|x_t^\theta-x_t^{ref}\|^2dt$。用积分 Cauchy–Schwarz 得到速度上界，权重为 $w(r)=[(t_s-t_e)^2-(t_s-r)^2]/2$。离散为 $K$ 步 Euler 后，速度误差权重 $\alpha_i=[K(K+1)-i(i+1)]/2$，实现归一化为和 1（第 3 节、附录 A/B）。

$K=1$ 即共享学生状态上的速度 MSE；$K>1$ 在师生各自后续轨迹状态评价速度差。起始状态来自学生 SDE rollout 时是 OPD，来自离线图文数据轨迹时是 SFT 扩展。方法不减少推理采样步数，目的在后训练质量和多专家能力整合。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未独立列 limitations。正文与附录 E 反复指出更长监督范围会增加优化困难、压低细节偏好分；扩展 $K$ 应视作可调权衡，不能称通用增益。作者也明确与并行工作 DanceOPD 的单状态速度匹配有重合，区别主要在轨迹推导、时域分析与多步扩展。

#### 我的分析：优点

目标由真实连续动力学推导，并在相同多教师流程下控制比较，时间权重错误这一解释有明确数学和经验落点。等监督状态预算消融保留了偏好分下降，使“多步更强”的主张保持具体。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

速度上界小不直接保证所有图像评价指标提高；学生以专家 LoRA 均值初始化，成果不等于从原始基座独立得到全部能力。主要实验单一 SD3.5 架构且未报告训练种子方差，细小数值优势仍需重复验证；部分泛化指标如颜色、纹理和非空间关系实际低于 KL-OPD。
