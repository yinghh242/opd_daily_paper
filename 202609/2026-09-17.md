### 1. When EOS Tokens Disagree: Understanding Length Inflation in On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20511
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20511
- Authors: Yuxiao Yang, Tianrun Yu, Shangzhe Li, Kaixiang Zhao, Xuchao Zhang, Chetan Bansal, Huaxiu Yao, Taylor W. Killian, Weitong Zhang
- 作者单位：University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill；Brigham Young University；Microsoft。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-17
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-17
- Categories: cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> We study length inflation in on-policy distillation (OPD), where student responses can become excessively long and even exhaust the generation budget. We identify \emph{termination-token mismatch} between base students and post-trained teachers as an important source of this behavior. Across Qwen3, Llama, and Gemma, the two models can place their stopping probability on different EOS tokens, even when their declared stopping sets are identical. This mismatch can suppress the student's preferred termination action without reliably transferring the teacher-preferred alternative. We show that aligning the decoding stopping set alone is insufficient, while treating functionally equivalent EOS tokens as a shared semantic stopping action substantially mitigates mismatch-induced length inflation across all three model families. To further understand how termination behavior evolves over training, we study OPD across different K2-Horizon training stages. This stage-wise analysis shows that termination preferences can shift substantially during training, while also revealing a distinct length inflation late in the OPD run that persists beyond termination alignment. Together, these results identify termination mismatch as an important, but not exhaustive, source of OPD length dynamics. We release an implementation incorporating the proposed termination-handling corrections.

#### 摘要中文翻译

本文研究OPD中学生回答过长甚至耗尽生成预算的现象，指出base学生与post-trained教师终止token偏好不一致是重要来源。Qwen3、Llama、Gemma中，即使声明的stop集合相同，师生也可能把停止概率放在不同EOS上。这样教师会压低学生常用的停止token，却无法可靠传递学生几乎不采样的另一个EOS。只对齐解码stop集合不够，将语义等价EOS合为一个停止动作能明显缓解该问题。K2-Horizon分阶段实验进一步发现训练中终止偏好改变，但即使EOS对齐，后期仍可能长度再膨胀，因此终止失配不是全部解释。

#### 研究背景

“有正确答案却继续输出重复内容”与推理能力退化不同。sampled-token反馈只直接监督学生访问的动作，若teacher对该EOS低概率，学生受到停止惩罚；teacher喜欢的替代EOS若几乎无学生质量，就很难得到直接训练。配置层合法停止与目标分布层语义对齐必须分开。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20511)正文及附录A–D。比较四种修正：仅将双方EOS加入decoder；把教师全部等价终止质量映射到学生原生EOS；将双方EOS质量相加视为同一stop类；或再从学生动作空间删除其他EOS并在采样与actor评分同时重归一。

语义类方案对$\mathcal E$内token定义

$$
\bar\pi(\mathrm{stop}|s)=\sum_{e\in\mathcal E}\pi(e|s),\qquad
g_t=\operatorname{sg}[\log\bar\pi_T(\bar y_t|s)-\log\bar\pi_S(\bar y_t|s)]\nabla\log\bar\pi_S(\bar y_t|s).
$$

非EOS保持原token动作，所有等价EOS都可终止。Qwen显式失配用teacher侧映射就能修正；跨家族实验各自在家族内部蒸馏，采用语义聚合，非不同家族间词表蒸馏。全词表reverse KL仍有学生概率加权，所以扩大词表计算不自动解决几乎零质量EOS的弱梯度。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

第6节明确EOS失配不能解释全部长度动态，尤其K2-Horizon后期再膨胀原因仍未知。当前仅单轮数学，工具交接、消息结束、文档结束在多轮智能体里可能是不同事件，不能盲目合并。模板/grader分析只在Qwen三模板两判分器展开，未证明同规律适用所有家族。

#### 我的分析：优点

先测decoder修正无效，再测分布级修正，定位了真正起作用的接口。Gemma相同stop集合仍失败和K2中间阶段不失败，构成有信息量的机制对照。明确分开冗余长度、数学能力与答案解析，避免把格式错误当训练退化。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

末位置统计混合自然EOS和预算截断，两类状态分布不同，不能单独作为全程停止hazard估计。语义合并需逐协议确认等价性，直接用于agent可能改变控制流。缺少full-vocabulary训练对照，关于其同样可能弱恢复的论证主要是局部梯度分析。修正EOS后仍须追踪长度、截断与原始答案文本，不能视为一劳永逸稳定化方案。

### 2. What Does Privileged Information Add to On-Policy Self-Distillation?

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20612
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20612
- Authors: XiuYu Zhang, Wei Chow, Junfeng Fang, Zhenkai Liang, Tat-Seng Chua
- 作者单位：National University of Singapore（新加坡国立大学）。依据HTML首页。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-17
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-17
- Categories: cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) lets a language model learn from a frozen copy of itself that sees an answer or a worked solution. Giving the teacher this extra information seems to offer the student more to learn, but how much does it add beyond distillation itself? To isolate that contribution, we construct AMPLE-Math, a reusable suite of 5,319 mathematical problems with six reasoning views that share the same answer, and compare each view with matched reference-free distillation. With a thinking-enabled teacher supervising direct-response rollouts, reference-free distillation accounts for much of Qwen3-1.7B's improvement under thinking-enabled evaluation, both in domain and on external benchmarks. Evidence for an additional reference benefit is modest in Qwen, strongest for a polished solution, whereas complete traces add two percentage points in SmolLM3-3B at step 50. These benefits depend on the student being trained. At the same checkpoint, replacing short direct-response rollouts with long thinking-enabled rollouts turns gains into losses in both families while the problems, references, and evaluation stay fixed. Teacher profiles and matched loss interventions in Qwen further show that changing token-level supervision can leave student behavior largely unchanged. Together, these findings suggest that OPSD can improve access to existing reasoning capabilities through parameters shared by direct-response and thinking-enabled inference. The value of a privileged reference is what it adds to this cross-mode transfer, not how much of the solution it reveals.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPSD让模型向能够看到答案或完整解答的冻结自身学习，但额外信息究竟比蒸馏本身多带来多少收益？作者构建AMPLE-Math：5319道数学题，每题有六种共享同一答案的推理视图，并为每种视图设置匹配的无参考蒸馏对照。在开启thinking的教师监督直接作答学生轨迹、评测开启thinking的设置下，无参考蒸馏已解释Qwen3-1.7B的大部分域内和外部改善；额外参考收益较小，整理后的解答证据相对更强，而SmolLM3-3B的完整轨迹在第50步额外提升约2个百分点。收益依赖被训练的学生：相同步数下，将短直接回答换成长thinking轨迹，可让两家族中的收益变为退化。教师评分诊断及匹配loss干预还显示，改变token级监督并不一定改变学生行为。作者据此提出，OPSD可能通过两种推理模式共享的参数改善已有推理能力的调用；参考的价值应按其额外迁移收益判断，而不是按内容完整程度判断。

#### 研究背景

相对初始模型有提升，不能单独归因于特权解答：即使移除解答，thinking教师与直接作答学生仍处于不同条件分布。本文把“蒸馏有效”与“参考额外有用”分开，并检验更完整的解答、反思词压制和监督窗口等机制解释是否真的对应训练改善。

#### Method

这是受控研究和数据资源，主干沿用冻结教师OPSD。六视图为Answer Only、Gist、Key Points、Clean Solution、Summary、Full Trace，每题共享同一规范答案。学生只读问题，教师在相同学生前缀上读取相应视图或不读参考。实际目标采用released实现的teacher→student KL逐词表贡献上截断0.05，再在受监督响应位置平均，不沿采样轨迹求导。

主配置学生关闭thinking、生成最多1024token，教师开启thinking；另一配置允许长thinking rollout，却仍只监督最前1024token。因此该比较同时改变模式、长度和监督覆盖率，不能只归因为thinking开关。

教师诊断在固定响应上计算平均采样token log-prob差 $\Delta_v(y)$，再在每题同时有对/错响应的子集上求

$$
C_v=\mathbb E_x\left[\mathbb E(\Delta_v\mid x,\mathrm{correct})-
\mathbb E(\Delta_v\mid x,\mathrm{incorrect})\right].
$$

它度量正确响应是否得到相对有利的分数变化。另测反思词位置压力和沿响应位置的KL分配。随后做真实训练干预：替换成长度匹配的别题参考、截断参考开头、减小或删除反思词loss、把loss窗口从Early-1K扩到First-4K或分散到四个256token窗口。所有效果回到匹配checkpoint比较。

附录D.4给出明确边界：正 $C_v$ 可在固定样本的指数重加权中提高正确率，但这不是参数更新。作者构造了平均alignment和KL相同、共享参数梯度却使准确率一升一降的反例，说明诊断不能替代训练收益。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

Discussion明确将证据限定于两模型家族、数学任务和短程LoRA训练。文中承认长thinking对照同时改变生成模式、horizon与loss覆盖；统计区间描述当前seed下的问题抽样不确定性，seed变异另行报告，区间含0不建立等价。按学生演化动态选择参考属于后续方向。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

### 3. RetireOPD: Self-Retiring On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20784
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20784
- Authors: Yan Yu, Zhengxi Lu, Yizhou Liu, Yichen Pan, Aozhe Wang, Qipeng Chen, Hua Yang, Wenqi Zhang, Weiming Lu, Qianglong Chen, Yongliang Shen
- 作者单位：Zhejiang University；Alibaba Group。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-17
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-17
- Categories: cs.CL, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Multi-turn agents trained with reinforcement learning (RL) receive a single scalar reward per trajectory, which motivates self on-policy distillation (OPD) to supply dense token-level supervision from a self-teacher with privileged task skills, letting a skill-free student internalize them. This recipe, however, is undermined by two findings in agentic tasks: privileged information alone does not always make a teacher reliable, and the benefit of teacher supervision is stage-dependent. We therefore propose RetireOPD (Self-Retiring On-Policy Distillation), which first optimizes a decoupled, skill-conditioned teacher with environment rewards and then trains a skill-free student jointly with RL and OPD. Rather than following a predefined distillation schedule, RetireOPD adopts Adaptive Retirement: the student drops the teacher on its own once their discrepancy stops shrinking and it reaches a target fraction of the teacher's success rate, after which training proceeds with RL alone. Across Qwen2.5 models from 1.5B to 7B, RetireOPD improves ALFWorld success rate over RL baseline by 14.1% to 18.8% and WebShop accuracy by 11.8% to 19.0%, and surpasses its own skill-conditioned teacher in every setting.

#### 摘要中文翻译

多轮智能体的RL每条轨迹只得到一个标量奖励，因此可用带特权技能的自教师提供密集OPD监督。但特权并不自动带来可靠教师，教师帮助也随训练阶段变化。本文提出RetireOPD，先用环境奖励训练解耦的技能条件教师，再让无技能学生联合学习RL与OPD。无需预定蒸馏日程，学生在师生差距不再缩小且达到教师某比例成功率时自动退役教师，之后只做RL。Qwen2.5从1.5B至7B在ALFWorld和WebShop上均改善GRPO并超过自己的技能教师，家庭成功率提高14.1–18.8点，购物准确率提高11.8–19.0点。

#### 研究背景

同权重有技能分支不一定优于无技能分支，先训练教师利用技能是第一步；教师固定后，持续匹配可能限制学生进一步利用环境奖励。文章研究的是教师何时退出，而不是逐样本或词元门控。其“自教师”与学生同容量同初始化，但已额外独立RL训练，不是训练时同一权重分支。

#### Method

技能从SkillRL的SkillBank按关键词检索。先训练Skill-GRPO教师并用验证成绩选择、冻结；学生不见技能，优化 $\mathcal L_{\rm GRPO}+0.01\mathcal L_{\rm OPD}$，OPD用采样词元教师学生log-ratio近似RKL。

每5步统计窗口平均log-prob差 $\bar K_m$，计算反弹指示 $\rho_m=(\bar K_m-\bar K_{m-1})/\bar K_m$，以及相对能力 $\eta_m=(SR_m+SR_{m-1})/(2SR_T)$。首次同时满足 $\rho_m\geq0$、$\eta_m\geq0.9$ 时移除教师，之后继续GRPO并停止教师前向。差距停滞是局部训练诊断，不是无条件证明未来教师已无用。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20784)，正文及教师构造、恢复和阈值附录。

### 4. MAGMA-GEN: Validated Recovery Supervision from Ambiguous Failures via Counterfactual Re-Execution

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20056
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20056
- Authors: Loan Bernat, Matthieu Grard, Ariane Herbulot, Florent Lamiraux
- 作者单位：Siléane；LAAS-CNRS。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-17
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-17
- Categories: cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要同时包含 on-policy/on policy 与 distillation/self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Hierarchical robotic systems executing long-horizon manipulation tasks must make high-level semantic decisions that orchestrate stochastic low-level skills. In this setting, failed rollouts are ambiguous: a poor downstream state may reflect an invalid high-level decision, partial observation, or a valid decision whose physical execution failed. Traditional supervised learning lacks data for such recovery states, while reinforcement learning struggles with sparse rewards and non-local credit assignment. We propose MAGMA-GEN, an on-policy data-generation pipeline that converts ambiguous failed rollouts into validated recovery supervision. MAGMA-GEN first uses a privileged coach to hypothesize an early decision-level error and propose localized correction or recovery actions. Because this diagnosis is fallible, candidates are retained only if re-execution from the same state under matched conditions improves downstream progress. This produces supervised examples from the agent's own failure distribution without per-step human demonstrations. Evaluated on interactive long-horizon manipulation tasks, MAGMA-GEN improves task success and recovery capabilities, against distillation and trajectory-repair baselines under evolving task constraints in both simulation and real-robot execution.

#### 摘要中文翻译

长程机器人系统需要用高层语义决策调度随机低层技能，失败原因因此含糊：可能决策错误、观察不完整，也可能动作合理但物理执行失败。普通监督缺少这些恢复状态，RL又面临稀疏奖励和非局部信用分配。本文提出MAGMA-GEN，用在策略数据生成把含糊失败变成经验证的恢复监督。特权教练先假设较早的错误决策并提议局部纠正或恢复动作，再从同一状态、匹配条件重新执行，只有确实改善后续进展的候选才保留。这样无需逐步人工示范即可从学生自身失败分布采集监督。仿真任务的恢复及整体成功优于被测蒸馏修复基线，真实机器人展示变化任务约束下的迁移可行性。

#### 研究背景

抓取失败不一定说明“选择抓取”决策错误；错误可能是下一步仍假定手中已有物体。MAGMA-GEN把教练诊断看作候选而非真值，以环境重执行验证其作用。该工作主要贡献是高质量数据生成管线，后续模型更新是标准监督学习，不是教师分布KL匹配。

#### Method

把任务分为随交互改变目标和规则的阶段。4B学生在含执行及观察扰动的仿真中rollout，20B教练见特权任务规范，提出可疑错误轮和局部替换或后继恢复动作。同胞分支共享阶段开始时采样的扰动，从相同物理状态重放，之后继续由当前学生行动。每状态还可扩展 $B$ 个普通学生动作。

对任一候选动作，以其后代rollout完成当前阶段的比例 $\operatorname{Succ}_j(s,a)$评分，选择 $a^*=\arg\max_a\operatorname{Succ}_j(s,a)$；仅当该值大于0时加入监督集。教练动作和普通动作同等竞争，不自动优先教练。最后用 $-\log\pi_\theta(a^*\mid m,o,U)$ 训练。部署无需教练或仿真器。来源：[arXiv 官方PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20056)，方法第4–6页、实验第7–9页及附录配置。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

第6节指出只能处理现有高层技能可恢复的状态；物理无法恢复时无效。采集依赖可恢复仿真状态及足够真实的动作后果，仿真观察或执行偏差会影响真机。真实环境重置不可用时如何验证尚未解决；整体成功率仍较低，长期记忆和推理架构也限制表现。

#### 我的分析：优点

### 5. Intrinsic Sequence-Likelihood Confidence in Retrieval-Dominated Extractive QA: Two Pre-Specified Negatives, and What They Do and Do Not Attribute

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19942
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.19942
- Authors: Gunwoo Lee, Changmin Sung, Sang-Hwan Gwak, Ina Kim, Ji-Young Choi, Kyong-Ha Lee
- 作者单位：Large-scale AI Research Center, Korea Institute of Science and Technology Information（KISTI）；Department of Applied AI, University of Science and Technology（UST），韩国。依据HTML首页。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-17
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-17
- Categories: cs.CL, cs.IR, cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

历史误收：旧规则将 abstention policy 等词尾误匹配为 on policy。全文研究检索辅助适配触发与拒答路由，并非学生 rollout 上的 OPD/OPSD；保留真实全文笔记供追溯。

#### Abstract 原文

> In extractive document question answering whose questions were generated from the passages that contain their answers -- so that retrieval recovers 92-99.8% of what any mode combination could reach, whatever its absolute accuracy -- confidence-driven mechanisms have little to gain. Fine-tuning an open language model on a specialized domain corpus yields a model whose own confidence is a tempting control signal: it could decide which queries warrant further adaptation, and which answers to trust. We evaluate both uses under criteria fixed before the runs were executed, across four 7-9B model families whose adaptation moved closed-book F1 by at most +0.03, and both fail: a distillation trigger on all four families, under its pre-specified three-step transfer budget, and a routing-and-abstention policy in its single-model pilot. Retrieval alone recovers 92-99.8% of best-case combined accuracy under every correctness criterion we test, leaving routers no meaningful gain. The sequence-likelihood signal is insufficient relative to that mode -- area under the receiver operating characteristic curve 0.65-0.81 under the registered criterion -- before adaptation as well as after, unchanged by scalar recalibration and not consistently improved by token-level temperature rescaling. And the finer diagnostics depend on the correctness criterion and on answer length; on the three adapted combinations where we could test it, selector ablations show no statistically detectable downstream benefit from the confidence term on any seed; on Gemma, removing it changes the selector from failing to passing both registered criteria. The usable product is a set of pre-specified negatives with their dependencies made explicit.

#### 摘要中文翻译

在问题由包含答案的原文段落生成的抽取式文档QA中，检索已达到所有回答模式理想组合可实现成绩的92%—99.8%，使置信度驱动机制可改善的空间很小。作者在四个7—9B模型家族上，按运行前固定的标准评估两个用途：用序列似然置信度触发进一步蒸馏，以及在参数记忆、检索回答和拒答之间路由。前者在四家族均未通过预设标准，后者的单模型试验也失败。注册正确性标准下，内在置信度AUROC为0.65—0.81，适配前后均不足以支持比检索更有用的控制；标量单调校准不能改变排序，token温度缩放也未稳定改善。细粒度诊断受答案长度和判分标准影响；在三种实际适配组合中，移除置信项未显示下游损失，Gemma上还使筛选器从失败转为通过。论文贡献是预先规定标准的负结果及其适用条件，而非对所有置信度方法的一般否定。

#### 研究背景

本篇主要研究检索辅助自蒸馏的触发器和推理路由，不是OPD/OPSD学生rollout蒸馏方法论文；旧脚本可把“abstention policy”等词尾误匹配为“on policy”，属于历史严格相关性误收。其负结果仍可用于理解置信度proxy、训练选择机制和任务设置之间的区别。

#### Method

参数模型置信度取生成响应token概率的几何平均 $p_{FT}(q)=\exp(T^{-1}\sum_t\log p(y_t\mid y_{<t},q))$，它不是“答案正确的概率”。另计算检索回答与上下文的embedding相似度 $\phi$，以及检索回答与参数回答的分歧 $d$。三个量变为问题集内百分位rank，等权形成两个方向：

$$
S_{R\to F}=d_{rank}+\phi_{rank}+(1-p_{FT,rank}),\qquad
S_{F\to R}=d_{rank}+(1-\phi_{rank})+p_{FT,rank}.
$$

每方向选top32，入循环时计算一次，此后三轮重复使用。R→F每轮只做一步LoRA更新，损失为0.5倍检索答案上的masked CE，加0.5倍参数回答分布到检索条件化冻结副本的KL；F→R把偏好加入检索重排，不改模型权重。能力回退guard可能恢复原模型，但未被单独消融。

路由试验比较检索相似度rank与置信rank减去语料级KBI偏置，并允许低分或冲突时拒答。KBI偏置的实际符号与注册说明的理由相反，论文保留并报告这一问题。所有“注册”指运行前提交到版本控制，不是外部预注册平台。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷
