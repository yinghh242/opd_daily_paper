### 1. Who Teaches Which Token? Verifier-Gated Multi-Expert On-Policy Distillation for Scientific Reasoning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15404
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15404
- Authors: Xun Xu, Zaixi Zhang
- 作者单位：Fudan University；Hong Kong University of Science and Technology。依据HTML首页。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-14
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-15
- Categories: cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (OPD) is becoming the standard way to integrate specialist capabilities into one model: train experts with RL, then distill them into the student on its own rollouts. Existing recipes assign supervision at the sequence level - each prompt goes to one domain teacher and every token receives the same weight - which implicitly assumes that a teacher is uniformly useful across a response. We find instead that useful teacher signal is sparse and heterogeneous along a reasoning trajectory, which raises a finer question: who should teach which token? Verifier-Gated Multi-Expert On-Policy Distillation (VG-OPD) answers it by verification: the counterfactual gain of an expert on a specific answer criterion licenses that expert to teach, its disagreement with the student localizes the supervision, and criterion importance sets its weight; the gated KL enters GRPO as an additive token-level advantage. Instantiated for scientific reasoning with RL-trained capability experts, VG-OPD attains the best overall performance on seven benchmarks for 4B and 8B students, ranking first on five at both scales, with the largest gains on knowledge-intensive scientific reasoning tasks. Further analysis shows that the gains come from localizing verified supervision rather than from adding teachers or distillation loss: misplacing the same supervision budget is the single most damaging change, and indiscriminate distillation drags RL below its own floor where gated distillation lifts it.

#### 摘要中文翻译

多教师OPD常按问题领域分配一个专家，并对整条响应均匀监督，隐含教师在全程都同样有用。本文发现有用教师信号沿推理轨迹稀疏且异质，提出VG-OPD：专家在特定答案标准上的验证优势决定其是否有资格教学，专家对学生token的不认同决定监督位置，标准重要性决定权重，门控蒸馏作为token级advantage加入GRPO。四种RL训练的能力专家用于4B和8B科学推理学生，在七个基准上取得较好总体表现，知识密集科学任务收益最大。消融表明，同样监督预算若放错位置会明显退化，选择和定位比无差别添加教师更重要。

#### 研究背景

科学答案可能同时需要数值计算、符号推导、机制解释和证据判断。一个领域专家可能只纠正其中部分标准，因此路由粒度从“问题属于什么学科”细化为“该响应失败了哪个标准、哪个专家实际更好”。主实现每能力轴只有一个候选专家，因此先按标准轴指定候选，再验证是否启用；不是在任意专家池中自由发现最佳教师。

#### Method

学生一题生成8条响应，以rubric的带权标准得分形成GRPO任务奖励。仅对学生未满足的正权重标准 $j$，让对应能力专家独立greedy回答原问题，缓存后由相同verifier评估。若 $V_j(x,y_k)-V_j(x,y)>0$，专家获准；pitfall标准和verifier abstention不触发门控，奖励已达0.75的响应跳过probe。

获准专家在学生响应上teacher forcing，选其给学生实际token的log-prob最低10%位置，再左右扩一token。多个标准/教师mask重叠时，取最大权重者，每token最多一个教师。权重压缩为 $\bar\omega_j=\sqrt{\min(\omega_j,5)/5}$，结合门控和mask形成 $w_t$。

实际教师advantage使用非负k3估计再取负：

$$
\Delta_t=\log\pi_E(y_t\mid h_t)-\log\pi_S(y_t\mid h_t),\qquad
A_t^{KD}=-w_t\min(e^{\Delta_t}-\Delta_t-1,10).
$$

它与GRPO任务advantage分别进入policy-gradient loss，权重 $\lambda=0.25$。KD流处处非正，作用是局部压低选中的采样token，正向任务学习仍依赖GRPO。附录只对未clamp、固定权重的k3给出KL期望等式；实际clamp及依赖响应的mask使“精确KL”解释需区别对待。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

Limitations指出，七基准都是文本科学问题，集中在物理、化学、生物、医学和数学；图表、分子结构、实验数据等模态尚未覆盖，需要扩展criteria bank及verifier。仅测试4B和8B，规模规律还未系统验证。附录还披露证据/机制judge误差及pitfall误触发问题。

#### 我的分析：优点

区分专家有无教学资格和把监督放在哪里，再用等覆盖随机mask、随机路由与去gate实验分别检验。缓存同题专家响应、复用一次teacher forcing完成打分和mask，实施路径清楚。提供完整系统相对RL的+55%墙钟成本，避免把局部mask无额外前向误解成整体无开销。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

专家独立回答某标准更好，并不保证在学生错误前缀上的每个低概率token都是应修正位置；所谓disagreement主要是专家低log-prob排序，不直接比较师生分布差。正权重标准也受judge误差影响，最终答案通过不能保证自动提取的所有中间标准正确。KD流使用detached非负KL估计的负值，属于特定压制式surrogate，不能自动等同普通reverse-KL梯度。相对RL-only的配置还将entropy bonus从0改为0.01，需结合该差异解释“只增加教师”的归因；主表未给多训练seed区间。

### 2. Temporal Self-Distillation: Faster Inference in Discrete Diffusion Language Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15177
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15177
- Authors: Shijian Xu, Andrea Miele, Metod Jazbec, Volker Roth, Eric Nalisnick, Ilija Bogunovic
- 作者单位：University of Basel；University of Amsterdam；Johns Hopkins University。依据HTML首页。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-14
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-14
- Categories: cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要同时包含 on-policy/on policy 与 distillation/self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Diffusion language models (dLLMs) promise fast inference by generating multiple tokens in parallel, but suffer severe performance degradation when parallel decoding is pushed too aggressively. We introduce Temporal Self-Distillation (TSD), a simple on-policy method that trains dLLMs for fast inference by distilling predictions across time. Specifically, TSD distills the model's denoising distribution at earlier timesteps toward its distribution at the final timestep at which a token is committed. This encourages earlier predictions to better anticipate the model's eventual output, enabling much more aggressive parallel decoding. Because its teacher signal comes from the model itself, TSD requires no offline teacher generation and applies seamlessly to both base and post-trained policies. Across seven benchmarks in mathematics, planning, and code, TSD substantially shifts the speed--quality frontier toward the low-compute regime. TSD thus provides a simple, single-stage approach to accelerating dLLMs, achieving speedups competitive with offline distillation while avoiding a complex two-stage pipeline.

#### 摘要中文翻译

离散扩散语言模型可以并行生成多个token，但过早一次确定太多位置会降低生成质量。Temporal Self-Distillation（TSD）在模型自身的on-policy去噪轨迹上跨时间蒸馏：让某位置较早时刻的预测，接近它最终被确定时的预测分布，从而更早形成可靠判断，支持更激进的并行解码。教师信号来自同一模型，无需离线教师轨迹或独立教师checkpoint，可以用于原始策略和RL后训练策略。数学、规划和代码七个基准显示，它主要改善低推理预算下的速度—质量前沿，与离线蒸馏方法竞争，但不保证大预算下最高准确率改善。

#### 研究背景

Fast-dLLM按预测置信阈值选择可同时unmask的位置；降低阈值虽减少模型前向次数（NFE），但早期预测不准会放大错误。TSD直接训练早期分布，使其预见之后更完整上下文中的预测。这里“老师”是同一次轨迹中该位置的commitment-time分布，而非最终文本的硬token标签。

#### Method

在仅unmask、不回改已定token的采样器中，每个位置 $\ell$ 有唯一确定时刻 $t_\ell$。当前模型先无梯度生成整条轨迹及各位置确定时分布，再从该轨迹的去噪时刻均匀抽一个 $\hat t$，仅在此时仍masked的位置上反向：

$$
\widehat L=V(x^0)\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal M_{\hat t}}
D_{JS}\!\left(p_\theta(\cdot\mid c,x^{\hat t})_\ell,
\operatorname{sg}[p_\theta(\cdot\mid c,x^{t_\ell})_\ell]\right).
$$

默认对称JSD。均匀抽时刻无偏估计的是**轨迹和教师目标已detach后的时间平均蒸馏目标**，不包含离散轨迹采样的policy gradient。每条rollout只在一个时刻计算蒸馏反向。

奖励 $V$ 选择质量更好的轨迹：数学用二元答案正确性；Countdown正确1、仅语法正确但数值错0.1；Sudoku用空格正确比例；代码用测试通过率与格式分等权组合。数学和代码再按完成长度超过目标的程度线性降权，默认达到canvas上限时降至零，规划任务不加长度项。因此方法无离线教师，但仍依赖验证反馈，并非完全无标签自训练。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

第7节明确定位为提前实现已有行为，不承诺新增能力或提高大预算峰值；MATH500是主要例外。方法依赖unmask-only解码，若采样器允许修改已确定token，就需重新定义teacher target。奖励加权需要程序化正确性信号，开放式生成未覆盖；长度目标也需要按任务设置。

#### 我的分析：优点

利用同轨迹中更完整的上下文作为自教师，避免离线完整轨迹库和独立模型；单时刻估计降低反向成本。对基础策略和GDSD后训练策略分别验证，说明能力训练与推理加速可组合。跨canvas、无奖励和长度消融都保留了失败边界，没有把低NFE优势表述成统一峰值提升。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

最终确定位置的分布未必更正确，只是上下文更多；Countdown数值错仍有0.1权重、代码格式分也可给错误程序正权重，不能说所有失败轨迹都被排除。NFE未覆盖每次前向的序列长度、缓存与硬件效率，实际时延还需测量。主结果多来自阈值曲线的近似读数，缺少多训练seed区间；不同任务独立训练，且MATH500使用不同停止步数，完整复现成本需包含多模型和消融。

### 3. Reducing the Output-Mode Gap in Speech Language Models via Joint-Output On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15313
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15313
- Authors: Daxin Tan, Dehua Tao, Chengxi Deng, Hanlin Zhang, Xiao Chen
- 作者单位：AI Lab, Leibniz Research Center, Huawei；The Chinese University of Hong Kong；City University of Hong Kong。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-14
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-14
- Categories: eess.AS, cs.CL, cs.SD

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Autoregressive generation of interleaved text and acoustic tokens is a common approach to spoken-response generation in speech large language models. Although this design enables streaming generation with explicit textual guidance, generated acoustic tokens become part of the context for subsequent text predictions. Given identical speech inputs, we observe markedly lower answer accuracy for the internal text generated in speech-to-text-and-speech (S2TS) mode than for speech-to-text (S2T) responses. We term this discrepancy the \emph{output-mode gap} (OMG). To reduce OMG, we propose \emph{Joint-Output On-Policy Distillation} (JO-OPD), which distills the model's stronger S2T policy into joint generation using student-generated S2TS trajectories. At each text position, the S2T teacher provides soft targets from a text-only projection of the student's preceding outputs, while the student predicts from the corresponding full interleaved history. A preservation objective further regularizes native non-text predictions. Experiments on Step-Audio-2-mini and Baichuan-Audio-Instruct reveal OMG across two interleaved generation architectures. On Step-Audio-2-mini, JO-OPD reduces OMG from 42.87 to 16.26 percentage points on Spoken-MQA and from 29.72 to 13.04 points on speech-rendered GSM8K, with little change in S2T accuracy and substantially larger reductions than matched SFT baselines. ASR-based evaluation further shows a 7.49-point improvement in spoken-answer accuracy on Spoken-MQA.

#### 摘要中文翻译

文本与声学token交错生成支持流式语音，但已有声学token进入后续文本预测上下文。相同语音输入下，S2TS联合输出的内部文本准确率明显低于S2T纯文本回答，本文称为output-mode gap（OMG）。JO-OPD用模型较强的S2T策略监督学生S2TS轨迹：每个文本位置上，教师只看学生历史的文本投影，学生看完整交错历史；另用保留目标约束非文本预测。Step-Audio-2-mini的Spoken-MQA和语音GSM8K上，OMG分别42.87→16.26、29.72→13.04点，S2T能力变化很小；Spoken-MQA实际语音答案准确率提升7.49点。

#### 研究背景

这不是语音输入对文字输入的模态差距，输入语音保持完全相同，区别只在输出模式。内部文本改善也不保证最终音频说对答案，需要单独转写评测。方法针对已有交错架构，不重新设计Thinker–Talker。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15313)第3–5节。主实验先由初始学生采集固定S2TS轨迹，训练一epoch。对文本位置，冻结S2T教师读取同语音及文本历史投影，学生读取原声学/控制token交错历史，以teacher top-32文本概率重归一的soft CE训练；Hard仅用top-1。学生概率只在文本词表归一，所以该项不直接改变文字与声音的总质量分配。

非文本位置另对冻结base S2TS分布做保留：Step-Audio使用完整输出词表top-32 CE、权重0.05；Baichuan分别对controller和codec做KL、权重1和10。总损失$\mathcal L=\mathcal L_T+\mathcal L_{\rm pres}$，音频编码等组件冻结，更新共享语言模型。补充Refresh实验才阶段性重新采轨迹；主结果是初始on-policy数据的离线复用。

### 4. Reason What Matters: Retrieval-Grounded Reasoning for Universal Multimodal Embeddings

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15296
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15296
- Authors: Mingzhou Jiang, Peixi Wu, Hang Cheng, Yunhao Zhou, Biao Yang, Wei Yuan, Yun Li, Fan Yang, Wenwu Ou, Honghui He
- 作者单位：Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School, Tsinghua University；School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, University of Science and Technology of China；Kuaishou Technology；College of Future Information Technology, Fudan University。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-14
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-14
- Categories: cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要同时包含 on-policy/on policy 与 distillation/self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Universal multimodal embedding (UME) learns unified representations across modalities, enabling a single model to support diverse retrieval tasks. Recent methods use Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning to better interpret multimodal inputs before generating embeddings for complex retrieval tasks and further optimize this reasoning process through GRPO with retrieval-based rewards. However, two limitations hinder corpus-scale deployment. GRPO assigns all CoT tokens the same advantage, without identifying input-supported claims or evidence that distinguishes the positive from negatives. Moreover, generating a complete CoT before each embedding introduces substantial latency, even when a partial trace already provides sufficient retrieval evidence. To address these limitations, we propose Reason What Matters (ReWAM), a retrieval-grounded reasoning framework that uses retrieval feedback to guide both credit assignment and reasoning computation. Specifically, we introduce Retrieval-aware Self-Distillation (RASD), which constructs privileged guidance from input-supported evidence that distinguishes the positive item from retrieved hard negatives. An on-policy self-teacher uses this guidance to refine trajectory-level feedback into token-specific supervision for retrieval-relevant reasoning. We further develop Retrieval-adaptive Inference (RAI), which uses a retrieval confidence head to estimate the remaining retrieval utility of a partial CoT. It stops unproductive traces early and accelerates useful continuations with speculative decoding. Extensive experiments on MMEB-V2 and MRMR demonstrate that ReWAM achieves state-of-the-art retrieval performance while delivering up to 5x the inference throughput of competitive explicit-CoT UME methods. These results bridge the gap between retrieval quality and inference efficiency, making reasoning-enhanced UME practical for large-scale deployment.

#### 摘要中文翻译

通用多模态embedding把多种输入映射到共享检索空间。已有方法用检索奖励优化CoT，但候选感知监督与仅输入生成之间有错位：轨迹奖励不指出哪些输入证据区分正例与难负例，推理时也无法知道继续生成是否仍改善检索。本文提出ReWAM，RASD从输入事实和正负决策边界构造特权指导，由同模型自教师对学生CoT逐词评分，细化检索优势。RAI则从前缀检索反馈学习停止条件，无候选输入时截去冗余推理，并用推测解码进一步加速。MMEB-V2和MRMR表现较强，相对部分显式CoT基线吞吐最高约5倍。

#### 研究背景

embedding模型与reasoner分开：前者把输入加CoT编码，后者负责产生有用CoT。训练能看到正负候选，但部署编码查询或文档时只能看自身输入，因此特权必须蒸馏掉。高效检索还需区分OPD训练收益和停止/推测解码的推理收益。

#### Method

先用1.4M对数据以双向InfoNCE训练CoT条件embedder，再冻结它评价reasoner轨迹。检索奖励按相对无CoT的正负cosine margin提升给+1/0/-1，阈值0.01。122B多模态分析器读取输入、正例、top-3难负例和CoT，形成证据、边界、支持及冲突声明。当前reasoner有无特权重评同词元，构造

$$
\hat A_t=A\{1+\lambda_R\operatorname{clip}[e^{\operatorname{sign}(A)\Delta_t}-1,-\epsilon_R,\epsilon_R]\}.
$$

正权重保留轨迹优势符号，不另加可微教师KL。RAI用完整CoT每前缀剩余可得检索margin训练轻量utility head，预测小于0.01即停；另训练半自回归draft及Markov头，冻结reasoner验证。部署无需候选比较或训练分析器，但仍运行reasoner、停止头和draft。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.15296)，正文及数据、推理配置、完整结果附录。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

附录E指出查询侧CoT仍增加延迟，CEPI依赖额外外部分析器调用。未来需内化证据抽取与核验，并统一决定是否推理、何时停及draft多少词元。目标文档可离线缓存，但初次索引仍需CoT和embedding计算。

#### 我的分析：优点

奖励以无CoT为基线，衡量推理的额外检索价值，避免把原本容易的输入误当好推理。RASD与RAI分工明确，停止标签来自可检索效用而非仅长度。推理消融报告小幅精度代价和各模态吞吐，便于评估成本收益。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

最终系统包含8B reasoner、另一个embedder、utility head和draft，不能只按4B嵌入底座宣称总模型更小。RASD独立增量约0.5点，却需122B分析器构造特权；RAI还要730K轨迹，完整训练预算较大。基于训练候选的停止效用迁移到新检索库仍需检验。推测解码可保持给定目标的采样语义，提前停止本身会改输出，不能称整个RAI完全无损。
