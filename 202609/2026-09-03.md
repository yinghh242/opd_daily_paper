### 1. Sequential Beats Joint: On the Interplay between On-Policy Distillation and RLVR

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04108
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.04108
- Authors: Boyan Li, Bingsen Chen, Chenghao Yang, Ping Nie, Chen Zhao, Xi Ye
- 作者单位：New York University；University of Chicago；University of Waterloo；University of Alberta；NYU Shanghai；Alberta Machine Intelligence Institute（Amii）。依据HTML首页。

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) and on-policy distillation (OPD) have emerged as two dominant methods for post-training reasoning LLMs. Prior work uses OPD's dense token-level supervision to complement the sparse RL reward, fusing the two signals within a single step: either as a \emph{weighted-additive combination} or a \emph{teacher-modulated rescaling} of the RL advantage. In this paper, we show that a simple two-stage scheme, OPD-then-RL, consistently outperforms pure OPD, pure RLVR, and all such joint baselines across logic and math reasoning benchmarks. Beyond the empirical results, we further provide a systematic understanding of this through pass@$k$ behavior, learning dynamics, and parameter updates, yielding a consistent explanation: OPD expands the student's coverage of teacher-supported solutions and RL sharpens within that support, while jointly optimizing the two signals causes them to interfere.To provide a practical recipe, we find that the OPD validation score is the key signal for when to switch to RL, and that OPD is a better cold start for RL than SFT. Together, our results establish OPD-then-RL as a simple yet strong way to combine the two methods, turning two entangled signals into complementary stages.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD提供密集教师信号，RLVR提供稀疏但任务相关的验证奖励。已有方法通常在同一步中相加两种advantage，或用教师信号缩放RLadvantage。本文比较简单两阶段OPD-then-RL，在所测逻辑任务上明显优于纯OPD、纯RL及联合方法，在数学上取得最高平均pass@1但与最强几种方法统计相当。作者用pass@k、分布变化和参数更新解释：OPD先扩大教师支持解法的覆盖，RL再提高其中成功解法的概率；同时融合可能产生干扰。切换时的OPD验证成绩是后续RL成绩的重要预测信号，OPD也比所测SFT冷启动更有利。

#### 研究背景

教师模仿与任务奖励不必在同一阶段使用。学生尚缺乏成功轨迹时，OPD可扩大可采到正确解的范围；之后移除持续教师牵引，让GRPO提高正确解的单次成功率。本文主要建立一个应纳入后训练比较的顺序基线，而非新蒸馏loss。

#### Method

将共同PPO框架中的tokenadvantage写成教师项 $d_t=\log\pi_T(y_t\mid h_t)-\log\pi_S(y_t\mid h_t)$ 与组内奖励advantage $\widehat A$。联合方法分为加权相加 $w_R\widehat A+w_Td_t$ 和正权重调制 $m(d_t)\widehat A$；顺序方案直接采用

$$
A_t=\begin{cases}d_t,&s\le S,\\\widehat A,&s>S,\end{cases}
$$

默认第60步切换。逻辑总150步、数学总120步，所有比较保持总更新预算。教师冻结，tokenteacher项mask学生EOS，以免Qwen-Base和Instruct的不同EOS造成纯格式惩罚。SRPO、HDPO、RLSD原为自蒸馏方法，此处保留组合规则但换为外部教师，因此是受控机制改编，不是其原始端到端recipe。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

Limitations指出，目前重点是外部强教师、冻结教师和较弱学生；多教师、任务专家、特权自身教师尚需验证。先用RL改善教师再蒸馏的预算分配未与本方案作同计算量比较；OPD主要采用采样reverse-KL，完整分布、forward-KL或JSD可能形成不同冷启动，结论仍需扩展。

#### 我的分析：优点

方法简单且总步数匹配，是联合训练研究应有的强对照。既看单次成功率也看多次覆盖，避免只因RL分布变尖就断言新增能力；还包含EOS接口修正、任务专属混合系数调参和切换点分析。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

标题结论在逻辑上强、数学上小：平均31.8与31.6差距有限，数学pass@32还有TRRD数值更高，不能写成所有任务、所有k严格获胜。大k覆盖是有限采样下的可达性，不能直接等于新增推理能力；“在教师support内”也主要由交叉熵与top-k质量诊断支持。最终参数符号相同不等于优化过程没有梯度冲突，因顺序模型本来就继承OPD更新。缺少广泛独立训练seed和严格墙钟匹配，当前更适合作为强经验基线而非普适训练顺序定理。

### 2. Rethinking On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models II: One Training Example

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04172
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.04172
- Authors: Zixuan Fu, Bingxiang He, Yuxin Zuo, Haohuan Huang, Jinqian Zhang, Ruhang Xiao, Cheng Qian, Qinyu Luo, Huan-ang Gao, Yudong Wang, Zhiyuan Liu, Ning Ding, Chaojun Xiao
- 作者单位：Tsinghua University；University of Chinese Academy of Sciences；Northeastern University；University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign；Johns Hopkins University。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-03
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-03
- Categories: cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) combines student-generated rollouts with dense token-level supervision from a teacher. Existing work has mainly studied its algorithmic behavior, leaving the role of training data unclear. We examine this role at the data-minimal limit by training on a single query. One-shot OPD keeps improving for hundreds of steps and recovers most of full-data OPD's gain across task domains and model families. We explain this result through the states visited during training and the rate at which the student aligns with the teacher. We measure \emph{state coverage}, the fraction of the states full-data OPD visits that a query set's rollouts reach. A single query already reaches \(71.5\%\), most of it within the first 100 steps. Adding semantically distinct queries raises coverage and validation accuracy together, until 16 queries reach \(98.9\%\) and match full-data training. Yet alignment slows at a similar pace whether OPD trains on one query or the whole dataset, and even a fixed set of states takes hundreds of steps to absorb. OPD is therefore data-overfed but algorithm-starved. Its rollouts quickly expose broad supervision, while the student absorbs that supervision increasingly slowly. The state-coverage result extends to multi-teacher OPD, where 16 semantically diverse queries per domain match full-data MOPD. As a further stress test, content-light templates and off-domain WildChat queries also approach the real-query baseline. Task content and induced state coverage can therefore come apart. We hope these findings direct future work toward the step efficiency of OPD, and prompt a re-examination of the data and the mechanisms behind its recent successes in frontier post-training.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD结合学生rollout与密集教师反馈，已有研究更多关注算法而忽略数据作用。本文在只有一个训练问题的极端设置研究它：单问题仍能持续学习数百步，恢复多数全数据OPD收益，跨任务和模型家族成立。作者从状态覆盖与对齐速度解释：一个问题到达全数据rollout语义聚类的71.5%，多数在前100步出现，16个语义多样问题达到98.9%并匹配全数据；但无论一个问题或全数据，对齐速度都逐步变慢，固定状态也要数百步学习。多教师每域16题同样匹配全数据，少内容模板和域外WildChat也有效。结果提示应研究状态生成与监督吸收效率，而不只增加问题数量。

#### 研究背景

“一题”是一个固定输入，但每步64条新回答可产生数万带教师反馈的前缀，绝不是一个固定训练样本。文章同时区分恢复教师能力差距和恢复全数据训练收益，两者分母不同。它将prompt视为访问有用状态的生成器。

#### Method

重复同一问题采样，学生在实际前缀接受教师监督；首组数学实验用学生top16截断反馈，机制组改用采样词元，因此两组绝对分数不同。状态用教师最后层向量表征，全数据轨迹抽8个位置，经PCA、200簇K-means定义参考空间，覆盖为被访问簇比例 $\operatorname{Cov}(S)=|\{c(s):s\in S\}|/200$，不按访问频率加权。

对齐距离取已采词元的绝对log-ratio均值 $d_t$，吸收率定义为 $(d_t-d_{t+1})/d_t$，后期用窗口平滑。另固定初始64条轨迹反复训练、改变问题数与多教师域数，区分新状态供给与优化速度。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.04172)，正文及状态统计、训练、数据极简附录。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

第9节明确状态覆盖是基于全数据语义参考空间的代理，忽略簇访问频率和剩余教师信号。究竟什么决定吸收率仍未解答；MOPD只测三域各一教师。数据选择若需先跑全数据才能估覆盖，尚不能直接形成低成本筛选器。

#### 我的分析：优点

把不同问题数与新轨迹数分开，极端一题设置有助隔离数据与算法。固定状态、域外模板、多家族和多域实验相互补充，说明现有教师监督能从较少输入触发。明确机制实验与主数学实验反馈估计器不同，避免错误拼数。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

高语义簇覆盖不证明覆盖任务所需全部信息，更不证明簇覆盖导致性能。吸收率随自身rollout分布变化，可能混合状态变易和真实参数学习；相似下降趋势不足以证明算法是唯一瓶颈。少题节省数据构造，但数百步rollout、教师和长回答仍昂贵。不能由若干低数据设置推导数据质量或任务内容普遍无关，也不能外推到新知识学习及所有师生配对。

### 3. MINERVA: How Small Can a Manipulation Policy Be and Still Solve LIBERO?

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03715
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03715
- Authors: Kohei Sendai, Tatsuya Matsushima, Yusuke Iwasawa
- 作者单位：Matsuo-Iwasawa Lab, The University of Tokyo。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-03
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-03
- Categories: cs.RO

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

历史相关性复核：旧规则将 manipulation policy 的词尾误匹配为 on policy。本文研究 LIBERO 的紧凑策略容量、回归与 flow-matching，对蒸馏仅作部署方向讨论，不满足 OPD/OPSD 严格摘要收录条件；保留原条目供追溯。

#### Abstract 原文

> Vision-language-action (VLA) models with billions of parameters now dominate the LIBERO manipulation benchmark, but the model capacity actually required by the benchmark remains unclear. We introduce MINERVA (MINimal Efficient Robotic Vision-Action policy), a family of deliberately compact visuomotor policies designed to measure this task-specific capacity floor. A 0.54M-parameter policy achieves 95.1% average success over 2,000 rollouts on the four standard LIBERO suites, only 2.4 points below the reported LeRobot $π_{0.5}$ result despite using 7,700$\times$ fewer parameters. Performance saturates near 1M parameters and collapses below 0.25M. Across broad architectural, training, and inference sweeps, only action-chunk length and vision capacity consistently exceed a $\pm$1-point training-seed band. Flow matching provides no detectable advantage over direct L1 regression across three seeds, while regression is up to 3.8$\times$ faster on GPU. A task-ID permutation probe shows that standard LIBERO instruction conditioning primarily selects among memorized tasks: changing only the task-ID mapping reduces success to near chance. The same recipe achieves 94.6% success across 89 LIBERO-90 tasks, while LIBERO-Plus perturbations reduce performance to 46--56%, with near-zero robustness to photometric shifts. The 0.54M policy replans every control step in 5--9 ms per chunk on a laptop CPU, 113$\times$ faster than SmolVLA and 1,400$\times$ faster than $π_{0.5}$, without a GPU. These results establish a first empirical estimate of LIBERO's task-specific capacity floor and motivate capacity-aware design and distillation for deployment-efficient robot policies.

#### 摘要中文翻译

大型VLA在LIBERO操控基准上接近饱和，但固定任务集到底需要多少模型容量尚不清楚。MINERVA逐步缩小极简视觉动作策略：0.54M参数在四套标准LIBERO共2000次rollout达到95.1%，比文献报告的4.1B pi0.5低2.4点；约1M后饱和，低于0.25M明显崩溃。三种子消融中，flow matching不显著优于直接L1回归，后者GPU最多快3.8倍。仅改任务ID映射就使成功率接近随机，显示该固定基准主要考验任务选择与已知动作记忆。LIBERO-90单种子扩展94.6%，LIBERO-Plus扰动则降到46%–56%，光照鲁棒性接近零。0.54M可在笔记本CPU以5–9ms输出action chunk。

#### 研究背景

标准LIBERO重复训练中的场景、任务与指令，大语言理解和开放词表能力未必被充分测试。本文主动去掉语言模块，用task ID研究封闭任务集合的部署容量。其蒸馏是示范数据上的速度匹配，不是LLM on-policy distillation，属于当前OPD日报的外围/误收文献。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03715)第III–VI节。两相机共用从零训练的轻量CNN，task embedding用FiLM调制；视觉关键点、8维本体状态与两帧历史拼接，动作head生成长度16的chunk。flow matching目标

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm FM}=\mathbb E\|v_\theta(a^\tau,\tau,c)-(a-\epsilon)\|^2,\quad a^\tau=\tau a+(1-\tau)\epsilon.
$$

较小模型还匹配冻结大模型在同$(a^\tau,\tau,c)$的velocity，权重1；这些状态来自离线示范插值，不是当前策略环境rollout。对照将head换成一次前向L1回归。部署每控制步重规划，对覆盖当前时刻的历史chunk按年龄指数平均；子1M模型噪声温度0.85。小模型不支持新任务或语言指令推理。

第VI节明确任务ID无法处理未训练任务；结果是封闭任务的测量工具，不是通用VLA。headline模型、LIBERO-90及部分蒸馏对照单种子，一点内差异可能落在种子波动中。仅一个模拟器和评测seed，真实机器人验证未做，光照外观变化鲁棒性很弱。

#### 我的分析：优点

把标准基准成绩与开放泛化分开，用task-ID置换和扰动测试直接探查模型依赖。L1与flow三种子比较及同硬件延迟测量具有实用价值，避免将复杂生成head的必要性当成默认前提。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

0.54M只是所测架构与训练协议的经验容量点，不是任务信息论最小模型下界。与数千倍大VLA的比较使用不同评测样本数，且大模型额外能力没有被固定任务基准衡量。该文不提供on-policy LLM蒸馏实验，应从OPD核心方法列表单独标为任务容量/机器人压缩参考。

### 4. FlashRender: Few-Step Generative Rendering via Camera-Controlled Video MeanFlow

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03563
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.03563
- Authors: Byeongjun Park, Byung-Hoon Kim, Hyungjin Chung
- 作者单位：EverEx；Yonsei University；Korea University。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-03
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-03
- Categories: cs.CV

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要同时包含 on-policy/on policy 与 distillation/self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> We present FlashRender, a few-step generative rendering framework that retakes a source video along a target camera trajectory in seconds. We identify sampling-step-dependent camera control as a prominent manifestation of discretization error in existing multi-step generative rendering models and show that resolving this inconsistency substantially lowers denoising trajectory curvature, facilitating subsequent step distillation. To this end, we introduce Representation Transformation and Alignment (RETA), which aligns hidden source-video representations with target-video features from a frozen visual geometry model. This directly encodes the geometric transformation within the source-video stream, enabling sampling-step-consistent camera control. We then fine-tune the model with the MeanFlow objective on the lower-curvature denoising trajectory induced by RETA, allowing the model to more effectively address discretization error. Finally, we apply on-policy flow map distillation to correct self-rollout errors under fixed few-step sampling. Extensive experiments show that RETA, MeanFlow, and on-policy flow map distillation play complementary roles in few-step generative rendering. Together, they enable our approach to match multi-step baselines in video quality and geometric consistency at 25x lower sampling cost while achieving superior camera controllability, even under out-of-distribution target camera trajectories.

#### 摘要中文翻译

FlashRender是少步生成式重渲染框架，可沿目标相机轨迹重新拍摄源视频。作者指出现有多步模型的相机控制随采样步数变化，是离散化误差的重要表现；修正这种不一致可降低去噪曲率，为后续步数蒸馏创造条件。RETA把中间源视频表征对齐到冻结视觉几何模型的目标视角特征，直接编码几何变换。随后用MeanFlow学习平均速度，最后做在策略flow-map蒸馏纠正固定少步自采样误差。实验中仅4次网络评估即可接近多步视频质量与几何一致性，采样评估次数低25倍，相机控制更好，并可推广到分布外目标轨迹。

#### 研究背景

减少去噪步数不仅损害画质，还可能改变实际相机运动，所以直接对任意多步底座蒸馏未必有效。FlashRender先稳定几何对应，再训练少步映射，最后纠正自身路径分布。本文的OPD是连续潜变量分布匹配，不是语言词元蒸馏。

#### Method

第一阶段从Wan2.1-1.3B-CamCtrl初始化，把源到目标逐帧相对pose注入源视频流，两流用目标相机条件的共享位置编码；RETA用3D卷积投影中间源特征，对齐冻结VGGT目标视频特征的余弦方向。第二阶段保留RETA，MeanFlow预测区间平均速度，以有限差分替代昂贵JVP，并把CFG烘焙进目标。

第三阶段学生按固定四步时间表自生成，第一阶段模型作真实分数教师，另训fake-score模型，以DMD差梯度指导学生，加入系数0.03的真实/生成特征判别损失：$\mathcal L_3=\mathcal L_{\rm DMD}+0.03\mathcal L_{\rm GAN}$。第三阶段不再使用RETA损失，纠正的是少步自rollout分布。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03563)，正文及训练和阶段消融附录。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

正文没有独立Limitations。第三阶段明确只针对固定四步，而非任意采样步数；比较的运行时间仅包含视频生成部分。作者指出外部几何估计误差影响显式方法，本方法推理仍需已知或估计的源相机轨迹；对遮挡和未观察区域的生成仍依赖预训练先验。

#### 我的分析：优点

从相机控制的步数不一致入手，解释为何先几何适配再少步蒸馏，比只比较画质更具体。将同蒸馏流程应用其他底座、替换RETA及跳过阶段的对照，有助判断性能来自哪里。几何一致性与相机误差并报，避免只优化通用视频分数。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

曲率降低与更好蒸馏相关，未证明其是唯一因果机制，RETA还改变表征和条件质量。25倍是评估次数，表内时间又排除预处理，不能直接说完整重渲染25倍。训练约两周、多辅助网络，适合模型发布阶段而非低算力快速适配。DAVIS固定相机轨迹集及无多种子误差限制对任意真实镜头和长视频的泛化。
