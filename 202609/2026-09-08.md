### 1. Video-MOPD: Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Video Understanding

- Authors: Zhenxin Qin, Peng Shi, Cong Han, Yinlong Qian, Zequn Jie, Lin Ma
- 作者单位：Tongji University；Bilibili Inc.。

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Video understanding demands a convergence of complementary capabilities across perception, temporal understanding, and complex reasoning, which are difficult to jointly optimize within a single model. We introduce Video-MOPD-8B, an open-weight model dedicated to video understanding tasks. To fundamentally enhance its capabilities, we conduct targeted reinforcement learning (RL) optimization across three core domains: video temporal grounding (VTG), general video comprehension, and video STEM reasoning. We then unify their complementary capabilities via Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD), which consolidates expert knowledge by supervising student-generated trajectories with routed teacher feedback. We further introduce Reliability-Aware Informative Sampling (RAIS), which selects examples with consistently reliable teacher supervision and large teacher-student performance gaps. Together, these components enable Video-MOPD-8B to achieve coordinated and comprehensive performance gains across diverse video understanding tasks. Extensive experiments on comprehensive benchmarks covering general video understanding, temporal grounding, video reasoning, and video STEM tasks demonstrate that Video-MOPD-8B achieves state-of-the-art performance among existing models at a comparable scale. The trained model weights are available at https://huggingface.co/LandH/Video-MOPD-8B.

#### 摘要中文翻译

视频理解需要感知、时间理解与复杂推理等互补能力，难以在单一目标下联合优化。本文提出Video-MOPD-8B，先对视频定位、一般视频理解及视频STEM推理分别做目标RL训练，再以域路由多教师OPD把专家能力合并到一个学生。作者还提出RAIS，选教师多次正确且师生表现差距较大的实例，以兼顾监督可靠性和学习空间。综合评估显示，单一8B模型在多个视频任务协调提升，最终无需专家或额外路由部署。

#### 研究背景

三类任务的反馈不同：定位强调时间边界，视频理解强调跨帧信息，STEM还依赖OCR和图表等单帧能力。STEM专家完全用静态图像训练，后续检验其能力能否转移视频。RAIS是离线师生多采样筛选，不是每步动态根据当前学生刷新。

#### Method

三个专家均从Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct出发：一般视频GRPO用正确性和格式奖励；定位复现TimeLens2的SFT加GRPO、时间IoU和Wasserstein奖励；STEM在Orsta47K及ViRL39K图像上做DAPO。RAIS对每候选从域教师与初始学生各采 $K$ 次，只保留教师全部正确者，再优先选 $\Delta(x)=1-\operatorname{Acc}_S(x)$ 大的实例。

三域近似平衡混合，学生每题生成一条轨迹，按数据域确定唯一教师，在同一前缀上用采样词元RKL反馈更新同一语言模型；视觉编码器和aligner冻结。教师仅训练期参与。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09300)，正文及配置、混合数据和token支持附录。

从同一底座建立三个专家并用参数平均对照，能直接看整合而非参数规模收益。静态STEM到视频的路线有明确能力拆分；最终域路由只训练使用，部署结构简单。附录给出主训练两轮与支持消融一轮，便于区分协议。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

### 2. TV-Regulated OPD: Direction Matters in On-Policy Distillation

- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08341
- Authors: Han Xiao, Yifan Niu, Dongyi Liu, Chang Luo, Jia Li
- 作者单位：The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)；The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology；The University of Edinburgh。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-08
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-08

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-Policy Distillation (OPD) facilitates the transfer of knowledge from domain expert to student in the post-training phase of Large Language Models (LLMs). However, the supervision signals in mainstream OPD methods suffer from high variance and noise which is generally instable during training. In this work, we systematically investigated what really matters to the performance and the fundamental mechanisms behind the instability during training. We found that retaining only the sign of token-level advantages is sufficient to achieve the performance comparable to standard OPD. Meanwhile, smoother and bounded advantages can stabilize the training process without sacrificing its performance. These motivated us to shape the advantages using the Total Variation (TV) and propose a robust TV regulated On-Policy Distillation (TV-OPD) method. Benefiting from the bounded and diminished advantages, TV-OPD exhibits stable training dynamics and steady late-stage performance. We conducted comprehensive experiments and found that, across various settings, TV-OPD consistently achieved better performance and lower variance in the late-stage of training.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD把领域专家知识转给学生，但标准log-ratio监督高方差且可能不稳定。本文分离词元优势的方向、相对大小和序列尺度，发现仅保留符号仍可得到接近普通OPD的表现，平滑有界幅度有助稳定。作者据此提出TV-OPD：保留词元方向，用总变差距离调节共享更新强度，随着师生接近而衰减。在被测师生设置中，方法保持较稳的后期表现并改善平均成绩。

#### 研究背景

优势 $A_t=\log p_T(y_t)-\log p_S(y_t)$ 可写为符号乘词元相对幅度再乘序列尺度。本文通过压平或打乱相对幅度、再删除序列尺度，检验幅度是否必要。这里不再精确优化原RKL代理，而转向固定前缀下与TV有关的有界方向信号。

#### Method

固定前缀下，采学生词元的符号策略梯度期望对应 $-2\nabla D_{\rm TV}(p_T,p_S)$。已有log-prob还能给有界TV估计

$$
\hat d_t=[1-e^{A_t}]_+,\qquad \mathbb E_{y_t\sim p_S}\hat d_t=D_{\rm TV}(p_T,p_S).
$$

跨有效词元和worker汇总后做EMA。下一步共享系数 $c_k=\operatorname{clip}[(\bar D_{k-1}/D_{\rm ref})^\alpha,c_{\min},1]$，$D_{\rm ref}$ 为首次有效估计，用 $c_k\operatorname{sign}(A_t)$ 替换PPO式优势。历史统计避免当前样本与尺度直接耦合；有变长和掩码时总体TV均值是比率估计，有限批不一定无偏。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08341)，正文及TV估计、选择协议和训练曲线附录。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

#### 我的分析：优点

### 3. NeoHorse-1: Towards Recursive Self-Improvement via Agentic Post-Training with Routing Harness

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08183
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08183
- Authors: NeoHorse Team, Guoliang Cao, Guohao Dai, Tianyu Guo, Kai Han, Hailin Hu, Zihan Jiang, Xiang Kuang, Boxun Li, Yulong Li, Zehua Pei, Yuchuan Tian, Jiamin Wang, Yu Wang, Yunhe Wang, Yihong Wu, Haiyang Xu, Shuo Zhang, Hang Zhou, Siyang Cheng, Jiayu Fan, Wei He, Qingrui Jiao, Hongguang Li, Zhiyuan Li, Runke Liu, Xi Liu, Xinchen Liu, Sinno Jialin Pan, Yi Ren, Liuyang Song, Chenyu Wang, Bei Yu, Quanlu Zhang, Xiangyu Zhang, Mengyu Zheng, Yingjie Zong
- 作者单位：TokenRhythm Technologies；Infinigence AI；Tsinghua University；Peking University；The Chinese University of Hong Kong；Visionplus Capital；WX Capital；Alibaba Group。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-08

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Recursive self-improvement (RSI) requires a concrete mechanism through which an AI system observes its capabilities and converts that evidence into the next round of learning. We present NeoHorse-1, a family of agent-native models developed to explore this path through agentic post-training. Our system combines a heterogeneous model pool with intelligent routing, recording the predicted capability demand, selected service tier, and subsequent interaction for each user turn. These records are converted into training examples that preserve interleaved reasoning, tool calls, and harness context, and are admitted through structural validation, six-dimensional semantic evaluation, and subscene-level labeling. Routing signals organize supervised fine-tuning into a three-stage curriculum and extend to routing-guided on-policy distillation, where a teacher supervises student-generated responses under the same progression. Capability-guided allocation then converts evaluation feedback into the next training mixture, closing an evaluation-selection-update loop in which what the system learns to do shapes what it learns from next. Across eleven benchmarks covering harness-based agents, tool use, coding, and instruction following, post-training raises the macro-average from 58.94 to 64.87 at 4B and from 65.60 to 69.04 at 9B, substantially narrowing the aggregate gap between the post-trained 4B model and the 9B base model. NeoHorse-1 provides an initial prototype of this feedback-driven process and a path toward harness-mediated RSI across successive iterations.

#### 摘要中文翻译

递归自我改进需要系统观察自身能力，并把证据转成下一轮学习。NeoHorse-1探索路由harness已有的反馈机制：异构模型池记录每个user turn的能力需求预测、服务层级和实际交互，转为保留推理、工具调用与环境上下文的训练样本，经结构校验、六维语义质量评价和subscene标注进入学习。路由分数安排三阶段SFT课程，并扩展为同课程的OPD；能力评估再调整下一轮数据配比。十项智能体、工具、代码和指令基准中，4B宏平均58.94→64.87，9B65.60→69.04。作者将其定位为反馈闭环初始原型，长期递归收益仍待验证。

#### 研究背景

真实agent记录含工具失败、恢复、任务修改和终止证据，通常比静态问答更丰富。但服务时选了强模型可能受价格、可用性或用户覆盖影响，不能直接当难度标签。本文区分需求预测、策略调整和实际服务三层记录，再用需求重估做课程。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08183)第3–6节及贡献者机构表。轨迹、user turn和共享局部目标的subscene分层组织；保留历史可见回复与工具交互，去掉更早turn的reasoning，只监督当前assistant的推理、tool call、可见回复与结束标记。结构闭合与六维质量分开，缺失评估不当成功。

路由C0–C3分数以硬层级或$\sum_{k=0}^3k\pi_{i,k}$排序，分三阶段逐步加入高需求样本，后段仍保留部分简单数据；不直接重权SFT loss。OPD从记录的assistant前上下文出发，学生生成新回复，固定teacher同前缀评分，以学生top-$K$加tail bin的reverse KL逐回答平均：

$$
\mathcal L=\frac1{\sum_rw_r}\sum_r\frac{w_r}{L_r}\sum_tD_{\rm KL}(\widetilde P_{r,t}\|\widetilde Q_{r,t}).
$$

按同课程调度起始上下文，后续学生checkpoint刷新。该生成是记录上下文中的回复，不等于每条历史都由当前学生重新执行。评估缺陷profile指导下一轮混合，而非增加单独失败loss。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

结论明确只验证一次evaluation–selection–update循环，尚不能说明连续多轮收益累积。主要评估agent、编码、工具与指令，harness服务的其他能力未全面验证。路由tier是相对能力需求代理，并不等于实际服务模型身份或逐动作难度。

#### 我的分析：优点

把真实执行上下文、路由决策与结果串成数据接口，比泛称“数据飞轮”具体。结构事实与语义评估覆盖分开，有助于避免把完成运行当成任务成功。与Toucan同课程对照说明真实harness数据的贡献，而非只列模型榜单。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

主结果是完整数据与后训练配方，缺少独立routing课程、OPD阶段和能力再分配的充分消融，不能归因任何单组件。未报告长程多代迭代，RSI目前是设计与一次原型，不是已证实自动持续改进。精确teacher、训练超参数和算力细节不足，复现成本与泛化需依赖公开manifest和代码补齐。

### 4. Miles v0.1: Production-Level Post-Training

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08368
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08368

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

#### Abstract 原文

> We present Miles v0.1, a full-stack, production-ready system for frontier post-training. Building upon the clean design of slime, Miles designs each stage of the reinforcement-learning (RL) training loop around a single principle: components should be verified, clean, and customizable. With accuracy, efficiency, reliability, and scalability as first-class goals, Miles aims to make frontier-scale RL accessible to researchers and enterprises alike. This report walks through the system end to end: rollout engines built on SGLang, a trainer with a choice of two backends (NVIDIA Megatron-LM and PyTorch FSDP), and three weight-synchronization transports for different deployment topologies. Beyond full-parameter RL, Miles also supports LoRA RL, on-policy distillation, supervised fine-tuning, and true-on-policy rollout-training alignment, and extends the same architecture to diffusion models. We close with an end-to-end case study: fully asynchronous agentic RL on a GLM-5.2 744B-A40B model over terminal-use coding tasks, running on 64 NVIDIA GB300 GPUs with a median step time of 263 seconds over the first 30 measured steps. #### 摘要中文翻译

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08368)第2–9节，以下为报告版本功能。异步模式分离rollout与trainer GPU池，按已完成trajectory group取数据；sample级补充并发，队列bounded且按组中最旧token的权重版本计算staleness。TITO服务保存原始token ID/log-prob，后续只tokenize追加后缀，减少消息重渲染改变训练轨迹；MoE routing replay进一步固定专家路由。

权重同步共享分桶准备，再选broadcast、RDMA P2P或磁盘delta；单机P2P可能更慢。优化器状态可按bucket流式存取本地磁盘，以I/O换HBM；恢复受相同parallel layout限制。低精度需checkpoint、训练前向、serving及export遵守一致量化路径。

OPD将冻结师生sampled-token reverse-KL信号减到advantage中，可与GRPO/PPO任务奖励组合；served teacher可异构架构但要求同tokenizer，in-process teacher要求同架构，top-k仅served路径。所谓true-on-policy profile通过确定性/批次不变内核让两引擎在已采token的log-prob一致，不消除旧权重rollout造成的策略滞后。

### 5. Eliciting Weak-to-Strong Generalization with On-Policy Reverse Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08798
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08798
- Authors: Youngrok Park, Sangmin Bae, Hojung Jung, Jongwoo Ko, Yunseon Choi, Young Jin Kim, Pashmina Cameron, Aaron Courville, Se-Young Yun
- 作者单位：KAIST AI；Microsoft；University of Toronto；Mila；Université de Montréal；CIFAR AI Chair。依据HTML首页。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-08
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-08
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要同时包含 on-policy/on policy 与 distillation/self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Weak-to-strong generalization asks whether stronger models can learn from weaker supervisors and surpass them. This question is particularly important for successive model generations and multi-domain consolidation, where repeating frontier-scale post-training from scratch can be prohibitively expensive. Yet conventional distillation treats the weak teacher as an optimization target, potentially imposing its capacity ceiling on the student. We introduce On-Policy Reverse Distillation (OPRD), which evaluates the teacher's policy shift relative to its reference policy on student rollouts and amplifies the component of the student's verifier-driven policy gradient along that direction. By rescaling only verifier-supported updates, OPRD preserves the stationary points of policy optimization while accelerating learning beyond the teacher. In both successive model transfer and multi-teacher distillation, OPRD achieves higher performance with fewer student updates than existing RL and distillation approaches. Response-style analysis shows that OPRD students remain closer to models trained with verifier-based RL alone than to their weak teachers, suggesting that teacher guidance accelerates rather than redirects the student's own optimization. Results in conventional strong-to-weak distillation further demonstrate that OPRD effectively combines verifier-driven policy optimization with teacher guidance regardless of capacity ordering.

#### 摘要中文翻译

弱到强泛化研究更强模型能否利用较弱监督并超过监督者。传统蒸馏把弱教师作为优化目标，可能限制学生。On-Policy Reverse Distillation（OPRD）在学生rollout上计算教师相对其训练前参考策略的变化方向，并放大学生验证奖励梯度沿该方向的分量，而非让学生模仿教师分布。逐代模型迁移与多教师整合实验中，它用更少学生更新取得更高成绩；学生响应风格更接近纯RL模型而非弱教师，强到弱设置也有收益。其“保持驻点”分析是在固定响应的logit空间中成立，不能直接当作共享模型参数或期望任务奖励的全局保证。

#### 研究背景

小模型的后训练变化可能提供有用方向，但其最终策略也包含容量限制和风格偏好。本文将教师作用从“目标分布”改成“对学生自身奖励梯度做定向缩放”，让程序化verifier继续决定学生优化信号。

#### Method

教师后训练logits减去其参考checkpoint logits并均值中心化，得到 $\Delta_t=\mathcal C(z_T-z_T^{ref})$，归一化为 $d_t$。对学生采样token的RL梯度 $g_t=A_t\nabla_{z_t}\log\pi_S(y_t\mid h_t)$，执行

$$
\widetilde g_t=g_t+\lambda_t(d_t^\top g_t)d_t.
$$

沿教师方向的分量放大 $1+\lambda_t$，正交部分不变。若点积为负，放大的是**反向离开教师**的、已有verifier支持的分量，并不是改为跟随教师。默认 $\lambda_+=0.5$，负分支由0逐步升至0.5；数学warm-up30更新，Reasoning Gym75更新。实际只在采样token与学生top-10的并集上构造修正。

固定响应、固定advantage和方向时，$I+\lambda dd^\top$正定可逆，所以logit梯度为零的点不变，且 $g^\top\widetilde g=\|g\|^2+\lambda(d^\top g)^2$。附录A进一步给出直接更新独立logits且步长足够小时的提升下界。实际将修正logit梯度经网络Jacobian回传、再由AdamW更新共享参数，与该直接logit更新不同。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

### 6. Distillation as Probability Transport: Routed On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.08337
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.08337
- Authors: Tianle Xia, Lingxiang Hu, Yiding Sun, Linfang Shang, Ming Xu, Lan Xu, Ning Zheng, Wei Xu, Jie Jiang
- 作者单位：Tencent。依据文首作者邮箱及机构文字；HTML作者区将部分姓名误放在Affiliation字段，不能将这些姓名当作单位。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-08
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-08
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers teacher knowledge on student-generated trajectories, but efficient sampled objectives reduce the teacher distribution to scalar credit on individual tokens. Such credit indicates whether a token should gain or lose probability, yet leaves the corresponding redistribution unspecified. We recast OPD as teacher-guided probability transport and propose RouteOPD (Routed On-Policy Distillation), which decomposes local teacher--student disagreement into student-excess sources and teacher-deficit destinations and couples them into explicit transport pairs. RouteOPD optimizes pairwise log-odds toward jointly realizable targets obtained from a bounded teacher potential, while adapting the transport budget to the concentration of teacher demand. This formulation directs updates toward teacher-preferred destinations and controls their magnitude within a single transport operator. Experiments across four teacher--student settings and four mathematical-reasoning benchmarks demonstrate that RouteOPD consistently outperforms sampled reverse-KL OPD, with improvements accompanied by higher routing fidelity and lower background leakage. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of explicitly modeling probability transport in on-policy distillation.

#### 摘要中文翻译

采样式OPD把教师分布简化为某个token的标量信用，说明该token该增减多少，却不显式指定概率应转到哪里。RouteOPD将局部师生差异拆为学生过量的源token和教师欠量的目标token，并构造显式配对；通过共享有界教师势函数给出可同时实现的成对log-odds目标，再按教师需求集中度调节更新预算。四种师生设置、四个数学基准上，它优于采样reverse-KL及完整词表KL，伴随更高路由一致性和更低无关概率泄漏。

#### 研究背景

单个采样token的log-prob梯度为 $e_a-p_S$，压低它时会把直接logit更新分散到学生本来就偏好的其他token。本文不否认该估计在期望中的KL解释，而是改变每一次实现的更新，显式指定teacher-preferred目的地。

#### Method

在学生与教师top-32并集上分别归一化概率，定义过量 $e=[p_S-p_T]_+$、欠量 $d=[p_T-p_S]_+$，两者质量相同 $M=\sum e=\sum d$。从 $q^-=e/M$ 与 $q^+=d/M$ 独立采样默认两对源—目的地，不求稠密最优传输计划。

对pair $(a,b)$，让相对旧学生的log-odds变化 $\Delta(a,b)$ 拟合教师目标。因为

$$
\nabla_z[\log p(b)-\log p(a)]=e_b-e_a,
$$

直接logit梯度只作用于两端。先计算教师势 $g(v)=\log p_T(v)-\log p_S(v)$，再用 $\phi_B(g)=\frac B2\tanh(2g/B)$ 限幅，目标为 $D(a,b)=\phi_B(g(b))-\phi_B(g(a))$。共享势使所有pair目标满足环一致性，避免各边独立clip导致互相矛盾。

预算 $B$ 按欠量分布的归一化Herfindahl集中度，在 $\log1.2$ 和 $\log1.5$ 间插值。损失为 $M\mathbb E[h_\kappa(\Delta-D)]$，默认Huber $\kappa=1$；routing、旧策略及目标均detach。未相关logit无直接梯度不等于其概率或共享参数完全不变。
