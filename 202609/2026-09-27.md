### 1. What Shared Prefixes Hide: Trajectory Dropout for On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33455
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33455
- Authors: Zizhuo Lin, Quanling Liu, Yi Yang, Yawei Luo
- 作者单位：Zhejiang University。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-29
- Categories: cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student model on its own trajectories using dense token-level feedback from a stronger teacher model. Since each update is conditioned on the reasoning prefix already generated by the student, the prefix also shapes how effectively teacher feedback is converted into learning. We find that shared prefixes can lead to weak token-level updates, a phenomenon we call Prefix-Induced Supervision Attenuation (PISA). This attenuation arises in two common cases. (i) High student confidence can weaken corrective gradients even when the teacher disagrees. (ii) Tokens that rely on earlier reasoning can receive learning signals as weak as those for simple local continuations. To solve this problem, we propose Trajectory Dropout, a simple training-time intervention that exposes these weakened signals. The student first performs a standard full-context rollout to generate a complete trajectory. During training, we randomly drop a certain proportion of the student's reasoning trajectory, while the teacher continues to observe the complete trajectory for token-level supervision. This intervention strengthens corrections for overconfident predictions and introduces additional supervision at prefix-sensitive positions. Trajectory Dropout consistently improves average performance across teacher--student model pairs of different scales and six mathematical reasoning benchmarks, while also yielding gains on two out-of-domain benchmarks. It can also be flexibly integrated into existing OPD variants with negligible computational overhead, further improving their performance. These results demonstrate that Trajectory Dropout provides a simple mechanism for strengthening token-level supervision across model scales and OPD objectives.

#### 摘要中文翻译

OPD在学生自身轨迹上提供教师反馈，但共享推理前缀也会影响反馈转成学习的强度。本文提出Prefix-Induced Supervision Attenuation（PISA）：学生过度自信时即使教师不同意也可能梯度很小；依赖早先推理的token也可能与简单局部续写一样只有很弱监督。Trajectory Dropout先用完整上下文生成正常学生轨迹，再在训练时随机屏蔽部分学生推理历史，教师仍看完整前缀。该干预强化过度自信预测的纠正，并在前缀敏感位置产生额外监督，在六数学基准、不同师生规模和两个域外任务上获益，也可接到现有OPD变体且开销很小。

#### 研究背景

token级更新是师生log-ratio优势与学生log-prob敏感度的乘积，任一接近零都会弱化反馈。文章改变学生优化时看到的历史，而不改生成轨迹和教师目标，试图让完整上下文下“已经会续写”的位置变成需要学习的信息恢复任务。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33455)正文及附录B–F。完整rollout后选择1–8段response source token，总长度20%。对于段$[a,b)$，从$b$之后的目标位置禁止直接attention到这些source；段内部因果注意力保留，prompt不遮、所有response目标都监督。可选局部保护窗口延后屏蔽生效，默认窗口0。

教师保持完整前缀概率$p_t$，旧/新学生用同一个保存mask计算$r_{\rm old,t},r_{\theta,t}$，训练surrogate为

$$
L_{\rm TD}=-\frac1N\sum_t\frac{r_{\theta,t}}{r_{\rm old,t}}\operatorname{sg}[\log p_t-\log r_{\theta,t}].
$$

原始动作仍由完整上下文学生采样，所以这是跨视图训练目标，并非masked-view真实rollout的无偏OPD。教师不赞同而学生置信度趋近1时，普通own-logit纠正$(1-q_t)\log(q_t/p_t)$趋近0，屏蔽可改变优势与score向量两者，实证需要两者一致使用masked view。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未单列limitations。消融明确过多dropout会降性能，逐token屏蔽若破坏紧邻上下文会严重不稳定，加入短保护窗口才能恢复。同模型teacher没有带来改善，说明dropout不能替代有效教师监督。一步机制验证以teacher-alignment KL为结果，并非完整训练任务奖励保证。

#### 我的分析：优点

生成、教师评分和学生优化视图三个接口明确，避免将mask误施加到真实rollout或目标token。配对梯度范数控制排除了纯步长变大的部分解释，单token与span/保护窗对照定位了局部连贯性的重要性。实际2.8%开销有测量支撑。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

制造更大KL或梯度不自动创造有益知识，训练学生被迫在缺失信息下匹配完整上下文教师，有不可预测目标的风险。屏蔽direct attention不等于彻底消除历史信息，其他保留token可能间接携带它。共享前缀的低信号有时正是合理匹配，不能普遍称缺陷；收益仍需依赖任务评测、教师质量及mask实现。

### 2. UOPD: Uncertainty-Aware Intervention for On-Policy Distillation of Multi-Turn Agents

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34036
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34036
- Authors: Wenbo Zhang, Pengcheng Xu, Weizhi Du, Jing Zhang, Hengrui Cai
- 作者单位：University of California, Irvine；University of Michigan, Ann Arbor。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student on its own rollouts using dense supervision from a teacher. In multi-turn environments, a mistake at a critical decision step can redirect the subsequent rollout toward poor outcomes. We use low teacher confidence on student actions to select high-uncertainty steps for correction. In a controlled ALFWorld study, a single teacher correction at a low-confidence step improves subsequent student behavior and task success, motivating selective intervention during distillation. We propose UOPD, an uncertainty-aware intervention method for on-policy distillation. At low-uncertainty turns, UOPD executes student actions and applies the standard OPD loss. At high-uncertainty turns, it samples and executes teacher actions and trains the student to imitate them through supervised fine-tuning, which minimizes forward Kullback-Leibler divergence in expectation. UOPD utilizes adaptive uncertainty thresholds to target a scheduled intervention rate. Empirically, we evaluate UOPD across a broad range of agentic tasks, including ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search, demonstrating its superior performance over OPD methods and their variants. UOPD improves WebShop score by up to $15.8\%$ relative to standard OPD.

#### 摘要中文翻译

多轮OPD中，关键动作错误会把后续轨迹带向差结果。本文用教师对学生动作的低置信度识别高不确定步骤，受控ALFWorld实验显示只修正一次此类动作，也能改善之后学生自主行为与成功率。UOPD在低不确定turn执行学生动作并做普通OPD，高不确定turn采样并执行教师动作，再通过SFT模仿这些动作；后者在教师采样期望下对应forward KL。自适应分位阈值跟踪预定干预率。ALFWorld、WebShop、Search实验中改善性能，WebShop最高较OPD提升15.8%相对任务得分。

#### 研究背景

教师给已执行错误动作打低分，无法改变这次rollout已经进入的坏状态。直接替换动作可改善后续数据，但需要控制教师接管量，并对来自教师分布的动作使用合适损失。本文把“执行纠正”与“学习纠正”分开检验。

#### Method

来源：[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34036)正文及附录A–F。学生每turn先提动作，由教师置信度或师生归一log-prob差评分；近期学生动作得分buffer的$(1-r_n)$分位作为阈值，每episode开始更新、episode内固定。目标干预率随batch从初值线性降至末值。

超过阈值时丢弃原提议、采样并执行教师动作，其后仍让学生提下一动作。损失按动作来源切换：未接管位置采用学生sampled-token OPD，接管位置用$-\beta\log\pi_\theta(a_T|h)$。教师动作SFT的期望为教师熵加forward KL，因此不能不分来源套用student-sampled reverse-KL surrogate。

理论给出辅助执行策略与原学生回报差等于所选纠正的学生续行$Q$优势和；只有所选教师动作平均优势至少$\gamma>0$时，才能保证回报增加。置信度阈值本身没有证明满足该假设。另用三动作构造展示：教师总体更好时，reverse KL单步仍可能降低奖励，SFT可提高奖励；这是存在性例子，不是普遍散度排序。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

未单列limitations。结论将更长时域与按任务难度、学生进展自适应干预预算留作后续。理论明确平均可恢复性假设，不要求每次纠正更好；实验承认高干预率可能降低Unseen和WebShop表现，且训练比普通OPD有额外成本。

#### 我的分析：优点

固定模型的一次干预实验、随机同预算选择、去SFT与不执行纠正消融，清楚区分何时改动作、是否真的改环境状态、是否学习教师动作。动作来源决定监督方向，避免直接把教师轨迹当学生分布样本。真实时间对照解释了相对FTB的效率来源。

#### 我的分析：缺点 / 风险

教师低概率可能来自合法不同策略或措辞，不必是有害动作；理论优势假设并未由该代理自动满足。混合行为改变状态分布，未接管动作也经过置信筛选，局部OPD项不等于原学生全分布无偏目标。主表评测种子不是训练种子，额外教师生成和阈值调度对更长环境的成本还需测量。

### 3. Teach Yourself Where to Look: On-Policy Attention Self-Distillation for Reasoning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33200
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33200
- Authors: Safaeid Hossain Arib, Rabeya Akter, Ismam Nur Swapnil, Md. Faiyaz Abdullah Sayeedi, Tasnim Mohiuddin, Md Mofijul Islam
- 作者单位：ACI PLC（孟加拉国）；University of Dhaka；BRAC University；QCRI（卡塔尔）；Amazon GenAI（美国）。首页注明Amazon作者相关工作在其公司职责之外完成。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-29
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy self-distillation trains reasoning models on their own trajectories using dense token distribution guidance from a privileged teacher with access to a verified solution. This supervision transfers what the teacher predicts without directly transferring where it attends within the preceding context. We introduce On-Policy Attention Self-Distillation (OPASD), which complements token-level supervision with solution-conditioned attention distillation. Because the privileged teacher can attend to verified solution tokens unavailable to the student, OPASD projects teacher attention onto student-visible positions and renormalizes the resulting distribution before alignment. Across three model sizes and four competition-level mathematics benchmarks, OPASD consistently outperforms token-only OPSD, improving average accuracy by 4.98 to 8.40 percentage points. OPASD also avoids the response-length inflation and performance degradation observed with token-only distillation, reducing generated rollout tokens by 73.9% and estimated model compute by 72.6% while training 1.53x faster. These results show that solution-conditioned attention provides a complementary supervision signal that makes on-policy self-distillation more accurate, stable, and compute-efficient.

#### 摘要中文翻译

On-policy self-distillation利用可见验证解答的特权教师，在学生自身轨迹上提供稠密token分布监督，但只传递教师预测什么，不直接传递它关注前文哪里。OPASD在token监督之外加入解答条件化的attention蒸馏。教师能够关注学生看不到的参考解答token，因此方法先把教师attention投影到学生可见位置并重新归一化，再与学生对齐。三个模型尺度、四个竞赛数学基准上，平均准确率较token-only OPSD提高4.98—8.40个百分点。同时，所测训练中避免了OPSD的响应增长和性能退化，rollout token减少73.9%、估计模型计算减少72.6%，墙钟加速1.53倍。结果支持解答条件attention能补充输出分布监督。

#### 研究背景

推理后段需要回看题目约束和前面的中间结果，输出词表匹配没有直接约束这些注意位置。难点是师生上下文不同：教师多出参考解答，不能要求学生模仿对不存在位置的注意力。本文解决这个支持集错位，然后检验额外监督是否改善结果和训练轨迹。

#### Method

学生仅用问题生成响应，冻结初始模型作为教师，同时读取问题、参考解答和同一学生前缀。教师与学生最后一层attention先对所有head平均。设学生可见支持集为 $V_S$，教师独有参考位置为 $\mathcal P$，取教师在可见位置的条件分布：

$$
\widetilde A_T(i)=\frac{A_T(i)}{\sum_{j\in V_S}A_T(j)},\qquad i\in V_S.
$$

该操作保留学生可见位置之间的attention比例；虽然删去参考位置，它仍由阅读了完整参考解答的教师计算，因此仍带有特权条件影响。用对称JSD对齐 $\widetilde A_T$ 与 $A_S$，与原token forward-KL相加：$L=L_{tok}+0.5L_{attn}$。仅学生接收梯度，推理时移除教师与解答。

附录O证明，在有限支持、可见质量为正、$0<\beta<1$ 的广义JSD下，条件归一化分布是可见支持上到完整教师attention的唯一最优分布。该定理证明的是attention目标的投影性质，不是推理正确性或训练收益保证。主实现仅蒸馏最后一层；token KL采用每位置整体裁剪，阈值0.05。

#### 论文自我评价的缺陷

### 4. SeOPD: Self-Evolving LLMs via Online Policy Distillation from Self-Generated Chain-of-Thought

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33181
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33181
- Authors: Xiaoshu Chen, Xiangyu Wong, Sihang Zhou, Ke Liang, Xinwang Liu
- 作者单位：National University of Defense Technology；Peking University。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27
- Categories: cs.AI, cs.CL

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要包含 OPD/OPSD 缩写，并同时具备 distillation 与 policy/LLM/RL 上下文。

#### Abstract 原文

> Recent advances in online policy self-distillation (OPSD) have demonstrated that large language models (LLMs) can improve their capabilities by leveraging external privileged information (PI), such as manual annotations or feedback from external environments. However, obtaining accurate annotations and constructing sophisticated environments often require substantial human effort and computation, limiting the scalability of OPSD. While a few recent studies have explored self-improvement without external PI, the resulting gains remain limited. In this work, we explore whether LLMs can achieve comparable self-improvement without external PI. Our key observation is that a single LLM can support multiple reasoning modes, such as deep-thinking and non-thinking modes, with deep thinking generating additional information during reasoning. Based on this observation, we propose Self-Evolving Online Policy Distillation (SeOPD), which enables LLMs to distill and internalize information generated by their own chain of thought (CoT). Specifically, it (1) generates CoT with the deep-thinking mode, (2) produces responses with the non-thinking mode, and (3) uses the generated CoT as PI to provide token-level supervision for the non-thinking response, allowing new information inferred during reasoning to guide the non-thinking mode and be internalized into the shared model parameters, thereby improving both non-thinking and deep-thinking capabilities. Extensive experiments across LLMs and tasks demonstrate the effectiveness of SeOPD.

#### 摘要中文翻译

既有在线自蒸馏常需标注、技能或环境反馈等外部特权信息，成本限制扩展。SeOPD利用同一模型的thinking与non-thinking模式：thinking生成CoT，non-thinking独立产生回答，再把CoT作为教师额外上下文，对non-thinking回答提供token监督，将推理时生成的信息内化到共享参数。作者认为这不仅能改善快速回答，也能改善thinking能力，跨科学问答、工具调用和代码实验支持两模式总体提升。

#### 研究背景

更长推理可以暴露基础模型直接回答时未可靠表达的信息，但这些推理并非新的外部事实来源，也可能错误。本文研究将自身推理当训练特权，并加入固定参考正则，防止两模式共同训练后thinking退化成直接回答。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33181)正文及附录A。每题在线生成独立的学生回答$y\sim\pi_\theta^S(\cdot|x)$和thinking轨迹$c\sim\pi_\theta^T(\cdot|x)$。teacher带$x,c,y_{<t}$评分，student只带$x,y_{<t}$；论文使用对称KL

$$
\mathcal L_{\rm OPD}=\tfrac12D_{\rm KL}(q\|p)+\tfrac12D_{\rm KL}(p\|q),\qquad
\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm OPD}+\beta\mathcal L_{\rm CoT}.
$$

CoT正则在自生成CoT前缀上约束当前thinking分布相对训练前冻结reference的reverse KL，默认$\beta=0.01$。实现采用top-100蒸馏支持，师生模式共享参数，CoT与student回答不断重新生成。该目标是对称KL，不是JSD；教师条件包含完整自生成推理，部署时可分别使用两种模式。

#### 摘要中文翻译

从头晶体生成（DNG）模型以同一组权重决定成分搜索分布和结构生成方式。作者主张将两者分开：晶体结构预测（CSP）应作为物理先验，用似然训练改善；包括相对搜索历史的新颖性在内的奖励，应作用于成分搜索。ANCHOR是在冻结CSP外围训练的多目标GRPO成分策略，配合连续自适应新颖性CAN，度量相对已知结构和增长中的发现历史的新颖程度。在同一CSP和内部评估器下，替换DNG成分为ANCHOR成分使MSUN从11.4%升至47.6%、SUN从1.1%升至22.1%，成分唯一性99.9%。直接对DNG或CSP做奖励微调未获得同样提升，而将搜索得到的结构加入似然训练可改善部分CSP骨干。MatterGen评估下MSUN为41.3%，再训练Crystalite-CSP后47.1%；但on-hull比例明显依赖使用的势能模型。

#### 研究背景

**本篇是GRPO材料成分搜索与离线似然训练，不属于OPD/OPSD；历史脚本将“composition policy”的词尾误匹配为“on policy”，再与distillation联立而误收。** 此处保留真实全文笔记，不把它改写成语言模型OPD工作。

发现新晶体的目标分布不等于训练数据中常见成分的分布。奖励若直接微调联合生成器，会同时改变“去哪类化学成分搜索”和“给定成分如何生成结构”，而搜索历史只改变某结构再次采样的价值，不改变其物理合理性。论文用冻结结构生成器作对照，分离成分策略与结构质量的贡献。

#### Method

将联合生成分解为 $p(c,x)=p(c)p(x\mid c)$，ANCHOR采用

$$
p_{\mathrm{ANCHOR}}(c,x)=\pi_\theta(c)p_{\mathrm{CSP}}(x\mid c),
$$

只更新成分策略。动作决定元素数量、元素身份、晶胞原子数和化学计量；冻结CSP生成结构，经势能模型与BFGS松弛后评分。奖励含稳定性、结构/成分多样性、CAN和随同一简化化学式访问次数衰减的count bonus；每组从Dirichlet采样奖励权重，并采样允许元素子集，在同一权重与元素集合下计算GRPO相对优势。

CAN将松弛结构表示为归一化径向分布函数直方图，只与相同简化化学式的结构比较。分别对固定MP-20库和本次搜索历史计算近邻RBF距离，取两者较小值；评分后加入历史，从而重复发现不再持续获得高新颖奖励。count bonus针对重复成分，CAN针对重复结构，两者并非同一指标。

分析给出联合KL的分解：

$$
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q(c,x)\|p_0(c,x))=
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q(c)\|p_0(c))+
\mathbb E_{q(c)}D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q(x\mid c)\|p_0(x\mid c)).
$$

因此同一个KL惩罚同时限制成分迁移和结构漂移；对有界奖励，理想KL正则解的成分重加权满足 $q^*(c)/p_0(c)\le e^{\Delta R/\beta}$。冻结条件生成器可直接消除结构漂移这一项。另一个独立阶段把搜索得到的结构加入MP-20，按原似然目标训练CSP；这不是在学生前缀上查询教师的OPD。

### 6. Leaky Students: Membership Inference against On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33136
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33136
- Authors: Zhexi Lu, Mingzhi Zhu, Stacy Patterson, Lei Yu
- 作者单位：Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.CR

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student to match a teacher's next-token distributions on student-generated trajectories. However, privileged information supplied to the teacher for OPD training may contain sensitive data. Whether the student leaks private information about the records supplied to the teacher during distillation remains poorly understood. To the best of our knowledge, we present the first systematic study of membership inference in this setting. We find that fresh student trajectories expose sparse membership signals that fixed reference-answer losses often miss. These signals are mixed with probability changes caused by training on other records. We introduce Leaky, which samples fresh trajectories from the target model and compares its token log-probabilities with the maximum across matched reference models trained without the candidate records. It applies Leaky ReLU to the resulting gaps, preserving positive gaps and downweighting negative gaps as an approximate correction for incidental positive gaps in non-members. Across fifteen targets spanning mathematics, medical question answering, and code generation, Leaky outperforms all evaluated baselines and achieves mean AUROC 0.875, compared with 0.614 for the strongest baseline on each target in the main evaluation. #### 摘要中文翻译

OPD在学生自生成轨迹上匹配教师下一词元分布，而教师的特权信息可能含敏感记录。学生是否会泄漏这些记录曾用于蒸馏训练，尚不充分清楚。本文系统研究成员推断，发现新采样学生轨迹暴露稀疏成员信号，而固定参考答案损失常看不出。这些变化还混有其他记录训练产生的共享效应。作者提出Leaky，将目标模型词元log-prob与排除候选记录训练的多个参考模型逐词元最大值比较，再用Leaky ReLU保留正差、压低负差。数学、医疗问答和代码共15目标上平均AUROC为0.875，主比较最强基线0.614；让基线也用相同生成轨迹时最强均值0.826。结果说明不向学生输入参考答案不等于不会泄漏训练成员身份。

#### 研究背景

目标是判断已知记录 $(x,r)$ 是否参与OPD，不是从模型恢复未知病历或答案。主威胁模型假设可查询条件词元概率，且已知初始化模型、教师、训练配置并有同域辅助数据；目标权重和原训练轨迹不可见。它是有较强背景知识的参考模型审计。

#### Method

对每条候选用目标在问题及参考答案条件下生成8条新轨迹；评分时移除参考答案，只用问题和前缀。另训练8个排除候选的参考模型，构造

$$
d_{i,t}=\log p_T(y_{i,t}\mid x,y_{i,<t})-\max_k\log p_{S_k}(y_{i,t}\mid x,y_{i,<t}),\quad
S(z)=\frac1N\sum_i\frac1{L_i}\sum_t([d_{i,t}]_+-\alpha[-d_{i,t}]_+).
$$

默认负斜率0.04。最大参考与整流共同作用，单独对最大参考差取平均会受参考间变异影响。文本接口扩展通过前缀查询的贪心匹配指示替代log-prob；仍有较多查询及参考训练。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33136)，正文及威胁模型、消融、隐私效用附录。

### 7. Hesitation-Aware On-Policy Distillation for Diffusion Language Models

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33301
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33301
- Authors: Jianguo Huang, Lipeng Wan, Yanchen Deng, Bo An
- 作者单位：Nanyang Technological University；Xi’an Jiaotong University。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27
- Categories: cs.CL, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Diffusion large language models (dLLMs) generate text by iterative unmasking. At each denoising step, a dLLM proposes a token at every masked position, but the decoder commits only a confident subset of these proposals. Trace-based on-policy distillation (TOPD) builds on this process by matching the student to a stronger teacher, yet only at the committed positions. We argue that this discards much of the useful signal, which resides in the uncommitted proposals, where the student has made a prediction but is not yet confident enough to commit it. We call these proposals hesitations. In our pilot study on an SDAR-4B student, hesitations make up only 24% of supervisable state-position pairs but carry 66% of the teacher-student divergence. To exploit this signal, we propose Hesitation-Aware On-Policy Distillation (HOPD), which extends teacher distribution matching to every masked position of each denoising step. Because hesitations are not equally informative, we further allocate supervision using hindsight from the completed trajectory, placing more weight on positions whose proposal was later disagreed with the final token and on blocks where first-step proposals rarely survive. Since both models already produce distributions at all masked positions, HOPD requires no additional forward passes over TOPD. The only extra cost is evaluating the loss at more positions. With SDAR-1.7B and SDAR-4B students distilled from TraDo-8B-Instruct, HOPD achieves the best average score among the evaluated methods on five math and coding benchmarks, under both static and dynamic decoding and at both scales. It also speeds up decoding. On SDAR-4B, the HOPD student hesitates less and commits 11% more tokens per denoising step than TOPD, while reaching higher accuracy.

#### 摘要中文翻译

扩散语言模型逐步解除掩码，每步对全部未解码位置提议词元，但只提交其中高置信部分。TOPD只监督已提交位置，忽略尚未提交的“犹豫”提议。SDAR-4B诊断中，这类状态位置对只占24%，却携带66%师生分歧。本文提出HOPD，在每个学生访问状态的全部掩码位置匹配教师分布，并用完整轨迹事后信息分配权重：经常改口的位置、首步提议较少存活的块得到更多监督。模型本已输出所有掩码位置分布，所以不新增模型前向，仅计算更多位置损失。两个SDAR规模、五个数学代码基准和静态动态解码下平均表现最佳；4B每去噪步比TOPD提交约11%更多词元，准确率同时较高。

#### 研究背景

扩散模型一个状态可以同时预测许多位置，已提交词元只是最后一次提议。只沿提交轨迹监督会遗漏模型尚未确定的中间预测。HOPD并不把学生最后答案当真值，而用它衡量提议稳定性，真正目标仍是教师在同一状态的分布。

#### Method

学生动态解码记录每步pre-action状态。位置改口次数 $e_j$ 是此前提议与最终提交词元不同的次数，块首步保留比例为 $\rho_b$；取 $w_j=1+e_j$、$w_b=\max(1-\rho_b,0.1)$。在全部掩码位置上计算

$$
\mathcal L=\mathbb E_\xi\sum_t w_{b(t)}\frac1{|\mathcal M(s_t)|}\sum_{j\in\mathcal M(s_t)}w_jD_{\rm KL}(\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid s_t,j)\|\pi_T(\cdot\mid s_t,j)).
$$

使用全词表RKL，减少每位置采样估计噪声；轨迹在一轮后续更新中固定，下一round同步学生后重新生成。推理规则不变，收益来自更早达到提交阈值。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33301)，正文及配置、全词表TOPD和权重消融附录。

### 8. Fisher-Informed Recalibration for Feedback-Based On-Policy Self-Distillation of LLMs

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34009
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.34009
- Authors: Seohyun Lee, Dong-Jun Han, Seyyedali Hosseinalipour, Christopher G. Brinton
- 作者单位：Purdue University；Yonsei University；University at Buffalo-SUNY。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27
- Categories: cs.LG

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy self-distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Feedback-based on-policy self-distillation has emerged as a promising approach for enabling foundation models, more specifically Large Language Models (LLMs), to learn from their own outputs under external feedback, with a single model serving as both teacher and student. However, such methods can exhibit unstable optimization, conducive to performance collapse during training. To address this limitation, we propose FIRE (Fisher-Informed REcalibration), a dual-branch framework that recalibrates the supervision applied to correct and incorrect on-policy outputs during fine-tuning. For correct responses, FIRE replaces self-distillation with re-weighted on-policy SFT, while for incorrect ones FIRE identifies feedback components that disproportionately influence the teacher-induced update and recalibrates the feedback-conditioned target accordingly. Both branches are influenced by a token-level radius derived in part from a softmax Fisher trace. FIRE separates which direction feedback should move the model from how far the model should move in that direction, while leaving well-behaved feedback supervision unchanged. Our experiments demonstrate that FIRE provides substantially more stable self-distillation while maintaining strong downstream performance, particularly in settings where standard feedback-conditioned distillation becomes unstable.

#### 摘要中文翻译

反馈式在策略自蒸馏让同一模型在收到外部反馈后作为教师，但训练可能不稳定乃至性能崩溃。本文提出FIRE，分别校准正确和错误轨迹的监督：正确回答用重加权在线SFT，错误回答则分析各反馈组成对教师诱导更新的影响，重新构造反馈目标。两支共享由softmax Fisher迹和学习率确定的词元半径，将反馈方向与更新幅度分开；原监督在半径内时保持不变。实验表明该方法在普通反馈蒸馏不稳定时，更能保持训练稳定并取得较好下游表现。

#### 研究背景

一个反馈字段很有影响，不意味着它一定有害。FIRE先判断完整反馈诱导的logit梯度是否超预算，再按逐字段删除影响进行校准；因此不是按概率差一概删强信号。Fisher迹刻画当前softmax分布离散度，用于输出梯度尺度，区别于完整参数Fisher或自然梯度优化。

#### Method

当前学生采一条轨迹并验证对错，EMA教师在原轨迹上用四个结构化反馈字段评分。定义学生分布 $p$，半径

$$
\rho_t=\frac{\sqrt{1-\|p_t\|_2^2}}{\max(1,a_k/a_0)}.
$$

正确支对CE权重取 $\min(1,\rho_t/\|p_t-e_{y_t}\|)$。错误支除完整反馈教师外，再做四个leave-one-field-out前向，测反向KL的logit梯度差能量。只有完整梯度能量超出 $\rho_t^2$，才按影响能量对完整与删字段教师做几何平均，得到 $q_A$；再以 $q_F\propto p^{1-\eta}q_A^\eta$、$\eta=\min(1,\rho_t/\|g(q_A)\|)$ 径向收缩。因为该几何插值线性缩放局部RKL logit梯度，最后满足输出梯度半径。

归因、权重和目标均停止梯度，实际仅更新学生LoRA，之后EMA更新教师。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34009)，正文及消融、超参、反馈模板、推导附录。

### 9. DuoOPD: Learning from Joint Teacher-Student Outcomes for Multi-Task On-Policy Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33711
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33711
- Authors: Ao Yu, Weibo Gao, Heng Zhou, Linan Yue, Rui Li, Suyi Liu, Yu Yan, Yizhong Zhang, Qi Liu
- 作者单位：所读公开HTML版本未列作者或作者单位，无法从该版本确认。

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) trains a student on its own responses with token-level feedback from a stronger teacher, yet the teacher can fail on questions the student already answers correctly, and how often each model succeeds varies across tasks. OPD ignores these outcomes and, on average, pushes down even the student's correct responses; gating feedback by student correctness fixes the direction but uses the teacher in the same way whether or not it succeeded. We introduce DuoOPD, in which the student's outcome sets the direction of feedback and the joint teacher-student outcome decides how the teacher supports it: when only the teacher succeeds, its verified answer becomes context for scoring the student's failed response, and when only the student succeeds, a weight shared within the task reinforces the whole response. Across Qwen3 and Llama, DuoOPD outperforms all five baselines in mean macro accuracy, improving over OPD by 2.58 and 5.98 percentage points, and it also leads on two further task mixtures spanning scientific calculation, instruction following, and code generation. #### 摘要中文翻译

OPD通过更强教师对学生自身响应提供逐token反馈，但教师可能在学生已答对的问题上失败，且师生成功比例随任务改变。普通OPD忽略这些结果，其反馈平均上甚至会压低学生正确响应；按学生正误门控可调整方向，却仍以相同方式使用成功或失败的教师。DuoOPD让学生结果决定反馈正负，让联合师生结果决定教师如何支持该反馈：仅教师成功时，把其验证通过的响应加入教师评分上下文；仅学生成功时，用任务内共享权重强化整条响应。一个规则覆盖四种正误组合，无需各任务专属超参数。Qwen3和Llama的主实验Macro较OPD分别提高2.58和5.98个百分点，在另外两种包含科学计算、指令遵循和代码的任务混合上也领先。消融显示，仅校正方向的收益有限，主要增益来自两种师生不一致情况的处理。

#### 研究背景

多任务环境中教师并非每题都比学生强；“教师总体更强”无法决定某一具体响应该加强还是抑制。本文利用训练期答案、指令检查器和代码测试，同时验证缓存教师响应与当前学生响应，再决定评分上下文和反馈分配。

#### Method

每个训练问题预先缓存并验证一条独立教师响应，训练时保持不变。令 $d_0=\log\pi_T(y_t\mid x,y_{<t})-\log\pi_S(y_t\mid x,y_{<t})$，$\operatorname{sp}(z)=\log(1+e^z)$；$d_T$仅将验证正确的教师响应加入教师上下文，学生输入不变。四种规则为：

| 教师结果 | 学生结果 | 每token权重 | 含义 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 正确 | 正确 | $\operatorname{sp}(d_0)$ | 全部为正，教师偏好调整强化幅度 |
| 错误 | 错误 | $-\operatorname{sp}(-d_0)$ | 全部为负，教师偏好调整抑制幅度 |
| 正确 | 错误 | $-\operatorname{sp}(-d_T)$ | 用正确教师参考指导错误轨迹的负向反馈 |
| 错误 | 正确 | $\bar m_k>0$ | 当前完整rollout batch内，同任务此类成功响应共享一个正权重 |

共享权重先对每条响应的 $\operatorname{sp}(d_0)$ 取token平均，再对该任务中“师错生对”响应平均；因此保留任务内平均尺度，同时移除失败教师对响应内部的权重差异。该尺度仍来自教师log-ratio，并非完全脱离教师。

最终损失 $-B^{-1}\sum_iT_i^{-1}\sum_t\operatorname{sg}(A_{i,t})\log\pi_\theta(y_{i,t}\mid x_i,y_{i,<t})$。权重在更新前固定；实现的policy ratio当前数值为1，单epoch单minibatch下剪裁不激活，梯度与上述式子相同。Softplus相对正误门控增加了非零反馈，但这是有验证反馈参与的新目标，不再是原始reverse-KL梯度的无偏估计。

### 10. Dual-Vocabulary Language Model for Cross-Tokenizer Distillation

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33816
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33816
- Authors: Kedi Chen, Chen Lin, Yutao Sun, Wei Zhang
- 作者单位：East China Normal University；Shanghai Innovation Institute；Tsinghua University。
- arXiv published: 2026-09-27
- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27
- Categories: cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) bridges teacher supervision and student behavior, but different teacher-student tokenizers introduce misalignment in both input tokenization (#1) and output logits (#2). Existing approaches address the former by matching same-text spans or converting tokens to bytes, often losing fine-grained token information or disrupting the native-token paradigm, while for the latter, strategies such as ranking, padding, or key-token selection retain only shared logit dimensions, resulting in much distribution loss. In this paper, we propose Dual-Vocabulary Language Model (DVLM), which replaces the teacher's LM head with a new student-vocabulary projection head and obtains full-dimensional student logits (for #2). To support student tokens (for #1), it takes a Parallel-Tokenized Sequence (PTS) as input, which concatenates the original teacher-tokenized sequence and a re-tokenized sequence formed by independently converting each student token into a teacher-token group. To avoid inference inconsistency with the original teacher tokens, the Hybrid-Prefix Attention (HPA) further restricts re-tokenized groups to their corresponding teacher prefix and uses its last state as the aggregation of the original student-token representation for projection into the student vocabulary space. Similarly, via the combined use of PTS and HPA, the DVLM teacher can provide distribution-aligned supervision with the student's input-tokenization and output-logit during OPD. Experimental results demonstrate that our DVLM teacher has a similar converged loss as the original teacher model and enables student models to improve performance across six reasoning tasks.

#### 摘要中文翻译

跨tokenizer OPD同时面临输入切词边界和输出logit词表不同。现有方法用文本段、字节或部分共享维度建立对齐，可能丢失细粒度分布信息。DVLM为冻结教师骨干训练新的学生词表LM head，直接输出完整学生词表分布。输入PTS拼接教师原生token序列与每个学生token单独转成的教师token组；HPA限制重切词组只看到匹配的教师前缀和组内既往token，避免未来泄露，取每组最后状态投影到学生词表。实验中改造教师的loss收敛接近原教师，并提升两个学生在六个推理任务的表现。

#### 研究背景

字节对齐可以避免硬词表匹配，但通常仍需决定如何分配概率。DVLM选择提前付出适配教师输出头的训练成本，保留教师骨干，之后以学生原生token空间执行完整分布蒸馏。适配成本可在同学生tokenizer的多次训练间复用，但每种学生词表需要对应head。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33816)正文及附录A。教师token序列为$\tau_T(x)$；每个学生token$s_i$独立解码再按教师tokenizer切成$e_i$。PTS为$[\tau_T(x);e_1;\ldots;e_n]$。先贪心找文本相同的teacher/student对齐组，HPA使原教师序列保持因果注意力，重切词token可见更早对齐组的原生教师token、当前对齐组更早学生token的展开，以及自身组内前缀，不能看未来。

每个$e_i$末位置隐藏态经$W_D$预测下一个学生token。共享token从原head复制，不共享项用展开教师token权重均值初始化；Stage I只更新新head。Stage II在学生新rollout上采用相同PTS/HPA抽取对齐logit，再最小化

$$
\mathcal L=\frac1{L_R}\sum_iD_{\mathrm{KL}}(p_D(\cdot|y_{<i})\|p_S(\cdot|y_{<i})).
$$

因此得到维度一致的forward KL，但教师目标是学习后的DVLM分布，并非原teacher联合文本分布的严格无损投影。训练和读取均需正确处理next-token位置偏移。

### 11. Do We Really Need KL Divergence for On-Policy Distillation of Large Language Models?

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33791
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33791
- Authors: Wenze Lin, Jiyuan Long, Jiale Zhao, Shenzhi Wang, Xitai Jiang, Ce Luo, Rui Lan, Qianli Ma, Fukang Wen, Hui Wu, Liyuan Chen, Shuoling Liu, Jiangpeng Yan, Gao Huang
- 作者单位：LeapLab、Qiuzhen College，Tsinghua University；Beihang University；National University of Singapore；The Chinese University of Hong Kong；E Fund Management Co., Ltd.。

- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27
- Categories: cs.LG, cs.AI

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Since the advent of knowledge distillation, KL divergence has been the standard loss in distillation. Recently, on-policy distillation (OPD) has emerged as an efficient post-training paradigm for LLMs. As a distillation method, OPD naturally inherits KL divergence as its standard loss. However, in this work, we find that KL divergence may not be necessary for OPD. We show that simply preserving the update direction is sufficient for effective OPD. As long as the update direction is toward the teacher, OPD works. More precisely, it is not the direction of every token, but the direction of a small subset of tokens where the teacher and student disagree strongly. We first show that simply assigning a reward of (+1) to tokens where the teacher probability is higher than the student probability and (-1) where it is lower, which merely encourages updates toward the teacher, reproduces almost the same training mode as OPD with reverse KL. We further show that only the direction of a small subset of tokens with large teacher-student disagreement is critical, and training works as long as their update direction is toward the teacher, even if other tokens are pulled away from the teacher. And as an application of these findings, we introduce Consensus Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (C-MOPD) to improve Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD). Unlike MOPD, which routes each sample to a single teacher and may cause capability conflicts across domains, C-MOPD lets every sample be supervised by all teachers. Experiments show that C-MOPD consistently outperforms MOPD on both math and code benchmarks. Our code is available at https://github.com/LeapLabTHU/KL-Free-OPD.

#### 摘要中文翻译

KL是知识蒸馏的标准目标，但本文发现OPD未必需要其幅度信息，只保留教师方向也能有效训练。对教师概率高于学生的采样词元给+1，低于学生给-1，即可得到与反向KL类似的训练趋势与成绩。更进一步，关键是少量师生强分歧词元的方向，其他低分歧词元即使部分反向也未严重破坏训练。作者据此提出共识多教师C-MOPD：每条学生回答让所有教师评分，同向时按共识更新，轻微冲突时允许沿域专家方向，强冲突时不更新。数学和代码实验优于域路由MOPD，提示更简单的方向监督值得研究。

#### 研究背景

BinaryOPD把log-ratio优势量化成符号，而不是不需要教师概率；教师仍必须对学生采样词元给出概率。作者把成功归因于大量近似重复状态上的持续反馈，但状态相似诊断尚不构成普遍理论充分条件。

#### Method

令 $\ell_t=\log[\pi_T(y_t\mid h_t)/\pi_S(y_t\mid h_t)]$，BinaryOPD使用 $r_t=\operatorname{sign}(\ell_t)$ 的策略梯度。按阈值0.8分成A（教师明显更高）、B（低分歧）、C（学生明显更高），分别只对部分组给统一正或负奖励，测试哪些方向敏感。

C-MOPD对每个采样词元计算所有教师的 $\ell_k$：全部同正或同负时采用该符号；冲突时采用域教师符号 $d$，但要求其他教师反向分歧均小于 $\epsilon$，否则给0。默认 $\epsilon=0.8$。这对齐的是采样词元概率方向，不能保证完整参数梯度同时改善所有域。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33791)，正文及高分歧、评估、冗余状态附录。

### 12. Dense Is Not Enough: Hierarchical Supervision Allocation for Long-Horizon On-Policy Distillation

- Authors: Yuhao Sun, Binrui Wu, Zhuoer Xu, Ming Wen, Haoxiang Xu, Bin Chen, Yan Lin, Qianzijing Zhang

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers the capabilities of a large language model to a smaller student by providing teacher supervision on the student's own rollouts. These stages are nested: each finer allocation is conditioned on the coarser decision, rather than being optimized as an independent importance score. Experiments across multiple long-horizon agent benchmarks and student-teacher configurations show that LENS-OPD consistently improves task performance over vanilla OPD and strong curriculum- and selection-based baselines. #### 摘要中文翻译

OPD在学生轨迹上给密集教师监督，但长程智能体任务中，局部分歧大未必改善未来，有用指引也可能超出学生当前能力或无法在移除提示后保留。本文将其表述为分层监督分配，提出LENS-OPD的定位、验证、细化三阶段。定位按能力调整轨迹深度并提议干预轮；验证测试同一学生收到提示后未来行为是否改善；细化把有效提示诱导行为蒸馏到无提示策略，并关注该轮中明确的师生冲突词元。各层相互嵌套，实验在多个长程智能体基准和师生配置上改善普通OPD，说明监督深度、轮次和词元均需选择。

#### 研究背景

作者在286次有效分支中发现，局部KL与后续收益关联弱，37%的提示不产生正收益，最大KL轮也仅约三分之一时候是最高收益轮。所谓未来收益实际用教师对后续动作/内容的长度平均对数似然衡量，不是环境任务成功的直接差值；应把它视作可计算代理。

#### Method

当前最深轮测学生对教师top-1词元相对自身top-1的支持比例，按进入该深度时的差距与当前差距之比调节课程时钟，速率限制在0.5–1.5。每轨迹选平均KL最大的一个轮次，从相同恢复状态用同一冻结学生分别进行无提示与一句教师提示的三轮续写。若 $\Delta=V(\xi^H)-V(\xi^B)>0$ 才接受。

被接受轮有两种训练：提示学生生成的回答，用提示分布对无提示学生做前向KL；原始学生回答则按学生抵触程度与教师置信度乘积加权普通OPD，轮内重新归一保持平均权重。整体为 $\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm focus}+\lambda_{\rm GI}\mathcal L_{\rm GI}$。未接受轮保留原OPD权重。来源：[arXiv 官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33409)，正文及算法、训练和评估附录。

### 13. ChemOPD: Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Multi-Task Chemical Reasoning

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33838
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33838
- Authors: Yaoyao Xu, Xinjian Zhao, Xiaozhuang Song, Xuemin Chen, Tianshu Yu
- 作者单位：School of Data Science, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen；Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory。依据HTML首页。

- arXiv updated: 2026-09-27

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Large language models are increasingly expected to support diverse chemical reasoning capabilities within a unified model. One approach is to develop specialized capabilities separately and consolidate them through multi-teacher on-policy distillation, but this raises two questions: how should specialization be organized, and how should specialist guidance be integrated? We introduce ChemOPD, which addresses both. We estimate task affinities from supervised fine-tuning gradients and solve a constrained mixed-integer program(MIP) to construct partially overlapping specialist groups. During distillation, we retain a generalist teacher trained on all tasks so that specialist guidance supplements rather than replaces its supervision. Our anchor-residual objective gradually increases the routed specialist's contribution on student-generated responses. On ChemCoTBench, affinity-guided specialization produces task-dependent gains over the generalist teacher and improves several capabilities beyond semantic task grouping. Yet stronger teacher-side performance does not automatically yield stronger students: with the same specialists and routes, anchor-residual OPD improves most reported metrics over specialist-only distillation and realizes a larger share of the available teacher gains. #### 摘要中文翻译

统一化学模型需要支持多种化学推理能力。先发展专长再通过多教师OPD整合是一条途径，但存在两个独立问题：如何组织专长训练，以及如何将专家指导传给学生。ChemOPD先从SFT梯度估计任务亲和性，通过带约束的混合整数规划构造部分重叠的专家任务组；蒸馏时保留全任务通用教师，使专家信号补充而不是替代通用监督。其anchor-residual目标在学生生成响应上逐步增加路由专家的贡献。ChemCoTBench实验显示，亲和性分组能在部分任务上超过通用教师及语义分组，但教师更强不自动意味着学生获益；使用相同专家和路由时，渐进混合在多数指标上优于纯专家蒸馏，恢复更大比例的教师提升。论文因此区分了专长形成与能力整合两项设计问题。

#### 研究背景

化学编辑、结构理解、性质优化和反应推理语义类别不同，但学习关系可能跨类别共享。某任务参与专家训练，也不意味着最终必须由该专家指导它。另一方面，专家在任务上有更高分数，并不保证其在学生所访问前缀上提供更有效监督；学生还需要通用能力作为锚点。

#### Method

在Qwen3-8B-Base初始化处，对每任务取互不重叠的100条训练probe和50条验证probe，计算最后两层梯度，排除LM head，再以共享8192维CountSketch压缩。源任务梯度与目标验证梯度的内积是一阶局部loss变化代理；实际分组用方向余弦，标准化有向非对角项后构造对称兼容度 $W_{ij}=S_{ij}+S_{ji}$。

以 $x_{ig}$ 表示任务是否属于专家组，枚举可行组大小后求解：

$$
\max_x\sum_g\frac1{k_g}\sum_{i<j}W_{ij}x_{ig}x_{jg}
-\lambda\sum_{i,g}x_{ig},\qquad
\sum_i x_{ig}=k_g,\quad1\le\sum_gx_{ig}\le q_{\max}.
$$

约束确保任务覆盖、限制组大小与重叠。最终为8/8/6个任务的三组，LogP与溶解度同时参与反应和优化专家训练，但OPD时只路由给优化专家。使用的是求解器返回的较好可行方案，不声称唯一全局最优。

每个学生前缀取学生top-16候选，支持集内归一化的学生概率乘完整softmax下师生log-prob差，形成detached advantage。通用教师与当前固定路由专家在同一前缀、候选上评分：

$$
\widehat A_{\mathrm{ChemOPD}}(s)=\widehat A_A+
\alpha(s)(\widehat A_g-\widehat A_A).
$$

前65%更新中将 $\alpha$ 从0线性升至0.5，之后保持。该式是两种advantage的凸组合，不等于独立证明专家残差代表可迁移新知识。实现为PPO风格surrogate，但每次一个actor epoch、一个逻辑minibatch，形成更新时ratio为1，剪裁数值不激活，局部梯度就是advantage加权score-function。部署只保留统一学生。

### 14. Beyond Timestamps: Decision-Aligned On-Policy Distillation for Long-Horizon Agents

- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33391
- PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.33391
- Authors: Mingju Chen, Can Lv, Jinrong Liu, Huan Zhang, Heng Chang, Shiji Zhou
- 作者单位：Beihang University，School of Artificial Intelligence、Beijing Advanced Innovation Center for Future Blockchain and Privacy Computing；Tsinghua University。

#### 与 OPD / OPSD 的相关性依据

标题或摘要中明确出现强相关短语：on-policy distillation。

#### Abstract 原文

> Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) often relies on sparse outcome rewards, providing coarse supervision for long-horizon agents. On-policy self-distillation (OPSD) complements this signal with dense privileged feedback. However, we identify \emph{Decision--Timestamp Mismatch}: privileged guidance may be misaligned with the student's functional decision because the corresponding decision can occur at a different timestep, while the student's decision itself may span multiple timesteps rather than being tied to a single timestamp. Thus, timestamp-local supervision can misalign both the context and the temporal scope of credit. To address this mismatch, we introduce \textsc{AlignOPSD}, following the principle of aligning supervision before assigning credit. Decision-Aligned Supervision Rectification re-scores the same student-sampled response in functionally matched contexts across sibling rollouts to calibrate local teacher evidence. Semi-Markov Hierarchical Credit Assignment then derives variable-duration decision spans from correspondence changes and uses rectified evidence to allocate outcome-grounded credit across spans and their constituent turns. We evaluate \textsc{AlignOPSD} with Qwen2.5-3B and Qwen2.5-7B on ALFWorld, WebShop, and Search-QA against representative baselines. \textsc{AlignOPSD} outperforms both GRPO and StepOPSD across all eight backbone--aggregate-metric comparisons, improving on GRPO by 5.5--8.7 \% and ranking first in six. #### 摘要中文翻译

长时域智能体RLVR的结果奖励监督粗糙，特权OPSD能提供密集反馈，但功能相同的决策可能出现在不同时间步，一个决策也可能跨多个turn，因此按timestamp局部监督会错配上下文和信用范围。AlignOPSD先在同题兄弟轨迹中寻找功能相似上下文，对同一学生回答重新评分以校准教师证据；再从对应关系变化构造可变长度决策片段，分层分配受最终结果约束的credit。Qwen2.5-3B/7B在ALFWorld、WebShop、Search-QA的八项规模—聚合指标中均超过GRPO和StepOPSD，六项第一。

#### 研究背景

同为第三轮不等于在做同一决策，同一步检索或确认也可能持续多轮。本文把“找到适合当前动作的教师上下文”和“将奖励分给多大时间单元”串起来。但语义相似推理只是功能对应代理，不能替代真实环境状态等价。

#### Method

依据[官方全文](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33391)正文与附录C–F。普通学生和带检索SkillBank信息的teacher共享参数；Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B编码学生thinking与特权thinking。候选只来自同题其他rollout，每条最多一个，经过思考相似度和动作一致性过滤；WebShop额外要求操作类型及click目标一致。

教师把目标完整学生回答放到各匹配历史下teacher-force，先概率混合跨历史评分，再与原历史评分按匹配置信度插值，形成rectified log-prob gap。相邻turn对候选源的对应分布JSD变化切出decision span。turn证据为$\operatorname{sgn}(A_i^{\rm seq})$乘平均rectified gap，按span和内部turn两层分配非负有界权重，并保持token加权平均优势。

$$
\widehat A_{i,k}=A_i^{\rm seq}\operatorname{sg}(W_{i,k}).
$$

每token继承所属turn的优势，仍用clipped策略损失；没有单独加蒸馏loss。故本方法是在teacher证据帮助下重分配GRPO信用，整组$A^{\rm seq}=0$时不会由此凭空产生非零更新。匹配、span及权重均detach，仅训练使用。

跨rollout历史即使动作文本相同，隐藏状态和可执行条件仍可能不同；嵌入对应可能引入错误监督。所谓Semi-Markov更多是可变长span的credit抽象，未构建或证明真实高层决策过程。它不解决零优势组，不能与增加独立OPSD loss的方法混同。多次特权thinking和评分成本、敏感超参数及开发曲线选点应纳入完整复现评估。
